# D-2304 — Decomposer Drum

> ⚠️ HIGHEST HAZARD EQUIPMENT IN CDN SECTION. CHP decomposition is exothermic and autocatalytic. Loss of recirculation pumps with CHP present is a CRITICAL emergency. See [[hazards/cumene-hydroperoxide]].

**Technology:** UOP CDN Process | **Plant:** PTT Phenol Train II (PPCL)

## Function

The Decomposer Drum is the main acid-catalyzed CHP decomposition reactor. It is a **loop (recirculation) reactor**: a large pool of already-decomposed product dilutes the incoming concentrated CHP feed to an instantaneously low CHP concentration (~1–3 wt%), making the reaction controllable and safe. The high dilution ratio is the primary safety mechanism.

```
CHP → Phenol + Acetone   (ΔH ≈ −250 kJ/mol, acid catalyst)
```

## Overpressure Protection — X-2311

**X-2311** ("PIPE X-2311" per Drawing 0013) is the safety head (rupture disc) on D-2304:
- Full-bore instantaneous relief — no spring-loaded PRV (BS&B Type B disc, 24", + Safety Head Type FA-7R; 1 operating + 2 spares)
- Removable spool at CHP feed inlet nozzle for maintenance isolation
- Discharges to atmosphere at a safe location via 24" vent pipe with angle-cut cap

> ⛔ **BURST PRESSURE CONFLICT (2026-06-16).** Wiki previously recorded a single value: **12.16 kg/cm²g @ 60°C** (P&ID-era figure). The AS-BUILT Pressure Relief Valve Process Data Sheet (PS-0018) instead specifies: **burst pressure not to exceed 11.0 kg/cm²(g) @ 250°C; not less than 9.5 kg/cm²(g) @ 225°C**, basis = MAWP of D-2304. The new figure is referenced at D-2304's actual **design** temperature (250°C, matching the Physical Design Data table above) rather than its normal *operating* temperature (60°C, the 12.16 figure's basis) — making it the more physically appropriate value for relief-device rating. **DS band (9.5–11.0 kg/cm²(g) @ 225–250°C) adopted pending verification.** This is the highest-consequence relief device in the CDN unit (sole overpressure protection on the highest-hazard vessel) — close this conflict against the physical disc nameplate before HAZOP node analysis touches this vessel. See [[instruments/pressure-relief-valves-cdn]].

## Internal Structure (Detail "C" — Drawing 0013)

- **Two Distributors**: at CHP feed inlet and circulation return — ensure even mixing of CHP into the recirculation pool
- **Baffle**: internal baffle to direct flow
- **Grating**: at vessel bottom for uniform distribution
- **Detail "C"**: 3" SCH 160 Alloy 20 pipe, one 8-diameter hole, 50mm from pipe side, 6mm thick plate; ¾" acid injection nozzle ≥1000mm or ≥8 pipe diameters from any elbow

The Alloy 20 construction of the internal distributor is suited for dilute H₂SO₄ + organic mixture service.

## Level Control — Split-Range

- **LIC-1303 / LV-1303**: Split-range level control. LOW LEVEL SIGNAL → closes extraction side A; HIGH LEVEL SIGNAL → closes extraction side B.
- Maintains dilution ratio by controlling product extraction rate to P-2303A/B.
- **LY-1305**: Level function block (signal conditioning for split-range output)
- **"TO HIGH SIGNAL SELECTOR 23-0019"**: Level control output → P&ID 0019 Neutralization ratio/bias controller.
