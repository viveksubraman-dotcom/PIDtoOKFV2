# ST-2101 — Coarse Ore Stockpile

## Function

Coarse ore surge stockpile providing intermediate live buffer storage (60,000 t live capacity, equivalent to ~22.3 hours of concentrator design throughput at 60,000 tpd / 2,688 t/h) between Primary Crushing Area 21 and SAG Milling Area 31. Receives crushed run-of-mine ore via overland conveyor CV-2101 (Stream S-101) and discharges to SAG mill ML-3101 feed conveyor CV-3101 (Stream S-301) via three reclaim feeders FE-3101A/B/C located in the underground reclaim tunnel.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- Stockpile ratholing, bridging, and sudden rill collapse hazard; personnel and mobile equipment (bulldozers) must maintain safe setbacks from active drawpoints above reclaim feeders FE-3101A/B/C.
- Confined space and atmospheric hazard in underground reclaim feeder tunnel: requires continuous toxic/explosive gas monitoring, oxygen deficiency alarms, and emergency escape routing.
- Ore compaction and hang-up above reclaim feeders FE-3101A/B/C can cause sudden starvation of SAG Mill ML-3101; pneumatic cannons or rock breakers required in drawdown chutes.
- Fugitive mineral dust emissions from open stockpile surface during dry and windy conditions; external dust suppression spray systems and water cannon rings required (P&ID Note 2).
- Reclaim feeder chute plug and conveyor CV-3101 overload hazard if reclaim gates are drawn down unevenly.
