# CV-2101 — Primary Crusher Discharge Conveyor

## Function

Primary crusher discharge conveyor and overland transport conveyor transferring primary crushed ore (Stream S-101) from Primary Gyratory Crusher CR-2101 to Coarse Ore Stockpile ST-2101. Operates up to 5,500 t/h peak instantaneous dumping capacity (nominal 2,688 t/h continuous solids rate). Integrated with belt scale weight transmitter WT-2101 and dedicated plant feed reconciliation weightometer WIT-2102. Dust suppression sprays protect transfer points against silica dust dispersion.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- High-tonnage conveyor nip points, rotating idlers, and moving rubber belt hazard; emergency pull-wire stop switches must be installed along entire walkway length.
- Ore spillage and belt mistracking hazard during peak 5,500 t/h crusher surges; belt training sensors and chute plug switches required.
- Respirable silica dust emissions along open conveyor runs and transfer chutes; dust suppression water sprays must operate continuously during conveying (P&ID Note 2).
- Belt fire risk from friction, stalled idlers, or tramp metal gouging; requires rip detection and thermal monitoring.
- ⚠️ CONFLICT / CAPACITY SURGE NOTE: Conveyor CV-2101 must withstand peak instantaneous surges up to 5,500 t/h from CR-2101 (per RB-4410-PID-21-001 Rev B and RB-4410-PS-CR2101 Rev B) while continuous plant mass balance nominal rate is 2,688 t/h (per RB-4410-PFD-001 Rev B).
