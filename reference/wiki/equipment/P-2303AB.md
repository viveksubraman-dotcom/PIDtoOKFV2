# P-2303A/B — Decomposer Product Pumps

> ⚠️ CHP is a peroxide — thermal decomposition risk. See [[hazards/cumene-hydroperoxide]].

**Technology:** UOP CDN Process | **Plant:** PTT Phenol Train II (PPCL)

## Function

P-2303A/B extract the product overflow from the Decomposer Drum ([[equipment/D-2304]]) and transfer it to the Dehydrators ([[equipment/E-2308AB]]). The product is mostly phenol + acetone + water with traces of AMS, residual cumene, and trace CHP — still thermally reactive until the dehydrator section completes residual CHP destruction at 135°C.

## Motor Control

**Type B** — no automatic start/stop. Operator must manually initiate standby pump from DCS. See [[instruments/motor-control]].

Soft starters SRT-P2303A and SRT-P2303B are installed to reduce mechanical shock and current inrush when starting these pumps at decomposer conditions (0.898 SG, ~60–72°C product).

## Seal System — API Plan 2/53A (Detail L)

From Drawing 0001-L (Pump Seal Plan 4/4):
- **Plan 2**: Dead-ended inner seal chamber — no flush, barrier fluid sealed
- **Plan 53A**: Pressurized barrier fluid (above process pressure) on outer seal

Drain to **Acid Aromatics Closed Drain Header** per STD DWG 8-137 — same closed header used by P-2301A/B and other pumps handling CHP or decomposition product.
