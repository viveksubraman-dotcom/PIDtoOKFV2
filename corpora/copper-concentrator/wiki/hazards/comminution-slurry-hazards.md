## Safety Data Sheet Standards & Chemical Controls (SDS)
Site chemical safety standards and supplier Safety Data Sheets mandate specific physical controls and personal protective equipment across Area 45 reagents:
- **Anionic Polyacrylamide Flocculant (CAS 9003-05-8, SDS_9003-05-8_anionic-polyacrylamide-flocculant.pdf):**
  - **Classification / Hazard:** Low toxicity; spills extremely slippery when wet.
  - **Plant Use / Service:** Settling acceleration and solid-liquid separation in Concentrate Thickener `TH-5101` (15–25 g/t dry solids) and Tailings Thickener `TH-6101` (settling 6,314 m³/h tailings slurry); dosed from Area 45 system `TK-4531`.
  - **Storage & Handling:** Store cool and dry, away from acids and ignition sources; keep containers tightly closed to prevent atmospheric moisture absorption.
  - **Exposure Controls & PPE:** Local exhaust ventilation; chemical splash goggles; nitrile gloves; particulate respirator where exposure limits may be exceeded.
  - **First Aid:** Remove casualty to fresh air; flush eyes and skin thoroughly with running water for 15 minutes; seek prompt medical attention.
  - **Firefighting Protocol:** Dry chemical, $\text{CO}_2$, or foam. Critical directive: **Do not use water jet on burning xanthate** in adjacent Area 45 storage.
  - **Slip & Fall Safeguards:** Polymer powder/solution creates an immediate severe slip hazard upon contact with water; dry spills must be swept/vacuumed dry first, followed by sand/grit application before washing down.
- **Carbon Disulfide Off-Gas (Decomposition Product, CAS 75-15-0, SDS_75-15-0_carbon-disulfide.pdf):**
  - **Classification / Hazard:** Flammable vapor / acute neurotoxin; flash point -30 °C, auto-ignition 90 °C, LEL 1.3 %, TWA 1 ppm (ACGIH skin notation).
  - **Plant Use / Service:** Volatile hydrolysis off-gas evolved in the enclosed reagent preparation room housing PAX mixing tank `TK-4501` and dosing tank `TK-4502`.
  - **Storage & Handling:** Store cool and dry, away from acids and ignition sources; keep containers closed; make-up water temperature strictly maintained $<40$ °C.
  - **Exposure Controls & PPE:** Local exhaust ventilation via continuous mechanical extraction fan `FN-4501`; chemical splash goggles; nitrile gloves; organic vapor respirator / SCBA where exposure limits may be exceeded.
  - **First Aid:** Remove casualty to fresh air; flush eyes and skin thoroughly with running water for 15 minutes; seek prompt medical attention.
  - **Firefighting Protocol:** Dry chemical, $\text{CO}_2$, or foam. Critical mandate: **Do not use water jet on burning xanthate** (scatters molten solids and accelerates violent $CS_2$ decomposition).
- **Polyglycol Ether Frother (DF-250 Type, CAS 37286-64-9, SDS_37286-64-9_polyglycol-ether-frother.pdf):**
  - **Classification:** Combustible (closed-cup flash point >100 °C); Eye irritant.

  - **Storage & Handling:** Store cool and dry, away from acids and ignition sources; keep containers tightly closed.

  - **First Aid:** Remove to fresh air; flush eyes and skin with water for 15 minutes; seek prompt medical attention.
  - **Firefighting Protocol:** Dry chemical, $\text{CO}_2$, or foam. Critical directive: **Do not use water jet on burning xanthate** in adjacent Area 45 storage.

## Classification & Slurry Pumping Hazards (CY-3201 & PP-3201A/B/C)
- **Nuclear Radiation Hazard from Density Gauge DT-3201:** The density of cyclone feed slurry (Stream S-321) is measured using nuclear gamma attenuation transmitter `DT-3201`. Strict statutory radiation safety permits, radioactive source housing shutter lockouts (LOTO), and quarterly wipe testing apply per Note 3 of `RB-4410-PID-32-001 Rev B`. Maintenance crews must never perform hot work or line breaking on the vertical delivery column without confirmed source shielding shutter closure.
- **Cyclone Feed Sump Surge & Flooding:** The cyclone feed pump hopper receives 8,064 t/h of recirculating slurry from ball mill ML-3201 discharge overflow plus SAG mill trommel screen undersize. Level controller `LIC-3201` modulates pump speed (Note 1). A trip of duty pumps PP-3201A/B without immediate mill trip causes sump overfilling within minutes, dumping abrasive slurry onto plant walkways and electrical cable trays.
- **Standby Pump Cut-In Transients:** Standby pump PP-3201C is identical to duty pumps PP-3201A/B per Note 2 of `RB-4410-PID-32-001 Rev B`. Automatic cut-in upon duty pump trip requires rapid actuation of automated suction and discharge knife gate valves and instantaneous gland seal water pressurization to prevent slurry backflow and pipeline sanding.
- **Hydrocyclone Roping & Apex Choking:** If feed pulp density exceeds 60.0 wt% solids (SG 1.618) or pressure deviates outside the 100–130 kPa operating envelope (monitored by PT-3201; PAL 90 kPa, PAH 140 kPa), cyclones undergo roping, resulting in coarse solids reporting to overflow Stream S-324 (advancing to rougher flotation conditioning tank TK-4101), disrupting downstream recovery.
