## Circuit Operating Strategies & Bounds
### Tailings Thickening Operating Window (TH-6101)
- **Sedimentation & Water Clarification Window (TH-6101):** High-rate 60 m diameter tailings thickener `TH-6101` receives 2,637 t/h dry solids (Stream S-611 at 6,314 m³/h slurry, 33.0 wt% solids, SG 1.266) from rougher cells `FC-4101`. Sized with a **12.5 MNm mechanical design rake torque rating** per `RB-4410-PS-TH6101 Rev B` [^21].
- **Underflow Density Target & Pipeline Rheology:** Underflow pulp density is controlled at **62.0 wt% solids** (SG 1.652, 2,576 m³/h slurry, Stream S-621) discharging to 3-stage series disposal pumps `PP-6101A/B/C`. Operating below 58 wt% causes water wastage and excess pipeline volumetric load; operating above 64 wt% risks severe non-Newtonian yield stress spikes, rake torque overload exceeding 12.5 MNm, and sanding in the 6.2 km overland pipeline corridor [^4] [^21].
- **Bed Inventory & Lift Coordination:** Mud bed height is continuously monitored by `LT-6101` (0–10 m span). Operating above `LAH 6.5 m` alerts operations of impending rake drive torque overload, prompting automated underflow pump speed increase and motorized rake arm lift [^6] [^21].
- **Clarified Water Recovery:** Simultaneously decants **3,739 m³/h** of clarified overflow water over peripheral launders into the plant reclaim water circuit, closing ~69.8% of the tailings water balance [^4] [^21].
- **Sedimentation & Clarification Envelope (TH-5101):** High-rate 30 m diameter concentrate thickener `TH-5101` receives 51 t/h dry solids (Stream S-431 at 149 m³/h slurry, 28.0 wt% solids, SG 1.217) from cleaner column `FC-4301`. Synthetic anionic polymer flocculant is dosed into the feedwell at **15 to 25 g/t dry solids**. Dosing below 15 g/t impairs settling velocity and increases overflow suspended solids (>100 ppm), causing copper losses to the plant process water header. Dosing above 25 g/t creates structural network gelling in the pulp bed, producing high yield stress, rake drive torque overload, and severe cloth blinding on filter press `FP-5101` [^4] [^20].
- **Underflow Density Target:** Underflow pulp density is maintained at **65.0 wt% solids** (SG 1.966), discharging 40 m³/h slurry (Stream S-511) to underflow pumps `PP-5101A/B` and recovering **103 m³/h** of decanted clarified water [^4] [^20].
- **Primary Crushing (CR-2101):** The 60 x 113 gyratory crusher operates with an installed 1,200 kW drive motor and 165 mm CSS, receiving ROM ore through a 1,525 mm feed opening to deliver $P_{80} = 150\text{ mm}$ product at up to 5,500 t/h peak throughput to overland conveyor `CV-2101` [^8].
- **SAG Milling Circuit (ML-3101):** The 12.2 m diameter × 6.7 m EGL SAG mill processes 2,688 t/h fresh crushed ore (Stream S-301) and 484 t/h pebble recycle (Stream S-305) with 8.2 kWh/t specific comminution energy. The 22,000 kW gearless synchronous ring motor operates within 60–80% critical speed via a 33 kV cycloconverter [^14].
- **Secondary Ball Milling Circuit (ML-3201):** The 8.2 m diameter × 13.4 m EGL (27 × 44 ft) ball mill operates in closed circuit with cyclone cluster `CY-3201`, receiving 8,064 t/h solids at 75.0 wt% solids (Stream S-323, representing a 300% circulating load) and consuming 8.0 kWh/t specific grinding energy. Powered by a fixed-speed GMD (22,000 kW installed / 20,500 kW rated ring motor) turning at 75% critical speed with 32–35% vol ball charge (65 mm media) [^15].
- **Cyclone Feed Pumping (PP-3201A/B/C):** Secondary grinding pulp is delivered to hydrocyclone cluster `CY-3201` by heavy-duty centrifugal slurry pumps configured as 2 duty + 1 standby (PP-3201A/B duty, PP-3201C standby). Pumps feature high-chrome white iron wet-end liners, 1,600 kW motors, 32 m TDH, and 10 bar(g) casing design pressure per `RB-4410-PS-PP3201 Rev B`. Feed density is maintained at 60.0 wt% solids (SG 1.618 monitored by DT-3201 across 1.0–2.0 SG span), and manifold pressure is kept within 100–130 kPa (monitored by PT-3201; PAL 90 kPa, PAH 140 kPa) [^2] [^5] [^6] [^10] [^17].
- **Pebble Crushing (CR-3102):** The cone crusher (extra-coarse cavity, 750 kW motor, 12 mm CSS) operates in closed circuit with SAG discharge screen `SC-3101`, crushing critical-size pebbles at 484 t/h design capacity before recycling via Stream S-305 to SAG mill feed conveyor `CV-3101` [^2] [^9].
- **Concentrate Thickener Underflow Pumping (PP-5101A/B):** Slurry underflow from TH-5101 is transferred to filter press FP-5101 by centrifugal slurry pumps configured as 1 duty + 1 standby (PP-5101A duty, PP-5101B standby). Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-PP5101 Rev B specifies 210 m³/h rated capacity (increased from 180 m³/h in Rev A after filter trade-off; supersedes Rev A), 28 m TDH, 75 kW motor, and slurry SG 2.0, whereas Drawing RB-4410-PFD-003 Rev B (Stream S-511) establishes a continuous normal operating underflow rate of 40 m³/h at 65.0 wt% solids (SG 1.966). Variable speed drives or recirculation loops are required to prevent pump cavitation and internal recirculation under throttled flow conditions [^4] [^18].
- **SAG Milling Circuit (ML-3101):** The 12.2 m diameter × 6.7 m EGL SAG mill processes 2,688 t/h fresh crushed ore (Stream S-301) and 484 t/h pebble recycle (Stream S-305) with 8.2 kWh/t specific comminution energy. The 22,000 kW gearless synchronous ring motor operates within 60–80% critical speed via a 33 kV cycloconverter. Charge loading is managed within 12–15% vol ball charge and 30% vol maximum total filling (2,450 t total charge weight). Trunnion bearings (2 x 2 pad) are protected by ISO VG 460 lube oil delivered at 120 bar(g) normal lift pressure (170 bar(g) design). For maintenance and relining, 0.1 rpm inching mode and a 2.5 t relining manipulator handle 50 mm Cr-Mo alloy liners [^14].
- **Secondary Ball Milling Circuit (ML-3201):** The 8.2 m diameter × 13.4 m EGL (27 × 44 ft) ball mill operates in closed circuit with cyclone cluster `CY-3201`, receiving 8,064 t/h solids at 75.0 wt% solids (Stream S-323, representing a 300% circulating load) and consuming 8.0 kWh/t specific grinding energy. Powered by a fixed-speed GMD (22,000 kW installed / 20,500 kW rated ring motor) turning at 75% critical speed with 32–35% vol ball charge (65 mm media). Total charge weight at maximum volumetric filling is rated at 1,980 tonnes. Trunnion bearings are supported by a combined hydrostatic lift (110 bar(g) normal, 170 bar(g) design) and hydrodynamic lube unit (ISO VG 460 oil) monitored by RTDs `TT-3201` and `TT-3202` (0–150 °C). Confined space maintenance utilizes 0.1 rpm inching mode and a 2.5 t relining handler with mandatory 33 kV breaker LOTO [^15].
- **F80 / P80 Transition:** The comminution train achieves size reduction from $F_{80} = 150\text{ mm}$ down to $P_{80} = 150\text{ µm}$ across the SAG and secondary ball milling circuits, followed by fine regrind to $P_{80} = 38\text{ µm}$ for rougher concentrates.
- **Classification Envelope (CY-3201):** Cyclone feed solids at 60.0% w/w (1.618 SG monitored by DT-3201 across span 1.0–2.0 SG) with a 3.0 circulating load requires precise sump level and pressure management (design window 100 to 130 kPa on PT-3201 across 13 operating cyclones of 16 installed 660 mm units, with alarm boundaries at PAL 90 kPa and PAH 140 kPa) to avoid roping conditions [^5] [^10].
- **Rougher Bank Configuration (FC-4101 to FC-4107):** Flotation is conducted across $7 \times 300\text{ m}^3$ forced-air mechanical tank cells ($2,100\text{ m}^3$ total volume) powered by $7 \times 250\text{ kW}$ agitators ($1,750\text{ kW}$ total).
- **Flotation Residence Time & Aeration Window:** The bank operates with a 32-minute design retention time and superficial air rate of 0.8–1.6 m/s. Air rate below 0.8 m/s reduces particle recovery; air rate above 1.6 m/s creates excessive froth turbulence and gangue entrainment [^11].
- **Concentrate Regrind Milling (ML-4201):** Rougher concentrate (Stream S-411 at 215 t/h solids, 580 m³/h slurry at 30.0 wt% solids) is reground in vertical stirred mill `ML-4201` (3,000 kW installed power, 3–5 mm ceramic grinding media) down to target liberation size $P_{80} = 38\ \mu\text{m}$. Regrinding detaches micro-encapsulated copper sulfides from pyrite prior to cleaner column flotation [^1] [^3] [^16].
- **Cleaner Column Flotation (FC-4301):** Cleaner flotation is conducted in a single 4.5 m diameter × 12.0 m height column receiving 215 t/h solids from ML-4201 ($P_{80} = 38\text{ µm}$). Counter-current wash water addition is regulated at **35 m³/h** to maintain positive froth bias and reject entrained fine gangue, achieving the commercial concentrate quality target of **26.0% Cu** discharging to concentrate thickener TH-5101 (Stream S-431) [^1] [^3] [^12].
- **Alkalinity Control:** Maintaining pH 10.5 in the rougher feed suppresses pyrite ($FeS_2$) floatability while ensuring high recovery of chalcopyrite ($CuFeS_2$). Monitored by AIC-4101 with operating alarms at AL 10.0 pH and AH 11.2 pH [^3] [^6].
- **Reagent Dosing Bounds:**
  - PAX Collector: 25 g/t ore ($67.2\text{ kg/h}$) [^3].
  - MIBC Frother: 20 g/t ore ($53.8\text{ kg/h}$) [^3].
- **Mass Pull Target:** Controlled at 8.0% mass pull (215 t/h solids, 580 $m^3/h$ slurry) into regrind mill ML-4201 to liberate copper minerals to $P_{80} = 38\text{ µm}$ [^1] [^3].
- **Concentrate Underflow Density:** Maintained at 65.0 wt% solids (SG 1.966) to feed filter press FP-5101 via pump PP-5101A. Operating above 68 wt% induces severe rake torque and pump choking; operating below 60 wt% increases filter press cycle time and cake moisture.
- **Concentrate Filtration Performance (FP-5101):** Vertical plate pressure filter `FP-5101` (144 m² filtration area) operates in cyclic batch sequence to dewater concentrate cake to **8.5% w/w moisture**, providing a 1.3% safety margin below the Transportable Moisture Limit (**TML = 9.8% w/w**). Moisture testing must be performed on every batch to satisfy the IMSBC Code prior to bulk ocean carrier loading [^13].
- **Tailings Underflow Density:** Maintained at 62.0 wt% solids (SG 1.652, 2,576 $m^3/h$) into 3-stage series pumps PP-6101A/B/C. Pumping above 64 wt% causes excessive frictional head loss in the 6.2 km line; pumping below 58 wt% wastes water.
- **Mud Bed Inventory Management:** Bed level is monitored by LT-6101 (0–10 m span). Operating above LAH 6.5 m risks rake torque overload and requires automated underflow pump speed increase.
- **Overpressure Protection:** Overland tailings disposal pipeline is protected by SIF-6101 (SIL 2), which executes an emergency trip of PP-6101A/B/C if stage-3 discharge pressure reaches PAHH 38 bar(g) (warning alarm at PAH 36 bar(g)).
- **Concentrate Thickener Underflow Pumping (PP-5101A/B):** Slurry underflow from TH-5101 is transferred to filter press FP-5101 by centrifugal slurry pumps configured as 1 duty + 1 standby (PP-5101A duty, PP-5101B standby). Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-PP5101 Rev A specifies 180 m³/h rated capacity, 28 m TDH, 75 kW motor, and slurry SG 2.0, whereas Drawing RB-4410-PFD-003 Rev B (Stream S-511) establishes a continuous normal operating underflow rate of 40 m³/h at 65.0 wt% solids (SG 1.966). Variable speed drives or recirculation loops are required to prevent pump cavitation and internal recirculation under throttled flow conditions [^4] [^18].
