## Elevation Constraint

> **Note:** Vessel elevation must be ≥ 2500 mm above top of Decanter D-2205. Condensate drains by gravity D-2211 → D-2205.

## Function

Receives cooldown and blowdown gas from the Charcoal Adsorbers (D-2204A/B/C) during the cooling phase of the adsorber regeneration cycle. After steam regeneration, the hot adsorber is cooled by passing inert gas/air through the bed; this cooling gas picks up residual Cumene vapors and condensate. D-2211 knocks out the liquid before gas discharge, and the collected condensate drains by gravity to the Decanter D-2205.

**Key distinction from D-2203/D-2208**: The inlet nozzle J02 is **44"** diameter (vs 36" for D-2203 and D-2208) — reflecting the higher volumetric flow of charcoal adsorber cooldown blowdown versus the continuous oxidizer vent gas. The SG (0.9064) matches D-2203, confirming both vessels handle similar chilled gas condensate compositions.

## Safety Notes

- Subzero MDMT (−10°C): impact-tested 304L or SS-lined KCS mandatory; verify material certification before installation
- Cold insulation (C-type) must be maintained in good condition to keep vessel wall above −10°C MDMT during all operating modes
- Cooldown gas may carry trace CHP accumulated in the charcoal bed during long adsorption cycles — monitor bed chemistry
