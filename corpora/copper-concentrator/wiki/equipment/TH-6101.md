# TH-6101 — Tailings Thickener

## Function

Large-diameter high-rate tailings thickener receiving 6,314 m3/h of final flotation tailings (Stream S-611 at 2,637 t/h dry solids, 33.0 wt% solids, SG 1.266) from rougher/scavenger flotation cells FC-4101. Sized with a 60 m tank diameter and a 12.5 MNm design rake drive torque mechanism. Utilizes polymer flocculant to accelerate solid-liquid separation, producing a dense underflow slurry (Stream S-621 at 2,637 t/h solids, 2,576 m3/h slurry, 62.0 wt% solids, SG 1.652) pumped via 3 series pumps (PP-6101A series) through a 6.2 km pipeline to the Tailings Storage Facility (TSF). Compaction bed level is continuously monitored by LT-6101 (range 0–10 m, LAH 6.5 m). Simultaneously recovers 3,739 m3/h (~69.8% of tailings water) of clarified process water for immediate plant recycle. High-rate tailings thickener (60 m diameter, 12.5 MNm design rake torque) operating in Area 61 to dewater final flotation tailings from rougher cells FC-4101 ahead of 6.2 km overland pumping via PP-6101A/B/C to the Tailings Storage Facility. Registered in Mechanical Equipment List RB-4410-PID-00-002 Rev B. ## Hazards & Safeguards

- Thickener rake torque overload & bed consolidation: At 62.0 wt% solids (SG 1.652), thickened tailings exhibit pronounced non-Newtonian Bingham plastic rheology. Power interruption, underflow pipeline stoppage, or excessive bed inventory causes bed compaction and catastrophic rake drive torque trip, requiring automatic rake lift and emergency bed dilution.
- Massive volumetric hydraulic surge: Inflow of 6,314 m3/h slurry requires uninterrupted thickening; failure of flocculant dosing causes immediate bed cloudiness, slime overrun, and unclarified tailings overflow into process water systems or containment areas.
- Overland pipeline interface hazards: Direct coupling to a 6.2 km overland pumping line means any downstream pipeline blockage immediately creates thickener underflow choking and emergency tailings sump overflow risks.
- Mud bed level accumulation: Monitored by LT-6101; high bed level alarm LAH 6.5 m warns of impending rake torque overload and bed consolidation, triggering automatic underflow pump ramp-up.

At 62.0 wt% solids (SG 1.652), thickened tailings exhibit pronounced non-Newtonian Bingham plastic rheology. Power interruption, underflow pipeline stoppage, or excessive bed inventory causes bed compaction and catastrophic rake drive torque trip, requiring automatic rake lift and emergency bed dilution.
- Massive volumetric hydraulic surge: Inflow of 6,314 m3/h slurry requires uninterrupted thickening across the 60 m diameter basin; failure of flocculant dosing causes immediate bed cloudiness, slime overrun, and unclarified tailings overflow into process water systems or containment areas.
- Overland pipeline interface hazards: Direct coupling to a 6.2 km overland pumping line via PP-6101A series pumps means any downstream pipeline blockage immediately creates thickener underflow choking and emergency tailings sump overflow risks.
- Mud bed level accumulation: Monitored by LT-6101; high bed level alarm LAH 6.5 m warns of impending rake torque overload (approaching the 12.5 MNm mechanical rating) and bed consolidation, triggering automatic underflow pump ramp-up and motorized rake arm elevation.
- Excessive mud bed consolidation exceeding 12.5 MNm rake drive torque limit.
- Turbidity carryover into plant process water on flocculant failure.
- Mud bed level accumulation: Monitored by LT-6101 per RB-4410-PID-61-001 Rev B; high bed level alarm LAH 6.5 m warns of impending rake torque overload (approaching the 12.5 MNm mechanical rating) and bed consolidation, triggering automatic underflow pump ramp-up and motorized rake arm elevation.
- Flushing water connection verification (Note 3): P&ID RB-4410-PID-61-001 Rev B Note 3 notes that flushing water connections are not shown; emergency clean flush connections must be verified to prevent line sanding during pump trips.
- BED OVER-COMPACTION & TORQUE OVERLOAD: Underflow density > 62% solids or mud bed height > 6.5 m (LT-6101 LAH) causes rapid rake torque accumulation exceeding 12.5 MNm mechanical rating; auto-rake lift and underflow ramp mandatory per RB-4410-OM-001 Sec 11.
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — Underflow Solids Concentration: RB-4410-PFD-003 Rev B, RB-4410-PS-TH6101 Rev B; RB-4410-PID-61-001 Rev B, RB-4410-PS-TH6101 Rev B, RB-4410-PFD-003 Rev B specifies 62.0 % w/w, whereas RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2 specifies 62 % solids w/w — verify with engineer before HAZOP
- Anionic polyacrylamide flocculant slip & handling hazards (CAS 9003-05-8 per SDS_9003-05-8_anionic-polyacrylamide-flocculant.pdf): Polymer spills around thickener TH-6101 60 m basin perimeter, access bridge, feedwell, and dosing headers are extremely slippery when wet; wash down immediately or absorb with inert solids before walking; handling neat polymer or concentrated solution requires chemical goggles, nitrile gloves, and particulate respirator; store cool and dry away from acids and ignition sources; firefighting media: dry chemical, CO2, foam (do not use water jet on burning xanthate in Area 45).
