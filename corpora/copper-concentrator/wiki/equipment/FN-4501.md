# FN-4501 — PAX Mixing Room Extraction Fan

## Function

Extraction Fan FN-4501 provides continuous mechanical exhaust ventilation for the enclosed PAX mixing room housing PAX Xanthate Mixing Tank TK-4501 and dosing tank TK-4502. Continuous 24/7 operation is mandated by Note 1 of P&ID RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B to exhaust toxic, volatile, and flammable carbon disulfide (CS2) vapors evolved during xanthate batch preparation, maintaining negative room pressure and preventing flammable or lethal gas accumulation in the working enclosure. Depicted on P&ID RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B and referenced in Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-TK4501 Rev B. ## Hazards & Safeguards

- Lethal and explosive CS2 gas accumulation upon ventilation loss: Carbon disulfide has an extremely low autoignition temperature (100 °C), wide flammability range (1.3–50 vol%), and high toxicity (IDLH 500 ppm). Fan failure rapidly creates an immediately dangerous to life and health (IDLH) and explosive atmosphere inside the enclosed room. Continuous fan operation is strictly mandated per Note 1 of RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B.
- Loss of ventilation interlock: Fan run status must be interlocked with the plant DCS. Loss of fan operation must trigger immediate visible/audible alarms outside room access doors and inhibit operator entry.
- Spark-resistant construction: Fan wetted components and motor must meet hazardous area electrical and mechanical spark-resistant design standards.
- Lethal and explosive CS2 gas accumulation upon ventilation loss: Carbon disulfide has an extremely low autoignition temperature (90-100 °C), wide flammability range (1.3–50 vol%), and high toxicity (IDLH 500 ppm). Fan failure rapidly creates an IDLH and explosive atmosphere inside the enclosed room. Continuous fan operation is strictly mandated per Note 1 of RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B.
- HAZOP NODE HZ-45-03 (PAX MIXING ROOM CS2 ACCUMULATION): Wet/hot PAX (>40 °C) or acid contamination induces rapid xanthate decomposition into carbon disulfide (CS2), risking flash fire and toxic exposure. Existing safeguard is extraction fan FN-4501. Mandatory recommendation ACT-HAZOP-45-03 requires installing CS2 gas detector AT-4501 (alarm 10 % LEL) interlocked to FN-4501 high speed and PAX feeder stop.
