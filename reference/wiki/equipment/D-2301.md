*Large horizontal drum (2800mm × 9400mm) operating near atmospheric pressure at 38°C — essentially ambient conditions for liquid cumene. Size reflects the large cumene inventory required to support quench capability for two vacuum columns simultaneously.*

## Function

D-2301 is the central cumene distribution vessel for the entire Concentration sub-section. It has three purposes:

1. **Emergency / protective quench** to V-2301 (Preflash Column) and V-2302 (Flash Column) — injects liquid cumene into the column sumps to dilute CHP concentration and cool the bottom temperature if conditions rise toward dangerous levels.
2. **Normal cumene distribution** — maintains a controlled cumene flow to column distributors during operation.
3. **Cumene inventory buffer** — stores sufficient cumene to support sustained quench operation; overflow goes to Cumene Storage Tank TK-4104.

The drum receives fresh cumene make-up from **Fresh Feed Pumps at Oxidation Section** and from column overhead condensate returns.

## Safety Interlock Valves

| Tag | Type | SIS Controller | Action on ESD |
|-----|------|---------------|---------------|
| UXV-0601 | SIS isolation valve | UC-2301 | CLOSE — stops cumene quench flow to columns on Concentration ESD |
| UXY-0601 | Solenoid valve | UC-2301 | Per SIS logic |

**UXV-0601 close on ESD:** During a UC-2301 Concentration ESD, the quench flow is stopped. This is because the entire concentration section is being isolated — continuing to inject liquid into a shutdown column is not desired. Emergency cumene dilution is handled via cumene flush sequences (HXS-0106/0107 on UC-2302 for Decomposer), not the quench system.

## Internal Structure

D-2301 contains:
- **DISTRIBUTOR** — UOP DWG 3-183 Type C (per DS); distributes cumene evenly to multiple outlet nozzles
- **PARTITION** — 10mm thick internal weir separating sections of the drum (per DS); ensures orderly overflow and prevents short-circuiting between inlet and outlet
- **VORTEX BREAKER** — 150mm (per DS); fitted at liquid outlet nozzle to prevent vortex entrainment of vapor into pump suction

The drum has a **"5000 MIN ABOVE QUENCH NOZZLE AT BOTTOM TRAY OF FLASH COLUMN"** note on the P&ID — this means D-2301 elevation must be at least 5000mm above the flash column quench nozzle to provide adequate gravity head for quench flow without relying on pump pressure.

## Process Engineering Notes

1. **Gravity quench design**: The note specifying minimum elevation above the Flash Column quench nozzle confirms that quench flow is gravity-driven (at least partially). This ensures quench works even during pump failures.

2. **HIC-0601 manual override**: The operator can increase quench rate manually via HIC-0601. This is important during abnormal conditions — if the column bottom temperature or CHP concentration trends upward, the operator increases quench before the SIS acts.

3. **CHP Nitrogen Header requirement**: Per STD DWG 8-138 Type 1, D-2301 connects to the CHP Nitrogen Header (not regular plant N₂). Since cumene flowing through D-2301 will contain trace CHP, the nitrogen blanket must be from the CHP-rated header to maintain system segregation. The "CHP CLOSED DRAIN HEADER" connection is also per this same standard.

4. **Cumene overflow management**: When quench flow is high or fresh make-up exceeds drawdown, excess cumene overflows to Cumene Storage Tank TK-4104. This prevents D-2301 from overfilling while maintaining inventory.
