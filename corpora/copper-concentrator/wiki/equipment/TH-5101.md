# TH-5101 — Concentrate Thickener

## Function

Continuous high-rate solid-liquid separation thickener (30 m diameter) receiving final copper concentrate pulp (Stream S-431 at 51 t/h solids, 149 m3/h slurry, 28.0 wt% solids, SG 1.217) from cleaner flotation column FC-4301. Concentrates solids via gravity sedimentation and synthetic flocculant agglomeration (15 - 25 g/t dosage) to produce a dense underflow slurry at 65.0 wt% solids (Stream S-511 at 51 t/h solids, 40 m3/h slurry, SG 1.966) pumped by centrifugal slurry pumps PP-5101A/B (rated 210 m3/h per RB-4410-PS-PP5101 Rev B, 28 m TDH, 75 kW) to concentrate filter press FP-5101. Recovers 103 m3/h of clarified water via peripheral overflow launder for plant process water reuse. High-rate concentrate thickener (30 m diameter) operating in Area 51 to dewater copper concentrate froth from cleaner column FC-4301 ahead of filter press FP-5101. Produces underflow at 65.0 wt% solids and clarifies 103 m3/h overflow water. Registered in Mechanical Equipment List RB-4410-PID-00-002 Rev B. Continuous high-rate solid-liquid separation thickener receiving final copper concentrate pulp (Stream S-431 at 51 t/h solids, 149 m3/h slurry, 28.0 wt% solids, SG 1.217) from cleaner flotation column FC-4301. Concentrates solids via gravity sedimentation and synthetic flocculant agglomeration (15 - 25 g/t dosage from TK-4531 per RB-4410-PID-51-001 Rev B Note 1) to produce a dense underflow slurry at 65.0 wt% solids (Stream S-511 at 51 t/h solids, 40 m3/h slurry, SG 1.966) pumped by centrifugal slurry pumps PP-5101A/B (rated 210 m3/h per RB-4410-PS-PP5101 Rev B, 28 m TDH, 75 kW) to concentrate filter press FP-5101. Recovers 103 m3/h of clarified water via peripheral overflow launder for plant process water reuse. Receives recycled filtrate from filter press FP-5101 directly into feedwell per RB-4410-PID-51-001 Rev B Note 2. High-rate 30 m diameter concentrate thickener in Area 51 receiving final copper concentrate froth (Stream S-431) from cleaner column FC-4301. Sized to thicken concentrate slurry to a regulated underflow density of 65 % solids w/w for transfer via PP-5101A to pressure filter press FP-5101 per RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2. Decants clarified water back to plant process water. Corroborates 30 m sizing against the 25 m notation on P&ID RB-4410-PID-51-001 Rev B. High-rate concentrate thickener (30 m diameter) operating in Area 51 to dewater copper concentrate froth from cleaner column FC-4301 ahead of filter press FP-5101. Utilizes synthetic anionic polyacrylamide flocculant (PAM, CAS 9003-05-8 per SDS_9003-05-8) dosed at 15–25 g/t dry solids from Area 45 reagent system TK-4531 to achieve solid-liquid separation, producing underflow at 65.0 wt% solids and recovering 103 m³/h clarified overflow water for plant reuse.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- ⚠️ CONFLICT — CONCENTRATE PRODUCTION CAPACITY: Drawing RB-4410-PFD-003 Rev B and RB-4410-PFD-002 Rev B (Stream S-431 and S-511) specify 51 t/h solids, whereas Drawing RB-4410-PFD-000 Rev B specifies 50.6 t/h solids (26.0% Cu grade) — verify with engineer before HAZOP to ensure thickener rake drive torque and underflow pumps are sized for 51 t/h.
- Rake mechanism torque overload: Operating underflow at 65.0 wt% solids (SG 1.966) approaches non-Newtonian plastic yield conditions; failure of underflow transfer pumps causes rapid bed consolidation and rake drive torque overload, necessitating automated rake lift and emergency bed dilution.
- Flocculant dosing failure or under-dosing: Disrupts sedimentation kinetics, causing high suspended solids in peripheral overflow and carrying valuable chalcopyrite concentrate into the process water header.
- Thickener tank overflow / launder fouling: Froth accumulation or launder sanding causes localized overflow spillage into the concentrate dewatering containment area.
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — CONCENTRATE UNDERFLOW PUMP RATED CAPACITY: Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-PP5101 Rev A specifies a rated slurry flow of 180 m³/h for PP-5101A/B, whereas Drawing RB-4410-PFD-003 Rev B (Stream S-511) specifies an operating underflow slurry flow of 40 m³/h (51 t/h solids, 27 t/h water at 65.0 wt% solids). Operating a 180 m³/h rated slurry pump at 40 m³/h represents severe flow throttling (22% BEP), risking internal recirculation, severe hydraulic vibration, accelerated impeller/casing wear, and slurry settling/sanding in the pump volute — verify pump hydraulic sizing curve and minimum continuous flow recirculation line before HAZOP.
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — CONCENTRATE UNDERFLOW PUMP RATED CAPACITY: Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-PP5101 Rev B specifies a rated slurry flow of 210 m³/h for PP-5101A/B (increased from 180 m³/h in Rev A after filter trade-off; supersedes Rev A), whereas Drawing RB-4410-PFD-003 Rev B (Stream S-511) specifies an operating underflow slurry flow of 40 m³/h (51 t/h solids, 27 t/h water at 65.0 wt% solids). Operating a 210 m³/h rated slurry pump at 40 m³/h represents severe flow throttling (19.0% BEP), risking internal recirculation, severe hydraulic vibration, accelerated impeller/casing wear, and slurry settling/sanding in the pump volute — verify pump hydraulic sizing curve and minimum continuous flow recirculation line before HAZOP.
- Flocculant dosing window (15 - 25 g/t): Under-dosing (<15 g/t) impairs sedimentation kinetics causing high suspended solids carryover into peripheral overflow and valuable chalcopyrite concentrate loss to the process water circuit; over-dosing (>25 g/t) causes excessive polymer bridging, rapid viscoplastic gel formation, severe rake torque spikes, high yield stress, and blinding of filter cloths on downstream filter press FP-5101.
- Rake drive overload and structural arm damage if bed accumulates excessively.
- Flocculant dosing excursion (>25 g/t causing gelling, <15 g/t causing slime rise).
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — THICKENER TH-5101 DIAMETER: Piping & Instrumentation Diagram RB-4410-PID-51-001 Rev B specifies thickener diameter as 25 m, whereas Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-TH5101 Rev B and Master Drawing Register RB-4410-PID-00-001 Rev B specify 30 m diameter — verify settling area, rise rate, and hydraulic capacity with lead process engineer before HAZOP.
- Closed-loop filtrate recycle to feedwell (Note 2): Filtrate from filter press FP-5101 is returned directly to the TH-5101 feedwell per Note 2. Filter cloth tears or blinding will return fine concentrate solids into the thickener, increasing recirculating load, raising slurry yield stress, and aggravating rake torque overload.
- Flocculant dosing interruption (TK-4531 / Note 1): Polymer flocculant supplied from reagent storage TK-4531 (not shown) must be maintained within 15–25 g/t dry solids. Loss of flocculant causes immediate slime rise, high overflow turbidity, and loss of valuable copper concentrate to the plant process water network.
- Cake moisture compliance with TML (Note 3): Note 3 mandates concentrate cake moisture must remain strictly below the Transportable Moisture Limit (TML = 9.8% w/w; target 8.5% w/w) for bulk ocean transport.
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — Thickener Diameter: RB-4410-PS-TH5101 Rev B specifies 30 m, whereas RB-4410-PID-51-001 Rev B, RB-4410-PS-TH5101 Rev B specifies 25 (P&ID) / 30 (PDS) m — verify with engineer before HAZOP
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — THICKENER DIAMETER: Operating Manual RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2 and Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-TH5101 Rev B specify 30 m diameter, whereas P&ID RB-4410-PID-51-001 Rev B marks 25 m — verify thickener sizing and rise rate with lead process engineer before HAZOP.
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — Underflow Solids Concentration: RB-4410-PFD-003 Rev B specifies 65.0 % w/w, whereas RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2 specifies 65 % solids w/w — verify with engineer before HAZOP
- Anionic polyacrylamide flocculant slip & handling hazards (CAS 9003-05-8 per SDS_9003-05-8_anionic-polyacrylamide-flocculant.pdf): Polymer spills around thickener TH-5101 walkways, launders, and dosing manifold are extremely slippery when wet; wash down immediately with copious water or absorb with inert solids; handling neat polymer or concentrated solution requires chemical goggles, nitrile gloves, and dust/mist respirator; store cool and dry away from acids and ignition sources; firefighting media: dry chemical, CO2, foam (do not use water jet on burning xanthate in adjacent reagent storage).
