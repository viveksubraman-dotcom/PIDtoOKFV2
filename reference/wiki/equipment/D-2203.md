# D-2203 — Oxidizer Chilled Vent Gas Separator

> ⚠️ CHP is present in the vent gas from oxidizers. This separator handles condensate from chilled vent gas. Cold service (5°C, MDMT −10°C) — thermal shock risk if steam is admitted improperly. See [[hazards/cumene-hydroperoxide]].

## Function

Separates liquid condensate (primarily Cumene + water) from chilled oxidizer exhaust vent gas. The vent gas from OX-2201 and OX-2202 contains evaporated Cumene and air. Chilling condenses the Cumene and water vapors; D-2203 knocks out the liquid phase before the gas is sent to the Charcoal Adsorbers (D-2204A/B/C) for residual Cumene recovery.

**Key design feature — elevation requirement**: D-2203 must be located at least 2500 mm above the top of the Decanter D-2205 so that condensate drains by gravity from D-2203 → D-2205. This is a layout constraint specified on the data sheet.

**Cold service**: MDMT of −10°C is driven by the 5°C operating temperature. Cold insulation (C-type) prevents ice formation and maintains the low temperature needed for effective vapor condensation.

## Comparison with D-2208 (Warm Vent Gas Separator)

D-2203 is the CHILLED separator; [[equipment/D-2208]] is the WARM separator. Both have identical geometry (2800 mm ID × 3700 mm TL) and the same mist eliminator design, but operate at very different conditions:

| Parameter | D-2203 (Chilled) | D-2208 (Warm) |
|-----------|-----------------|---------------|
| Oper. Temperature | 5°C | 43°C |
| Oper. Pressure | 0.06 kg/cm²g | 0.12 kg/cm²g |
| MDMT | −10°C | 15°C |
| Insulation | C(50) cold | None |
| Min elev. above D-2205 | 2500 mm | 600 mm |
| SG | 0.9064 | 0.8453 |
