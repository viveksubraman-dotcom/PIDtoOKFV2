## Function

Collects steam condensate from the Cumene Column reboiler in the Alkylation Section. The pot separates any flash steam and discharges liquid condensate for return to the condensate recovery system. High-pressure design (50 kg/cm²g) reflects the high-pressure steam used to drive the Cumene Column reboiler at ~260°C.
