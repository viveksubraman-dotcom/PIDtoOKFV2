# D-2303 — Decomposer Feed Flush Drum

> ⚠️ CHP is a peroxide — thermal decomposition risk. See [[hazards/cumene-hydroperoxide]].

**Technology:** UOP CDN Process | **Plant:** PTT Phenol Train II (PPCL)

## Function

D-2303 is a small flush drum (constructed from 24"OD pipe, 1800mm long) located between the Flash Column Bottoms Pumps ([[equipment/P-2301AB]]) and the Decomposer Drum ([[equipment/D-2304]]). It serves two simultaneous roles:

1. **Startup permissive reservoir**: D-2303 must contain adequate liquid (≥85% of span per Note 5) before the Decomposer can start. This ensures flush fluid is available before CHP is admitted to the Decomposer loop.
2. **Process water mixing point**: Process water is injected here via ratio controller XY-1201 (DECOMPOSER WATER ADDITION/FEED RATIO). Water content in the Decomposer feed is critical for controlling the acid-catalyzed CHP decomposition chemistry.

## Critical Startup Permissive

> **Note 5 (Drawing 0012A):** "WHEN THE LEVEL IN THE DECOMPOSER FEED FLUSH DRUM IS HIGHER THEN LSL (85% OF SPAN), START-UP WILL BE ENABLE."

Label **"FLUSH RESERVOIR FULL (START-UP ENABLE)"** appears on P&ID 0012A adjacent to the level instruments. The LXSLL-1201 SIS level switch is the physical implementation: when D-2303 level drops below 85% of span (~1250mm calculated from LLL/HLL), the Decomposer cannot start. This permissive was added per SIL verification Rev S3.

## Pressure Control — CHP Nitrogen Blanket

Split-range pressure control (same configuration as [[equipment/D-2302]]):
- **PIC-1202** — Pressure indicating controller
- **PY-1202A** — Low pressure signal opens (N₂ supply valve)
- **PY-1202B** — High pressure signal opens (vent valve)

Nitrogen supply: **CHP Nitrogen Header** from 53-0051 (1"-N-S3-052048-B1A1-NI) — dedicated circuit, must not be mixed with regular facility nitrogen. See N₂ segregation note in [[instruments/sis-cdn]].
