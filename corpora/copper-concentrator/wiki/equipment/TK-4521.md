# TK-4521 — Milk-of-Lime Tank

## Function

Milk-of-Lime Tank TK-4521 stores and conditions 150 m³ of 20% w/w Ca(OH)2 hydrated lime slurry received from upstream packaged lime slaker SL-4521 (governed by vendor drawing per Note 3 of RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B). The slurry is metered and dosed directly into flotation conditioning tank TK-4101 under closed-loop control by analyzer indicating controller AIC-4101 (target rougher feed pH 10.5, AL 10.0 / AH 11.2) for slurry pH adjustment and pyrite depression ahead of rougher flotation cells FC-4101. Dosing rates are governed by Operating Manual RB-4410-OM-001 per drawing Note 4. Registered in Mechanical Equipment List RB-4410-PID-00-002 Rev B and P&ID RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B. Atmospheric 150 m³ storage tank in Area 45 storing and conditioning 20 % w/w Ca(OH)2 hydrated lime slurry prepared from quicklime in slaker SL-4521. Operating Manual RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2 notes the strongly exothermic nature of lime slaking in SL-4521, mandating full chemical face shields and gauntlets. Dosed under closed-loop control via AIC-4101 to conditioning tank TK-4101 to maintain rougher feed alkalinity at target pH 10.5. ## Hazards & Safeguards

- Caustic chemical burn and severe eye hazard: Calcium hydroxide (Ca(OH)2) slurry at 20% w/w is strongly alkaline (pH > 12) and causes severe ocular tissue destruction and chemical skin burns. Personnel require full chemical splash goggles, face shield, and neoprene/nitrile PPE with safety shower / eye wash access within 10 seconds.
- Slurry sedimentation and line sanding: Milk-of-lime slurry rapidly settles and forms hard scale/plugs in transfer spools and tank bottom upon loss of agitation; automated water flushing and recirculating loop design required.
- Alkalinity excursion risks: Under-dosing (pH < 10.0) fails to depress pyrite, causing grade degradation and promoting spontaneous decomposition of PAX into toxic and flammable CS2 gas. Over-dosing (pH > 11.2) causes chalcopyrite depression and severe carbonate line scaling.
- Severe alkaline caustic burns to skin and permanent blindness risk upon eye contact.
- Slurry settling and ring main line plugging if agitation is lost.
- Caustic chemical burn and severe eye hazard: Calcium hydroxide (Ca(OH)2) slurry at 20% w/w is strongly alkaline (pH > 12) and causes severe ocular tissue destruction and chemical skin burns. Full chemical PPE and safety shower/eyewash within 10 seconds mandatory.
- Slurry sedimentation and line sanding: Milk-of-lime slurry rapidly settles and forms hard scale/plugs in transfer spools upon loss of agitation; continuous tank agitation and recirculating ring main loop required.
- Reagent dosing governance: Dosing rates are governed per Operating Manual RB-4410-OM-001 (Note 4 of RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B).
- Vendor scope battery limit: Lime slaker SL-4521 is on vendor package drawing per Note 3 of RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B.
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — Connected Equipment (Downstream): RB-4410-PS-TK4521 Rev B, RB-4410-PFD-002 Rev B specifies TK-4101, whereas RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B, RB-4410-PS-TK4521 Rev B specifies TK-4101 (Flotation conditioning tank receiving lime dosing) — verify with engineer before HAZOP
- EXOTHERMIC SLAKING & CAUSTIC CORROSION: Quicklime slaking in SL-4521 is violently exothermic. Concentrated Ca(OH)2 slurry causes severe chemical burns and irreversible corneal destruction; full face shields and chemical gloves mandatory per RB-4410-OM-001 Sec 8.
- GHS Health Hazards: Classified as Skin corrosion 1B / serious eye damage 1 per SDS_1305-78-8_quicklime---hydrated-lime.pdf; contact causes irreversible corneal opacification, blindness, and chemical necrosis.
- Exothermic reaction hazard with water: Quicklime slaking in SL-4521 is violently exothermic per SDS_1305-78-8_quicklime---hydrated-lime.pdf and RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2 Sec 8. Dilution water failure causes boiling and caustic eruption.
- Cross-reagent firefighting directive (Area 45): Extinguish with dry chemical, CO2, or foam; do not use water jet on burning xanthate in adjacent Area 45 storage per SDS Section 5.
