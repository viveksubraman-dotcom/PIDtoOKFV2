# E-2309 — Crude Product Cooler

> ⚠️ CHP is a peroxide — thermal decomposition risk. See [[hazards/cumene-hydroperoxide]].

**Technology:** UOP CDN Process | **Plant:** PTT Phenol Train II (PPCL)

## Function

E-2309 is the final cooler in the CDN decomposition sub-section. It reduces crude product temperature from ~135°C (dehydrator outlet) to ~43°C using cooling water (CWS) from Unit 61 before the product enters the Direct Neutralization Static Mixers (P&ID 0019). This is the last controlled cool-down opportunity before the product stream leaves the decomposition boundary.

## Dehydrator Reactor Loop — X-2312

X-2312, the **Dehydrator Reactor Loop**, is the piping circuit of the dehydration reaction zone. Its total volume is constrained per UOP Project Specification 963766-840 (Residence Time Restriction):

| Parameter | Value | Unit | Source |
|-----------|-------|------|--------|
| Total volume (flange to flange) | 0.48 | m³ | Drawing 0014A, item data box |
| Middle loop volume | 0.24 | m³ | Note 3: shall be 50% of total |

The multi-loop design (outer loop + middle loop + possibly inner loop) gives flexibility in residence time for the DMBA→AMS dehydration reaction. The 0.24 m³ middle loop = 50% constraint ensures proportional sizing between the dehydration loops.
