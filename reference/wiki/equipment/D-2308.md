## Function

Collects steam condensate from the Preflash Column Steam Heater (E-2303). Level in the drum is controlled by LIC-0502, which regulates return of condensate to the steam condensate return system. Condensate is pumped by P-2308A/B.

The drum provides a surge volume to decouple E-2303 condensate drainage from the condensate return system. Design temperature of 195°C and design pressure of 7 kg/cm²g match E-2303 tube side ratings.
