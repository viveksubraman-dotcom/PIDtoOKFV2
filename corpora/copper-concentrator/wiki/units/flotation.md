## Reagent Handling & Safety Standards (RB-4410-OM-001 Sec 8 & SDS)
- **Carbon Disulfide Off-Gas (Decomposition Product, CAS 75-15-0, SDS_75-15-0_carbon-disulfide.pdf):** Evolved in the enclosed `TK-4501` room as the primary volatile hydrolysis product during potassium amyl xanthate (PAX) batch preparation. Acute neurotoxin with ACGIH 8-hour TWA of 1 ppm (Skin notation; systemic percutaneous absorption risk) and IDLH of 500 ppm. Flash point of -30 °C, auto-ignition temperature of 90 °C, and Lower Explosive Limit (LEL) of 1.3 %. Requires continuous 24/7 mechanical exhaust ventilation via extraction fan `FN-4501` maintaining negative pressure in the mixing enclosure per P&ID `RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B` Note 1. Continuous ambient $CS_2$ gas detector `AT-4501` (alarm at 10 % LEL) interlocked to switch `FN-4501` to high speed and execute an automatic trip of the solid PAX feeder per HAZOP recommendation `ACT-HAZOP-45-03` (Node HZ-45-03). Firefighting with dry chemical, $CO_2$, or foam; **do not use water jet on burning xanthate**.
- **Polyglycol Ether Frother DF-250 (Alternative Frother, CAS 37286-64-9, SDS_37286-64-9_polyglycol-ether-frother.pdf):** Evaluated as an alternative flotation frother (polypropylene glycol methyl ether); explicitly designated on SDS as *"Alternative frother (not specified for TK-4511)"*. Combustible liquid (closed-cup flash point >100 °C, eye irritant). Confirms that DF-250 is an alternative reagent rather than the baseline fill, clarifying the P&ID `RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B` labeling discrepancy against Process Data Sheet `RB-4410-PS-TK4511 Rev B` and Operating Manual `RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2` which specify MIBC (flash point 41 °C, Zone 2). Firefighting with dry chemical, CO2, or foam; do not use water jet on burning xanthate. HAZOP recommendation ACT-HAZOP-45-06 governs reconciliation.
- **PAX Collector (TK-4501 / TK-4502):** Delivered as solid pellets (potassium amyl xanthate, CAS 2720-73-2, chemical formula $C_6H_{11}KOS_2$). Classified as GHS Self-heating solid. Mixed to 20 % w/v aqueous collector solution in 40 m³ 316L stainless steel tank `TK-4501` and buffered in `TK-4502`. Dissolution make-up water must be kept strictly below 40 °C to prevent thermal hydrolysis into toxic and flammable carbon disulfide ($CS_2$, LEL 1.3 %, auto-ignition 90 °C). The enclosed mixing room extraction fan `FN-4501` must operate continuously 24/7 whenever PAX is present; ambient $CS_2$ detector `AT-4501` interlocked to `FN-4501` per HAZOP `ACT-HAZOP-45-03`. Non-sparking tools, chemical splash goggles, and nitrile gauntlets are mandatory per SDS `SDS_2720-73-2_potassium-amyl-xanthate.pdf`. Firefighting: dry chemical, $\text{CO}_2$, or foam; **do not use water jet on burning xanthate**. Target dosing: **25 g/t dry ore**.
- **Frother Reagent (TK-4511):** Stored in 30 m³ tank containing liquid MIBC (methyl isobutyl carbinol, CAS 108-11-2, formula C6H14O). GHS Flammable liquid Cat 3, flash point 41 °C, STOT SE 3. Tank bund and surrounding area classified Zone 2. Handlers require chemical goggles, nitrile gloves, and local exhaust ventilation. Target dosing: **20 g/t dry ore**. ⚠️ CONFLICT: P&ID RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B marks DF-250 polyglycol ether; HAZOP Node HZ-45-06 requires confirmation (ACT-HAZOP-45-06).
- **Hydrated Lime & Quicklime (SL-4521 / TK-4521):** Quicklime ($\text{CaO}$, CAS 1305-78-8) is slaked in vendor slaker `SL-4521` (strongly exothermic hydration). Classified as GHS Skin corrosion 1B / serious eye damage 1 per SDS `SDS_1305-78-8_quicklime---hydrated-lime.pdf`. Handling slurry requires full face shields, chemical goggles, nitrile gauntlets, and particulate/mist respirators. Safety shower and eyewash stations within 10 seconds. Slurry is stored as 20% w/w $\text{Ca(OH)}_2$ (CAS 1305-62-0) in 150 m³ tank `TK-4521` and dosed to conditioning tank `TK-4101` via `AIC-4101` targeting **pH 10.5** (AL 10.0 / AH 11.2). Firefighting with dry chemical, $\text{CO}_2$, or foam; do not use water jet on burning xanthate in Area 45.
- **PAX Collector (TK-4501):** Delivered as solid pellets, mixed to 20 % w/v in 40 m³ 316L stainless steel tank. Dissolution water must be kept strictly below 40 °C. The enclosed mixing room extraction fan `FN-4501` must operate continuously whenever PAX is present to prevent toxic and explosive carbon disulfide ($CS_2$, LEL 1.3 %, auto-ignition 90 °C) accumulation. Non-sparking tools mandatory. Target dosing: **25 g/t dry ore**.
- **Hydrated Lime (TK-4521):** Quicklime is slaked in vendor slaker `SL-4521` (strongly exothermic reaction). Handling slurry requires face shields and chemical gauntlets. Dosed to conditioning tank `TK-4101` via `AIC-4101` targeting **pH 10.5**.

## Circuit Summary
The Flotation and Regrind Circuit comprises pulp conditioning and rougher-scavenger flotation (Area 4100 / Area 41), rougher concentrate regrind milling (Area 4200 / Area 43), and multi-stage cleaner flotation (Area 4300). The circuit processes **2,688 t/h** of classified slurry solids (Stream S-324) from secondary grinding hydrocyclones CY-3201 at $P_{80} = 150\text{ µm}$, yielding **51 t/h** of copper concentrate (Stream S-431) and rejecting **2,637 t/h** of final flotation tailings (Stream S-611) [^1]. Forced aeration air is supplied to the rougher cells by low-pressure blowers `BL-4101A/B` under flow controller `FIC-4102`, and inter-cell slurry levels are regulated by modulating dart valves [^9].

## Rougher Cell Configuration & Aeration (RB-4410-PS-FC4101 & RB-4410-PID-41-001)
Rougher flotation in Area 4100 / Area 41 is conducted across a bank of 7 forced-air mechanical tank cells configured in series (tagged FC-4101 through FC-4107) [^3] [^9]:
- **Bank Configuration:** $7 \times 300\text{ m}^3$ cells providing $2,100\text{ m}^3$ total active volume.
- **Slurry Residence Time:** 32 minutes design retention time at $5,969\text{ m}^3\text{/h}$ slurry feed rate.
- **Air Addition & Blowers:** Low-pressure aeration air blowers `BL-4101A/B` (duty/standby) deliver forced air to the bank. Airflow is regulated under closed-loop control by flow controller `FIC-4102` to maintain superficial air velocity between $0.8 - 1.6\text{ m/s}$ supplied to rotor-stator dispersion mechanisms [^3] [^9].
- **Agitation Power:** Installed motor power of $250\text{ kW}$ per cell ($1,750\text{ kW}$ total bank installed power).
- **Pulp Level Regulation:** Per Note 3 of `RB-4410-PID-41-001 Rev B`, slurry level in each cell is individually regulated by modulating dart valves (typical across all seven cells), ensuring consistent froth recovery across the bank.
- **Process Connections:** Feed from conditioning tank `TK-4101`, concentrate froth advancing via Stream S-411 to regrind mill `ML-4201`, and underflow tailings discharging via Stream S-611 to tailings thickener `TH-6101` [^1] [^9].

## Operating Windows & Safety Interlocks
1. **Pulp Alkalinity Regulation (AIC-4101):** Note 1 of `RB-4410-PID-41-001 Rev B` establishes closed-loop lime addition to conditioning tank `TK-4101` on pH control `AIC-4101` targeting **10.5 pH** (operating boundaries AL 10.0 / AH 11.2). If pH drops below AL 10.0, pyrite floatability increases dramatically, diluting rougher concentrate and overloading regrind mill ML-4201, while accelerating xanthate decomposition into flammable CS2. If pH exceeds AH 11.2, chalcopyrite depression occurs, degrading total recovery [^1] [^2] [^9].
2. **Rougher Mass Pull Control (8.0%):** Mass pull is controlled around 215 t/h solids. Excessive pull overfills regrind mill ML-4201 and degrades cleaner column capacity; insufficient pull loses slow-floating fine copper particles to tailings S-611 [^1].
3. **Flotation Aeration Air Window (FIC-4102):** Superficial air rate must be maintained within **0.8–1.6 m/s** via blowers `BL-4101A/B` and controller `FIC-4102`. Airflow below 0.8 m/s leads to froth starvation and massive tailings copper loss; airflow above 1.6 m/s causes violent cell boiling and gangue entrainment [^3] [^9].
4. **Flotation Cell Dart Valve Level Control (Note 3):** Level control per cell by dart valves prevents pulp level surges. Jamming or scaling of dart valves causes immediate slurry overflow into concentrate launders or froth collapse [^9].
5. **Flotation Cell Sanding & Emergency Dump:** Flotation cell agitating mechanisms (7 x 250 kW) must be purged with high-pressure flushing water upon power outages. Unplanned slurry settlement causes sanding of rotors and jamming of dart valves [^1] [^3] [^9].
6. **Regrind Feed Low-Flow Interlock:** Slurry feed flow to ML-4201 must be interlocked with the 3,000 kW mill motor. Running without slurry feed causes thermal boiling, media breakage, and lockup of the ceramic media bed [^5].
7. **Cleaner Column Froth Washing & Bias Control:** Continuous wash water addition of 35 m³/h must be supplied to FC-4301 to preserve positive froth bias and reject entrained gangue, maintaining final concentrate quality at 26.0% Cu [^4].

## Concentrate Regrind Mill Configuration & Operation (RB-4410-PS-ML4201)
Rougher concentrate regrinding in Area 4200 / Area 43 is performed in vertical stirred mill `ML-4201` [^5]:
- **Mill Mechanism & Sizing:** Vertical stirred grinding mill powered by an installed **3,000 kW** electric drive motor, operating with an agitated vertical screw/impeller assembly.
- **Grinding Media Selection:** Charged with **3–5 mm ceramic beads**, providing high attrition energy intensity while eliminating iron media contamination that impairs sulfide mineral flotation collectors.
- **Liberation Target:** Comminutes rougher concentrate solids (Stream S-411 at 215 t/h solids, 580 m³/h slurry at 30.0 wt% solids) from rougher flotation size down to $P_{80} = 38\ \mu\text{m}$ to detach composite chalcopyrite/bornite grains from iron sulfides (pyrite) and silicate gangue.
- **Process Connections:** Directly receives Stream S-411 from rougher cells `FC-4101` and discharges by gravity flume to cleaner flotation column `FC-4301`.

## Cleaner Flotation Column Configuration & Operation (RB-4410-PS-FC4301)
Cleaner flotation in Area 4300 is performed in vertical pneumatic column `FC-4301` [^4]:
- **Column Geometry:** Diameter of 4.5 m and total height of 12.0 m providing deep froth retention and optimal bubble-particle disengagement.
- **Wash Water System:** Design counter-current wash water rate of **35 m³/h** applied over the top froth bed to preserve positive bias and reject fine gangue entrainment.
- **Product Quality:** Upgrades reground rougher concentrate ($P_{80} = 38\text{ µm}$) to final commercial specification of **26.0% Cu** concentrate grade.
- **Process Connections:** Directly receives discharge from Regrind Mill `ML-4201` and transfers final cleaned concentrate (Stream S-431 at 51 t/h solids, 149 m³/h slurry) by gravity launder to Concentrate Thickener `TH-5101`.
