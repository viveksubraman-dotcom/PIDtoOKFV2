# OX-2202 — Oxidizer No.2

> ⚠️ CHP is produced in this vessel. Cumene + O₂ reaction is exothermic. Temperature must be closely controlled — CHP thermal decomposition onset ~80°C. See [[hazards/cumene-hydroperoxide]].

## Function

Second-stage oxidation reactor in the Hock (Cumene) Process — identical design to OX-2201 but with minor operating differences. Parallel operation with OX-2201 increases total plant oxidation capacity.

Key differences from OX-2201:
- Slightly higher aerated SG (0.81 vs 0.80) — OX-2202 oxidate has marginally higher CHP concentration
- Higher maximum pumpout rate (1,419 vs 1,138 m³/hr) — OX-2202 has larger emergency drawdown capacity
- Operating temperature 82°C vs 86°C for OX-2201
- Vacuum vent valves designated PVRV-1401A/B (vs PVRV-1001A/B)

## Key Nozzles

Nozzle schedule is identical to OX-2201 (same nozzle designations A, B, D, F, G, H, K, L, M, N, P, Q, R, SI–S6, T1–T12, U, V, W, X). See [[equipment/OX-2201]] for nozzle table.

*External connections: ASME CL150 except D, G, Q, X = CL300. All flanges: Raised Face.*

## Safety Notes

- Identical safety considerations to OX-2201 — see [[equipment/OX-2201]] for full safety notes
- Temperature control critical: 82°C operating temperature provides ~0°C margin vs 86°C for OX-2201, somewhat closer to the safe operating upper limit
- PVRV-1401A/B: same specification as OX-2201 PVRV-1001A/B — dual redundant vacuum relief on roof

## Connections

Identical connection arrangement to OX-2201:
- **Feed (F, 16"):** Cumene from D-2201 Combined Feed Surge Drum
- **Air inlet (D, 2×18"):** Scrubbed compressed air from D-2207 Compressed Air Scrubber
- **Vapor outlet (A, 30"):** Vent gas to OXI vent gas separators
- **Circulation outlet (B, 24"):** To oxidizer circulation pumps → back via eductors
