# D-2310 — 98% Sulfuric Acid Injection Tank

> ⚠️ CHP is a peroxide — thermal decomposition risk. See [[hazards/cumene-hydroperoxide]].
> ⚠️ **98% Sulfuric acid — severe contact hazard.** Dilution heat may cause boiling and spattering.

## Function

D-2310 is the working (day) tank that supplies 98% H₂SO₄ catalyst to the six Decomposer Acid Injection Pumps (P-2305A–F). It is kept filled from the Acid Refill Tank [[equipment/D-2311]] via a gravity/transfer line. Level in D-2310 is continuously monitored and controlled.

## Operating Conditions

Low operating pressure (≈ atmospheric with slight positive head). Operated at ambient temperature ~40°C. The 3.5 kg/cm²g design pressure provides safety margin for fire case or utility connection pressure. MDMT of 15°C means the vessel is rated for low ambient winter conditions without impact testing requirement below 15°C.

| FF-1501B | Level Switch | Low level (LO2) |

| PCV-1507 | Pressure Control Valve | Vent pressure control; set at 7 mm H₂O (very low — near atmospheric) |
| HOSM-D2310 | Hand-Operated Switch | Manual drain control |

**Note on PCV-1507:** Set point of 7 mm H₂O is effectively atmospheric — maintains slight positive pressure to prevent air ingress and moisture pickup into the 98% H₂SO₄ (which is hygroscopic and would lose concentration if exposed to atmosphere).

## Connections

- **Feed inlet:** From [[equipment/D-2311]] (Acid Refill Tank) via gravity fill line
- **Vent:** To X-2303 (Acid Conservation Vent) — controls vapors from tank
- **Discharge:** To [[equipment/P-2305ABCDEF]] (Decomposer Acid Injection Pumps A–F) via 3/8" line
- **Drain:** To [[equipment/X-2320]] (Acid Neutralization System), then to oily water sewer (91-0058)
