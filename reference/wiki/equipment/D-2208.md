## Elevation Constraint

> **Note:** Vessel elevation must be ≥ 600 mm above top of Decanter D-2205. Condensate drains by gravity from D-2208 → D-2205.

## Function

Separates entrained liquid droplets from the warm oxidizer vent gas stream before routing to the Charcoal Adsorbers (D-2204A/B/C). Handles warmer, less-condensed vent gas compared to the Chilled Vent Gas Separator D-2203 — hence higher operating temperature (43°C vs 5°C), higher specific gravity (0.8453 vs 0.9064 for D-2203), and no cold insulation. The liquid outlet nozzle W03 is 6" (vs 3" on D-2203), reflecting higher condensate load at the warmer operating temperature.

Both D-2208 and [[equipment/D-2203]] feed vapor to the same Charcoal Adsorbers downstream.
