# P-2304A — Acid Aromatics Sump Pumps

## Function

P-2304A are the main transfer pumps for the CDN Acid Aromatics Drain System. They take suction from [[equipment/D-2307]] (Acid Aromatics Sump vessel) and pump the collected acid aromatics — a mixture of phenol, organic acids, and aromatics — to the Sodium Phenate Tank at the Phenol Recovery Section for product recovery.

**Note 3 (Drawing 0020A):** Common Spare is **P-13045** (maintained in the same motor type family, Type D). When P-2304A requires maintenance, P-13045 is deployed as the spare.

**WT-23-2001 Weigh Transmitter:** Unusual instrument — measures mass flow gravimetrically in addition to volumetric FIC-2001. Used for total acid aromatics accounting in the drain system.

## Motor Control

- **Type D motor:** Standard UOP arrangement as per drawing DWG 14780-8120-25-23-0001F. Controls include start/stop from CCR and local.
- **XC-2304 common alarm:** Any anomaly (high vibration, high temperature, seal failure) generates common alarm XC-2304 to DCS.
- **LOW LEVEL STOP:** Automatic pump trip on D-2307 LS-2005 (LLL 1030mm) to prevent cavitation/dry running.
