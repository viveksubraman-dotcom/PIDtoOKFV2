## Operating Conditions

| Condition | Pressure | Temperature | Unit |
|-----------|----------|-------------|------|
| Normal (adsorption) | 0.05 | 16 | kg/cm²g / °C |
| Regeneration (steam) | 0.2 | 119 | kg/cm²g / °C |
| Regeneration (design) | 0.2 | 119 | kg/cm²g / °C |

**Cycle time:** 6 hours (adsorption → regeneration → cooling → back to adsorption)

## Function

Removes residual Cumene from the oxidizer exhaust air before the air is vented to atmosphere. After chilling and liquid knock-out in D-2203 (and warm knock-out in D-2208), the vent gas still contains trace Cumene. Three horizontal vessels operate in rotation: one adsorbing, one regenerating with steam, one cooling. Activated carbon adsorbs the Cumene; regeneration steam strips and recovers the Cumene.

**Three-vessel rotation**: Because regeneration requires heating to 119°C and then cooling back to 16°C, continuous operation requires three vessels — while one is adsorbing, one is being regenerated (steamed), and one is cooling. This arrangement achieves continuous vent gas treatment.

**Severe cyclic service**: The thermal swing from 16°C to 119°C every 6 hours imposes severe fatigue loading. This drives the ASME Div. 1 code requirement with mandatory cyclic-service inspection provisions.

## Connections

- Vapor inlet (AI, 30"): From D-2203 Chilled Vent Gas Separator and D-2208 Warm Vent Gas Separator (vent gas)
- Vapor outlet (BN, 30"): Cleaned vent gas to atmosphere (or to D-2211 during regeneration/cooldown)
- Steam inlet (AS, 18"): Regeneration steam supply
- Condensate outlet (BH, 4"): Steam + desorbed Cumene condensate → D-2211 KO Drum → D-2205 Decanter
- Vent (BV, 2"): To flare/vent header

## Safety Notes

> ⚠️ Activated carbon in adsorption service can generate exothermic oxidation if oxygen-depleted gas bypasses the system and fresh air contacts hot carbon. Monitor bed temperatures (BX, BY, BZ). Steam inerting before and after regeneration is critical.

Regeneration condensate contains Cumene — route to D-2211 KO Drum for liquid recovery before venting.
