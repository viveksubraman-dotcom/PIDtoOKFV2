# TK-3105 — SAG Mill Lube Oil Reservoir

## Function

Lube oil storage and conditioning reservoir supplying high-pressure hydrostatic lift pumps PP-3105A/B and low-pressure hydrodynamic lubrication loops for the trunnion bearings of SAG Mill ML-3101. Sized with an operating holding capacity of 12 m3 of ISO VG 460 lubricating oil. Receives relief valve return from PSV-3105 and gravity drain returns from the mill trunnion bearing housings. Note that auxiliary oil coolers, duplex filtration assemblies, and immersion tank heaters are omitted from drawing RB-4410-PID-31-002 Rev B per Note 3 and reside in vendor documentation.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- Loss of reservoir lube oil inventory or oil starvation causing pump cavitation, loss of hydrostatic lift pressure, and catastrophic trunnion bearing wipe on SAG Mill ML-3101.
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — PSV-3105 Set Pressure Discrepancy: Drawing RB-4410-PID-31-002 Rev B specifies PSV-3105 set pressure as 180 bar(g) (which also contradicts drawing Note 1 stating set pressure shall not exceed system design pressure of 170 bar(g)), whereas Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-0032 Rev B specifies set pressure as 160 bar(g) — verify with mechanical/piping engineer before HAZOP.
- Thermal breakdown and viscosity loss: Lubricating oil temperature must be maintained within allowable limits (monitored by TT-3101) to ensure proper viscosity for hydrodynamic wedge formation and hydrostatic lift; auxiliary heaters and coolers are omitted from P&ID per Note 3.
- Particulate contamination: Metallic debris or particulate contamination in circulating oil accelerates white metal Babbitt wear; duplex filter status and differential pressure monitoring are omitted from P&ID per Note 3 and must be verified in vendor package.
