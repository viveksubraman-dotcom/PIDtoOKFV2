# FC-4301 — Cleaner Flotation Column

## Function

Cleaner flotation column performing final upgrading of reground rougher concentrate (Stream S-411 ground in ML-4201 to P80 = 38 µm) into commercial-grade copper concentrate (Stream S-431 at 51 t/h solids, 149 m3/h slurry, 28.0 wt% solids). Employs counter-current froth washing with 35 m3/h wash water across a 4.5 m diameter x 12 m height column geometry to reject entrained silicate and pyrite gangue, achieving target concentrate grade of 26.0% Cu before advancing by gravity launder to concentrate thickener TH-5101. Cleaner flotation column (4.5 m diameter x 12.0 m height) operating in Area 4300. Produces final commercial-grade copper concentrate (26.0% Cu) with 35 m3/h counter-current wash water. Discharges final concentrate to thickener TH-5101 via Stream S-431. Registered in Mechanical Equipment List RB-4410-PID-00-002 Rev B. Pneumatic cleaner flotation column in Area 43 performing final cleaning on reground rougher concentrate. Sized at 4.5 m diameter by 12 m height. Employs 35 m³/h counter-current froth wash water to reject fine non-sulfide gangue entrainment, producing 50.6 t/h of final concentrate at 26 % Cu commercial grade (Stream S-431) discharging to concentrate thickener TH-5101 per RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- ⚠️ CONFLICT — CONCENTRATE PRODUCTION CAPACITY: RB-4410-PFD-002 Rev B (Stream S-431) specifies 51 t/h solids (149 m3/h slurry @ 28 wt% solids), whereas RB-4410-PFD-000 Rev B specifies 50.6 t/h solids — verify with engineer before HAZOP to ensure TH-5101 thickener drive torque and FP-5101 filter press cycle times are sized for 51 t/h.
- Wash water pressure loss causing unwashed entrained non-sulfide gangue to report to final concentrate, degrading copper grade below 26.0% Cu.
- Sparger plugging or air pressure decay in flotation column collapsing bubbly flow regime and dropping stage recovery.
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — CONCENTRATE PRODUCTION CAPACITY: RB-4410-PFD-002 Rev B and RB-4410-PFD-003 Rev B (Stream S-431) specify 51 t/h solids (149 m3/h slurry @ 28 wt% solids), whereas RB-4410-PFD-000 Rev B specifies 50.6 t/h solids — verify with engineer before HAZOP to ensure TH-5101 thickener drive torque and FP-5101 filter press cycle times are sized for 51 t/h.
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — CONCENTRATE PRODUCTION CAPACITY: RB-4410-PFD-002 Rev B and RB-4410-PFD-003 Rev B (Stream S-431) specify 51 t/h solids (149 m3/h slurry @ 28 wt% solids), whereas RB-4410-PFD-000 Rev B specifies 50.6 t/h solids (26.0% Cu grade) — verify with engineer before HAZOP to ensure TH-5101 thickener drive torque and FP-5101 filter press cycle times are sized for 51 t/h.
- Wash water pressure loss or starvation (<35 m3/h): Causes loss of positive hydraulic bias, allowing unwashed entrained non-sulfide gangue and fine pyrite to report to final concentrate, degrading copper grade below the 26.0% Cu commercial target.
- Excessive wash water addition (>35 m3/h): Causes localized froth boiling, deep froth collapse, and excess slurry dilution entering downstream concentrate thickener TH-5101, reducing thickener underflow compaction.
- Column sparger plugging or air supply decay: Collapses bubbly flow regime, destroys gas holdup, and precipitates chalcopyrite particles down into cleaner tailings recycle, diminishing recovery.
- Column pulp-froth interface level excursion: Dart valve failure or tailing pump surge causes pulp carryover into concentrate launder (catastrophic grade collapse) or froth bed drowning.
- Wash water starvation causing gangue entrainment and concentrate grade drop below 26.0% Cu.
- Froth bed collapse upon excessive wash water addition.
- WASH WATER STARVATION: Loss of wash water (<35 m3/h) collapses positive froth bias, allowing gangue entrainment into final concentrate and dropping copper grade below commercial specification (<26% Cu) per RB-4410-OM-001 Sec 9.
