*2.2 kW motors — condensate service. Higher specific gravity at pumping temp (0.931) reflects the higher operating temperature (135°C) vs P-2308A/B (0.947 at 63°C). Type L motor control drives SIS connectivity despite small motor size.*

**FY-0703** provides AUTO-START: when flow drops below the low threshold (running pump tripped), standby pump auto-starts. Same logic as [[equipment/P-2308AB]] via FY-0502.

## Motor Control Type L and SIS Connection

- Motor control: **Type L** per Drawing 0001F (confirmed by Note 1 on Drawing 0007A)
- SIS: Connected to UC-2301 Concentration Section
- TSH-23-0704 (P-2309A bearing temperature)
- TSH-23-0705 (P-2309B bearing temperature)
- Both pumps STOP on UC-2301 ESD
