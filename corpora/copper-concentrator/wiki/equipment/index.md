# Equipment Concept

* [BL-4101A — Rougher Flotation Air Blowers (BL-4101A/B)](BL-4101A.md) - Low-pressure centrifugal air blowers configured in duty/standby service (BL-4101A/B) supplying continuous forced aeration air to the rougher flotation tank cells (FC-4101 to FC-4107) in Area 41. Air is delivered through a common header regulated by flow indicating controller FIC-4102 to maintain superficial air velocity between 0.8 and 1.6 m/s across the 7 x 300 m3 cells, sustaining selective bubble-particle attachment and mineralized froth formation for copper-gold flotation.
* [CR-2101 — Primary Gyratory Crusher](CR-2101.md) - Primary 60 x 113 gyratory crusher receiving run-of-mine ore (up to 5,500 t/h peak) via dump pocket/feeder FE-2101 and reducing rock to SAG mill feed size (P80 = 150 mm, CSS = 165 mm). Discharges to CV-2101 overland conveyor advancing to coarse ore stockpile ST-2101 via stream S-101. Lubrication and hydroset systems are engineered per vendor drawing VD-2101. Registered in Mechanical Equipment List RB-4410-PID-00-002 Rev B and detailed on P&ID RB-4410-PID-21-001 Rev B. Primary gyratory crusher (60 x 113) reducing run-of-mine ore down to P80 150 mm at up to 5,500 t/h design throughput to feed the coarse ore stockpile via discharge conveyor CV-2101 per RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2. Sized with 1,200 kW installed drive motor. Corroborates 1,200 kW drive rating against 1,000 kW in the mechanical equipment list.
* [CR-3102 — Pebble Crusher](CR-3102.md) - Cone crusher (extra-coarse) operating in the SAG mill closed circuit. Receives critical-size pebbles (typically 25–65 mm) screened from the SAG mill ML-3101 discharge via vibrating pebble screen SC-3101, crushes them down to a 12 mm closed side setting (CSS), and discharges onto conveyor CV-3101 via recycle Stream S-305 (484 t/h dry solids) returning to the SAG mill feed to maximize comminution efficiency and throughput. Registered in Mechanical Equipment List RB-4410-PID-00-002 Rev B. Extra-coarse cone crusher operating in closed circuit with SAG discharge screen SC-3101 in Area 31. Driven by a 750 kW electric motor with a design throughput of 484 t/h. Reduces critical-size pebbles to 12 mm CSS before recycling via Stream S-305 to SAG mill feed conveyor CV-3101 per RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2.
* [CV-2101 — Primary Crusher Discharge Conveyor](CV-2101.md) - Primary crusher discharge conveyor and overland transport conveyor transferring primary crushed ore (Stream S-101) from Primary Gyratory Crusher CR-2101 to Coarse Ore Stockpile ST-2101. Operates up to 5,500 t/h peak instantaneous dumping capacity (nominal 2,688 t/h continuous solids rate). Integrated with belt scale weight transmitter WT-2101 and dedicated plant feed reconciliation weightometer WIT-2102. Dust suppression sprays protect transfer points against silica dust dispersion.
* [CV-3101 — SAG Mill Feed Conveyor](CV-3101.md) - SAG mill feed conveyor transferring fresh crushed ore (Stream S-301) from stockpile reclaim feeders FE-3101A/B/C and recycled crushed pebbles from Pebble Crusher CR-3102 directly into the feed chute of SAG Mill ML-3101. Operates at nominal solids rate of 3,172 t/h (2,688 t/h fresh ore solids + 484 t/h pebble recycle solids). Integrated with weight indicating controller WIC-3101 to regulate mill feed rate. Governed by Safety Instrumented Function SIF-3101 (SIL 1) per RB-4410-STD-CE-001 Rev B, which automatically stops CV-3101 on SAG trunnion bearing high-high temperature trip (TAHH 85 °C, 1oo2 voting on TT-3101/TT-3102) simultaneously with ML-3101 GMD to prevent continuous ore surge packing in the SAG mill feed chute.
