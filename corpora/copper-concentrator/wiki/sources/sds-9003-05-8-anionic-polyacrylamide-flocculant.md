# Safety Data Sheet Summary — Anionic Polyacrylamide Flocculant (CAS 9003-05-8)

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> - **REAGENT IDENTITY & PHYSICAL SAFETY HAZARD:** SDS Summary `standards/SDS_9003-05-8_anionic-polyacrylamide-flocculant.pdf` governs **Anionic polyacrylamide flocculant**, CAS No. **9003-05-8**, chemical type **PAM** (Polyacrylamide). The SDS identifies low chemical toxicity but highlights an extreme physical safety hazard: **"spills extremely slippery"**. Powder or solution spills contacting water form a slick, high-lubricity hydrogel on concrete slabs, stairways, access bridges, and catwalks around Concentrate Thickener `TH-5101` and Tailings Thickener `TH-6101`. Immediate containment, washing, or absorbent application is mandatory to prevent slip-and-fall injuries.
> - **PLANT EQUIPMENT ALLOCATION & DOSING SERVICE:** Section 1 explicitly designates chemical usage for thickeners: **"use: TH-5101 / TH-6101"**. This corroborates Process Data Sheet `RB-4410-PS-TH5101 Rev B` (specifying polymer flocculant dosage of 15–25 g/t dry solids for copper concentrate settling), Drawing `RB-4410-PID-51-001 Rev B` Note 1 (referencing flocculant dosing from storage system `TK-4531`), Process Data Sheet `RB-4410-PS-TH6101 Rev B` (specifying flocculant-assisted tailings sedimentation in the 60 m basin), Drawing `RB-4410-PID-61-001 Rev B`, and Operating Manual `RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2` (Sections 3.51, 3.61, 10, and 11).
> - **FIREFIGHTING MEDIA & REAGENT STORAGE CO-LOCATION:** SDS Section 5 mandates extinguishing media: dry chemical, $\text{CO}_2$, or foam. Section 5 carries the critical directive: *"Do not use water jet on burning xanthate"*, reflecting the site arrangement where flocculant preparation/storage package `TK-4531` is co-located in Area 45 (Reagents) adjacent to potassium amyl xanthate storage `TK-4501`. Directing high-pressure water streams at burning xanthate scatters molten solid and drives rapid decomposition into flammable, toxic carbon disulfide ($\text{CS}_2$) vapor.

## Process Engineering & Operational Integration
1. **Concentrate Thickener (`TH-5101`):**
   - High-rate thickener (30 m diameter) receives final copper concentrate pulp (Stream S-431 at 51 t/h solids, 149 m³/h slurry, 28.0 wt% solids).
   - Anionic PAM flocculant is dosed into the feedwell at **15 to 25 g/t dry solids** (per `RB-4410-PS-TH5101 Rev B`).
   - Under-dosing (<15 g/t) leads to slow settling kinetics, cloudy peripheral overflow, and valuable chalcopyrite concentrate loss to the process water circuit.
   - Over-dosing (>25 g/t) causes rapid viscoplastic gel formation, severe rake torque spikes, high pulp yield stress, and blinding of filter cloths on downstream filter press `FP-5101`.
2. **Tailings Thickener (`TH-6101`):**
   - High-rate thickener (60 m diameter) treats 6,314 m³/h of final flotation tailings (Stream S-611 at 2,637 t/h dry solids, 33.0 wt% solids).
   - Anionic PAM accelerates solid-liquid separation to produce 62.0 wt% solids underflow (Stream S-621 at 2,576 m³/h) and recovers 3,739 m³/h clarified water.
   - Flocculant starvation causes immediate bed cloudiness, slime rise, and carryover of unclarified suspended solids into the plant process water network.
3. **Reagent Storage & Make-Up (`TK-4531`):**
   - Supplied from Area 45 Reagent Storage system `TK-4531` per Note 1 of `RB-4410-PID-51-001 Rev B`.
   - Dry polymer bags/bulk bags are dissolved with potable or clarified water to 0.1–0.25 wt% solution strength, aged, and transferred to feedwell dosing loops.
4. **Physical Slip/Fall Safety:**
   - Dry flocculant powder absorbs water rapidly to form a viscous, highly slippery lubricating film. Spills on walkways around thickener launders and rake access bridges must be immediately addressed: dry spills must be swept/vacuumed before washing; wet spills require sand/grit application prior to thorough water hosing.
