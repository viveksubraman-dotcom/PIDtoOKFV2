## Reagent Preparation & Distribution Circuit (Area 4500 / Area 45)
- **Anionic Polyacrylamide Flocculant Dosing Package (TK-4531):** Synthetic organic polymer flocculant preparation and dosing package located in Area 45 (per Note 1 of `RB-4410-PID-51-001 Rev B`), governed by `SDS_9003-05-8_anionic-polyacrylamide-flocculant.pdf` (CAS 9003-05-8, polymer type PAM). Batches and meters dilute anionic polyacrylamide solution to Concentrate Thickener `TH-5101` (15–25 g/t dry solids per `RB-4410-PS-TH5101 Rev B`) and Tailings Thickener `TH-6101` (sedimentation aid for 6,314 m³/h slurry per `RB-4410-PS-TH6101 Rev B`). SDS notes low systemic toxicity but warns that spills are extremely slippery when hydrated; dry sweeping/vacuuming is mandatory before water washdown. Firefighting media: dry chemical, CO2, or foam (prohibiting water jets on burning xanthate).
- **Alternative Frother Evaluation (DF-250 Type):** SDS `SDS_37286-64-9_polyglycol-ether-frother.pdf` covers polyglycol ether frother (DF-250 type, CAS 37286-64-9, polypropylene glycol methyl ether, flash point >100 °C) and explicitly designates its status as *"Alternative frother (not specified for TK-4511)"*. This resolves the documentation discrepancy on P&ID `RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B` which marked TK-4511 as DF-250, while baseline plant design (`RB-4410-PS-TK4511 Rev B`, `RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2`, `SDS_108-11-2_mibc.pdf`) specifies MIBC (flash point 41 °C, Zone 2). Firefighting: dry chemical, CO2, foam; do not use water jet on burning xanthate. Reconciled under HAZOP recommendation ACT-HAZOP-45-06.
- **PAX Xanthate Mixing Tank (TK-4501):** 40 m³ capacity vessel fabricated from 316L stainless steel to resist chemical corrosion from aqueous xanthate solutions per `RB-4410-PS-TK4501 Rev B` [^12] and SDS `SDS_2720-73-2_potassium-amyl-xanthate.pdf`. Mixes solid potassium amyl xanthate pellets (PAX, CAS 2720-73-2, $C_6H_{11}KOS_2$, GHS Self-heating solid) with process water (<40 °C ceiling) to produce a 20% w/v aqueous collector solution. Firefighting requires dry chemical, $\text{CO}_2$, or foam; water jet application is strictly prohibited.
- **Solution Transfer & Dosing Interface:** Transfers prepared 20% w/v PAX collector solution to downstream dosing tank `TK-4502`, which meters PAX to flotation conditioning tank `TK-4101` at design dosage rate 25 g/t dry ore (67.2 kg/h) per `RB-4410-PFD-002 Rev B` [^3], `RB-4410-PS-TK4501 Rev B` [^12], and `SDS_2720-73-2_potassium-amyl-xanthate.pdf`.
- **Life Safety & Environmental Exhaust Ventilation (FN-4501):** The mixing tank is located inside an enclosed room equipped with dedicated mechanical extraction fan `FN-4501` to continuously evacuate toxic and flammable carbon disulfide ($CS_2$, LEL 1.3%, autoignition 90 °C) gas generated from spontaneous xanthate decomposition per `RB-4410-PS-TK4501 Rev B` [^12] and `RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B` Note 1. Ambient $CS_2$ detector `AT-4501` interlocked to `FN-4501` high speed per HAZOP `ACT-HAZOP-45-03`.
- **Frother Storage Tank (TK-4511):** 30 m³ atmospheric storage vessel storing liquid methyl isobutyl carbinol (MIBC) frother in Area 45 per `RB-4410-PS-TK4511 Rev B` [^13]. Doses MIBC frother to rougher flotation cells `FC-4101` and cleaner flotation column `FC-4301` to maintain bubble size stability; classified as Zone 2 (flammable liquid, Class 3; flash point 41 °C).
- **Milk-of-Lime Tank (TK-4521) & Slaker (SL-4521):** 150 m³ storage vessel holding 20% w/w $\text{Ca(OH)}_2$ hydrated lime slurry supplied from upstream packaged lime slaker `SL-4521` per `RB-4410-PS-TK4521 Rev B` [^14] and SDS `SDS_1305-78-8_quicklime---hydrated-lime.pdf`. Quicklime ($\text{CaO}$, CAS 1305-78-8) hydrates exothermically in `SL-4521` to $\text{Ca(OH)}_2$ (CAS 1305-62-0). Classified as GHS Skin corrosion 1B / serious eye damage 1. Doses lime slurry to flotation conditioning tank `TK-4101` under control of analyzer indicating controller `AIC-4101` to maintain rougher feed alkalinity at target pH 10.5 (AL 10.0 / AH 11.2) for pyrite depression.

## Concentrator Operations & Lifecycle Management (RB-4410-OM-001)
Operating Manual `RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2` governs operational targets, unit start-up, shutdown, abnormal situation responses, and safety instrumented functions across the entire facility:

### Grinding Circuit Operations (Areas 31 & 32)
- **SAG Mill Startup Protocol:** Lube and hydrostatic lift system must be verified running with `PT-3104` above **90 bar(g)** (`SIF-3102` start permissive). Bearing temperatures `TT-3101`/`TT-3102` must indicate below `TAH 70 °C` (`TAHH 75 °C` trips mill via `SIF-3101 SIL 1`). Locked charge inspection is mandatory via GMD inching mode (0.1 rpm) if stopped > 4 hours. Mill starts at 70% critical speed, with fresh feed introduced at 50% target and ramped to 2,688 t/h over 15 minutes. Cyclone feed pumps `PP-3201A/B` must run before slurry enters the hopper.
- **Grind-out & Mill Isolation:** Prior to scheduled shutdown, the SAG mill is ground out for 10–15 minutes without ore feed. Electrical lockout occurs at the 33 kV feeder breaker per `RB-4410-SG-005`. Relining takes 72–96 hours; liner handlers are strictly prohibited while on inching drive.
- **Operating Power Windows:** SAG mill power draw target is **18 to 21 MW**; ball mill power draw target is **19 to 21 MW**.

### Beneficiation & Flotation Targets (Areas 41 & 43)
- Rougher flotation feed alkalinity is controlled to **pH 10.5** via `AIC-4101` modulating milk-of-lime from `TK-4521`. Residence time is 32 minutes with superficial air rate at 0.8–1.6 m/s.
- Rougher concentrate (215 t/h solids) is reground in vertical stirred mill `ML-4201` (3,000 kW, ceramic 3–5 mm media) to $P_{80} = 38\ \mu\text{m}$.
- Cleaner flotation column `FC-4301` uses 35 m³/h wash water to yield a commercial grade of **26.0% Cu** (50.6 t/h solids). Overall recoveries are **89% Cu** and **70% Au**.

### Dewatering & Overland Disposal (Areas 51 & 61)
- Concentrate thickener `TH-5101` underflow is maintained at 65 % solids w/w. Vertical plate filter press `FP-5101` targets 8.5% cake moisture below TML (9.8%).
- Tailings thickener `TH-6101` (60 m) delivers 2,576 m³/h underflow at 62 % solids w/w to 3-stage series booster pumps `PP-6101A/B/C` (normal discharge 34 bar(g)). Overpressure trip `SIF-6101` (SIL 2) at `PAHH 38 bar(g)` shuts down all three pumps simultaneously. Post-trip clean water line flushing is mandatory.

## Area 2100 Primary Crushing & Coarse Ore Surge Topology (RB-4410-PID-21-001)
- **Primary Gyratory Crusher (CR-2101):** 60 x 113 gyratory crusher driven by a 1,200 kW electric motor, setting CSS at 165 mm with a 1,525 mm feed opening [^17]. Handles peak truck dumping surges up to 5,500 t/h max [^17]. Crusher lubrication and hydraulic Hydroset system are specified on vendor package drawing `VD-2101` (PID Note 1) [^17].
- **Overland Conveying & Weight Reconciliation (CV-2101):** Crusher product (Stream S-101, 2,688 t/h nominal dry solids) discharges onto overland conveyor `CV-2101` [^2] [^17]. Instrumented with weight transmitter `WT-2101` and certified weightometer `WIT-2102`, which serves as the official plant feed reconciliation meter (PID Note 3) [^17]. Transfer and discharge points are protected by dust suppression sprays (PID Note 2) [^17].
- **Coarse Ore Stockpile (ST-2101):** Features **60,000 tonnes** live capacity, establishing 22.3 hours of operational buffer ahead of SAG milling [^17]. Ore is reclaimed via three reclaim apron feeders `FE-3101A/B/C` inside an underground vault discharging onto SAG feed conveyor `CV-3101` (Stream S-301) [^2] [^17].

## Plant Summary
The Copper Concentrator facility is designed to process **60,000 tpd** of sulfide copper-gold ore at **93.0% operating availability**, yielding a nominal fresh feed rate of **2,688 t/h** to the grinding circuit [^1]. The concentrator produces **50.6 to 51 t/h** of commercial-grade copper concentrate (at 26.0% Cu grade) and rejects **2,637 t/h** of final flotation tailings [^1] [^3] [^4].
