# P-2306A/B — Neutralizing Agent (Diamine) Injection Pumps

## Function

P-2306A and P-2306B inject **diamine** (neutralizing agent) from [[equipment/D-2312]] into the crude product stream ahead of the Direct Neutralization Static Mixers [[equipment/X-2310AB]]. The diamine neutralizes residual sulfuric acid catalyst in the crude product before it enters the Fractionation section. Injection rate is ratio-controlled to the crude product flow.

**Comparison with P-2305A–F (acid injection):** Diamine pumps have ~7× the volumetric capacity (6.65 vs 0.95 liter/hr each) reflecting the stoichiometric mass ratio of diamine to sulfuric acid for neutralization. Motor power identical at 0.2 kW — same pump frame, higher stroke capacity.

**No auto-start (Type B):** Manual restart required if a pump trips. Operator must ensure neutralization is maintained during pump changeover.

## Flow Ratio Control

Diamine injection rate is ratio-controlled to the crude product flow:

```
Crude Product Flow Signal (from FY-1301, signal from TIC-1301 at 23-0013)
    ↓
RATIO/BIAS controller
    ↓
P-2306A/B pump stroke → FI-1903 (Coriolis) → actual flow feedback
```

The ratio controller ensures diamine is always proportional to the acid residue, which in turn is proportional to crude product throughput.

**AT-1901 acid analyzer** (on Drawing 0019) measures residual/excess sulfuric acid in the product stream after neutralization — provides a feedback trim to the ratio setpoint if neutralization is incomplete or excessive.

## Connections

- **Suction:** [[equipment/D-2312]] (Diamine Injection Tank) — 1¼" DA lines
- **Discharge:** Into crude product line before [[equipment/X-2310AB]] (Direct Neutralization Static Mixers)
  - P-2306A → X-2310A side
  - P-2306B → X-2310B side
- **Drain:** To [[equipment/X-2320]] (Acid Neutralization System, Drawing 23-0015)
