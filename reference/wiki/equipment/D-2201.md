## Function

Receives and buffers the combined feed stream to the Oxidation Section — a mixture of recycle Cumene from the ALKY/CDN sections and fresh Cumene make-up. The drum provides surge capacity to decouple upstream supply variations from oxidizer feed flow requirements. The coalescer internal separates entrained free water from the Cumene before it reaches the oxidizers.

**Caustic service**: The caustic washing step upstream (V-2201 Feed Wash Column) means this drum may carry traces of caustic carry-over. Material 304L provides adequate corrosion resistance for this dilute caustic/hydrocarbon service.

## Connections

- Inlet: Combined Cumene feed (recycle + make-up, from ALKY/CDN sections)
- Outlet (N02, 12"): Feed to oxidizer feed pumps → OX-2201/OX-2202 oxidizers
- Outlet (W02, 3"): Secondary outlet
- Vent: Hydrocarbon vapor recovery / flare header

## Safety Notes

> ⚠️ CHP is not present in this drum under normal conditions, but backflow from the oxidizer circuit must be prevented. See [[hazards/cumene-hydroperoxide]].

Caustic water carryover from V-2201 represents a corrosion risk if the caustic concentration is elevated. 304L selected for compatibility.
