# PP-4501A — PAX Dosing Pump

## Function

PAX Dosing Pump PP-4501A is a chemical metering pump in Area 45 drawing 20% w/v aqueous potassium amyl xanthate (PAX) solution from buffer tank TK-4502 and injecting it into Flotation Conditioning Tank TK-4101 and rougher flotation cells. Reagent dosing rates are strictly controlled per Operating Manual RB-4410-OM-001 (nominal 25 g/t dry ore, equivalent to 67.2 kg/h pure PAX). Depicted on P&ID RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- Reagent dosing excursion: Under-dosing of PAX collector reduces chalcopyrite hydrophobicity and causes severe copper recovery loss to tailings; over-dosing floats non-valuable iron sulfides (pyrite) and degrades concentrate grade. Dosing rates must follow RB-4410-OM-001 (Note 4).
- Chemical hazard: PAX solutions decompose to toxic and flammable CS2 gas; pump gland leaks and piping spills must be contained in the bunded Area 45 drainage.
- Chemical PPE requirement: Maintenance on pump wet-end requires full protective chemical suit, goggles, and face shield.
