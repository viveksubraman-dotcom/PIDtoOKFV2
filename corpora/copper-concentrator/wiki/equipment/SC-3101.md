# SC-3101 — SAG Mill Discharge Screen

## Function

Heavy-duty vibrating pebble scalping screen receiving slurry and pebble discharge from the SAG Mill ML-3101 discharge trommel. Performs classification at 12 mm cut size; screen oversize (+12 mm pebbles, Stream S-305, 484 t/h dry solids) is directed to Pebble Crusher CR-3102 for size reduction, and screen undersize (-12 mm slurry) gravity drains into the common cyclone feed pump hopper feeding PP-3201A/B/C.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- Severe mechanical vibration, exciter drive fatigue, and structural cracking under continuous 3,172 t/h slurry and pebble dynamic impact.
- Screen deck blinding or panel pegging by near-size rock causing slurry wash-over into pebble crusher CR-3102 feed cavity, risking crusher cavity packing.
- Pebble crusher bypass chute omission on P&ID RB-4410-PID-31-001 Rev B (Note 4); operational diversion routing during crusher outages must be verified before HAZOP.
- Slurry overflow and splashing hazard leading to abrasive wear of surrounding structure and containment sump loading.
