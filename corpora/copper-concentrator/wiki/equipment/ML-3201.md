# ML-3201 — Ball Mill

## Function

Secondary ball mill operating in closed circuit with hydrocyclone cluster CY-3201, receiving classified coarse underflow (Stream S-323) at 8,064 t/h solids and discharging comminuted slurry to the cyclone feed pump sump. Secondary grinding in closed circuit with cyclone cluster CY-3201, receiving classified coarse underflow (Stream S-323) at 8,064 t/h solids and discharging comminuted slurry to PP-3201 hopper. Secondary ball mill operating in closed circuit with cyclone cluster CY-3201. Receives classified cyclone underflow (Stream S-323 at 8,064 t/h solids, 75.0 wt% solids) and comminutes ore pulp down to target liberation size before discharging by gravity overflow into the common cyclone feed pump sump. Registered in Mechanical Equipment List RB-4410-PID-00-002 Rev B. Secondary ball mill operating in closed circuit with hydrocyclone cluster CY-3201, receiving classified coarse underflow (Stream S-323) at 8,064 t/h solids (75.0 wt% solids) and discharging comminuted slurry by gravity overflow into the cyclone feed pump hopper (PP-3201A/B/C). Configured as an 8.2 m x 13.4 m (27 ft x 44 ft) overflow mill driven by a 22,000 kW Gearless Motor Drive (GMD) ring motor per RB-4410-PID-32-001 Rev B and RB-4410-PS-ML3201 Rev B. Secondary ball mill operating in closed circuit with hydrocyclone cluster CY-3201. Sized at 8.2 m diameter by 13.4 m EGL (27 ft x 44 ft) with 22,000 kW gearless motor drive (GMD, fixed speed). Treats classified cyclone underflow (Stream S-323) targeting a power draw of 19 to 21 MW per RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2 to deliver P80 150 µm product to rougher flotation.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- High pulp density operation at 75.0% solids w/w; risk of mill viscoplastic rheology dampening grinding efficiency and causing feed chute backing up or overflowing.
- Circulating load surge exceeding 300% causing sump level high-high (LHH) and slurry flooding on the grinding floor.
- Trunnion bearing overheating or oil starvation; dual interlocked lubricating oil pumps required.
- Trunnion bearing overheating or oil starvation; dual interlocked lubricating oil pumps required. Combined hydrostatic lift / hydrodynamic lube unit operates with ISO VG 460 oil with normal lift pressure of 110 bar(g) against 170 bar(g) design pressure; bearing temperatures monitored by TT-3201 and TT-3202 to prevent Babbitt liquefaction.
- Maximum charge weight at maximum volumetric filling reaches 1,980 tonnes (32-35% vol ball charge plus slurry/ore); structural overload risk on ASTM A516 Gr 70 (80 mm shell) and cast steel head/trunnions if overfilled.
- Confined space and mechanical hazards during liner maintenance; 22,000 kW GMD inching mode operates at 0.1 rpm and requires positive 33 kV cycloconverter breaker LOTO and mechanical brake locking before entry with 2.5 t mill relining machine.
- Trunnion bearing overheating and white metal wipe under 1,980 t charge load; RTD temperature monitoring required.
- Shell structural fatigue and slurry cushioning if pulp density exceeds 75 wt%.
- Discharge overflow to cyclone feed pump hopper must maintain continuous unhindered gravity drainage; high slurry density (>75% solids) or tramp oversized ore chunks can cause discharge grate blinding, slurry pooling in the mill belly, and overflow flume spillage onto the grinding floor.
- BALL MILL OVERPOWER / OVERLOAD: Maintain power within 19 to 21 MW target window; excessive pulp density or media over-charging causes motor overload or viscoplastic liner damage per RB-4410-OM-001 Sec 4.
