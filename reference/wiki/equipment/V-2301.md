# V-2301 — Preflash Column

> ⚠️ CHP is present in the column bottoms at elevated concentration. Column operates under deep vacuum — air ingress risk. See [[hazards/cumene-hydroperoxide]].

## Function

First-stage vacuum evaporator in the CDN Concentration sub-section. Removes the majority of Cumene from the oxidate feed by evaporation under vacuum, partially concentrating the CHP. Overhead Cumene vapor is condensed and becomes part of the recycle Cumene stream back to the Oxidation Section. Bottoms flow to Flash Column V-2302 for further concentration.

**Feed type note:** Feed enters V-2301 as a two-phase (vapor + liquid) mixture. This is because the oxidate is preheated to ~83°C in E-2302A/B and E-2303, causing partial flash before or at the column inlet. ## Control Philosophy

- **Normal feed control:** FIC-0401 → FV-0401 maintains feed rate. This is the DCS control loop.
- **SIS protection (feed):** UXV-0401 is the UC-2301 SIS valve. It operates independently of FV-0401. On ESD, UXV-0401 closes regardless of FV-0401 position.
- **Level to V-2302:** LIC-0402 → LV-0801/0802 maintains column sump level by controlling bottoms flow to V-2302 Flash Column.
- **Startup bypass:** HXS-0102 on UC-2301 bypasses FXSLL-0401A/B/C and LXSHH-0802 (Flash Column level) for a timed interval during startup to allow filling.
- **Cumene quench:** Emergency connection from D-2301 provides cumene quench to column sump if CHP concentration exceeds safe limits. UXV-0601 controls this quench.
