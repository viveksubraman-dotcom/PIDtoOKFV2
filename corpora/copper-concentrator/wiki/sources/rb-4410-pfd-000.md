# HAZOP Study - Concentrator (Selected Nodes) (RB-4410-STD-HAZOP-001 Rev 1)

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> - **MANDATORY HAZOP RECOMMENDATIONS:** Standard `RB-4410-STD-HAZOP-001 Rev 1` establishes that all HAZOP recommendations are **mandatory unless formally closed** through documented engineering management of change (MOC) and hazard closure sign-off.
> - **NODE HZ-31-02 (PSV-3105 SET PRESSURE CONFIRMATION):** HP lift pump dead-heading against a closed valve presents a severe overpressure oil line rupture and fire risk at the SAG mill. HAZOP mandates: *"Confirm PSV-3105 set pressure on P&ID matches data sheet"*. Data sheet `RB-4410-PS-0032 Rev B` specifies **160 bar(g)** (corroborated by `RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2` Section 12), whereas P&ID `RB-4410-PID-31-002 Rev B` erroneously specifies **180 bar(g)**, exceeding the system design pressure of **170 bar(g)**.
> - **NODE HZ-31-04 (SAG TRUNNION BEARING TRIP TAHH ALIGNMENT):** Loss of lube oil flow or cooling risks a trunnion bearing wipe and a 10–14 day unplanned concentrator outage. Existing safeguard is `TT-3101/3102` TAHH trip via `SIF-3101`. HAZOP mandates: *"Align TAHH setpoint between instrument data sheet and cause & effect"*. Data sheet `RB-4410-PS-0034 Rev B` specifies **TAHH 75 °C**, whereas Cause & Effect Matrix `RB-4410-STD-CE-001 Rev B` specifies **TAHH 85 °C** (1oo2 voting).
> - **NODE HZ-45-03 (PAX MIXING ROOM CS2 GAS DETECTOR AT-4501):** Wet or hot PAX (>40 °C) or acid contamination induces rapid xanthate decomposition into carbon disulfide ($CS_2$), posing flash fire and toxic exposure hazards. Existing safeguard is extraction fan `FN-4501`. HAZOP mandates: *"Install CS2 gas detector AT-4501 (alarm 10 % LEL) interlocked to FN-4501 high speed and PAX feeder stop"*.
> - **NODE HZ-45-06 (FROTHER REAGENT IDENTITY & AREA CLASSIFICATION):** Reagent specification change invalidates hazardous-area classification. Existing safeguard is Zone 2 classification for MIBC. HAZOP mandates: *"Confirm frother identity on P&ID vs data sheet and SDS"*. P&ID `RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B` specifies Polyglycol ether frother (DF-250 type), whereas Data Sheet `RB-4410-PS-TK4511 Rev B` and Operating Manual `RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2` specify MIBC (flash point 41 °C, Zone 2).
> - **NODE HZ-61-01 (TAILINGS CASING DESIGN PRESSURE CONFIRMATION):** Blocked tailings line or valve closure at TSF causes overpressure, pipeline rupture, and environmental release. Existing safeguard is `PT-6103` PAHH trip via `SIF-6101` (SIL 2). HAZOP mandates: *"Confirm stage-3 casing design pressure on P&ID"*. P&ID `RB-4410-PID-61-001 Rev B` erroneously marks **4.0 bar(g)** (typographical decimal error against 34 bar(g) normal operating discharge), whereas Process Data Sheet `RB-4410-PS-PP6101 Rev B` specifies **40 bar(g)**.
> - **IEC 61511 CONTROLLING AUTHORITY:** Standard `RB-4410-STD-CE-001 Rev B` is the governing plant authority for all Safety Instrumented Systems (SIS) and Safety Instrumented Functions (SIFs) under IEC 61511. All safety trip set points, voting logic, and executive final element actions defined herein take functional safety precedence.
> - **⚠️ CONFLICT — SAG TRUNNION BEARING TEMPERATURE TRIP SETPOINT (SIF-3101):** The controlling SIS Cause & Effect Matrix `RB-4410-STD-CE-001 Rev B` explicitly specifies the executive trip set point for SIF-3101 as **TAHH 85 °C** with **1oo2 voting** on duplex temperature transmitters `TT-3101` and `TT-3102`, tripping both the SAG mill Gearless Motor Drive (`ML-3101 GMD`) and the SAG feed conveyor (`CV-3101`). In contrast, Temperature Instrument Process Data Sheet `RB-4410-PS-0034 Rev B` and Concentrator Operating Manual `RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2` (Section 12) specify the trip set point as **TAHH 75 °C** (pre-alarm `TAH 70 °C`). An 85 °C setting provides a 10 °C higher operating threshold before tripping, but approaches the thermal softening point of white metal Babbitt bearing linings under the 2,450 t mill charge load. This discrepancy must be formally evaluated in the upcoming HAZOP / LOPA review and reconciled with mechanical bearing vendor limits.
> - **EXECUTIVE INTERLOCK ACTION EXPANSION (CV-3101 TRIP):** SIF-3101 mandates that an emergency bearing temperature trip does not merely trip `ML-3101 GMD`, but simultaneously stops SAG mill feed conveyor `CV-3101` to prevent un-ground ore accumulation in the feed chute.

## Detailed Functional Safety Philosophy & Architecture
### SIF-3101: SAG Mill Trunnion Bearing Thermal Protection (SIL 1)
- **Initiating Initiators:** Duplex Pt100 RTD temperature transmitters `TT-3101` (drive-end trunnion bearing housing) and `TT-3102` (non-drive-end trunnion bearing housing).
- **Measurement Span:** 0 to 150 °C.
- **Trip Set Point:** `TAHH 85 °C` (⚠️ CONFLICT: listed as `TAHH 75 °C` in `RB-4410-PS-0034 Rev B` and `RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2`).
- **Voting Logic:** **1oo2** (one-out-of-two) voting architecture. Either sensor detecting bearing temperature exceeding 85 °C will trigger the executive shutdown logic.
- **Final Control Elements & Action:**
  1. Main drive trip: Executive trip of the 22,000 kW Gearless Motor Drive (`ML-3101 GMD` ring motor) cycloconverter feeder breaker.
  2. Feed isolation trip: Immediate interlock stop of SAG feed conveyor `CV-3101`, preventing ore surge piling in the feed chute.
- **SIL Rating:** SIL 1 per IEC 61511.
- **Process Safety Intent:** Protects white metal Babbitt bearing pads from wiping, thermal softening, and journal seizure under the 2,450 t dynamic grinding charge load.

### SIF-3102: SAG Mill Hydrostatic Lift Oil Pressure Protection (SIL 1)
- **Initiating Initiator:** Pressure transmitter `PT-3104` installed on the high-pressure hydrostatic lift oil common header feeding the 2 x 2 trunnion bearing shoes.
- **Measurement Span:** 0 to 250 bar(g).
- **Operating Lift Pressure:** 120 bar(g) normal operating jacking pressure (pre-alarm low `PAL 90 bar(g)`).
- **Trip Set Point:** `PALL 70 bar(g)`.
- **Voting Logic:** **1oo1** (one-out-of-one) voting architecture.
- **Final Control Elements & Action:**
  1. Start Inhibit: Software interlock prohibiting start of `ML-3101 GMD` unless hydrostatic lift oil pressure is confirmed above the permissive threshold.
  2. Emergency Stop: Immediate running trip of `ML-3101 GMD` if lift oil pressure collapses to or below 70 bar(g).
- **SIL Rating:** SIL 1 per IEC 61511.
- **Process Safety Intent:** Guarantees hydrodynamic/hydrostatic fluid film separation between the mill trunnion journals and Babbitt pads to prevent catastrophic dry metal-to-metal rubbing and journal scoring.

### SIF-6101: Overland Tailings Pipeline & Pump Casing Overpressure Protection (SIL 2)
- **Initiating Initiator:** Stage-3 discharge pressure transmitter `PT-6103` on common slurry discharge line Stream S-621.
- **Measurement Span:** 0 to 60 bar(g).
- **Normal Operating Pressure:** 34 bar(g) (pre-trip warning alarm `PAH 36 bar(g)`).
- **Trip Set Point:** `PAHH 38 bar(g)`.
- **Voting Logic:** **2oo3** (two-out-of-three) voting architecture across redundant pressure transmitters.
- **Final Control Elements & Action:** Simultaneous executive shutdown and motor circuit breaker trip of all three series booster pumps:
  - Lead Stage Pump `PP-6101A` (2,000 kW)
  - Intermediate Stage Pump `PP-6101B` (2,000 kW)
  - Final Stage Pump `PP-6101C` (2,000 kW)
- **SIL Rating:** SIL 2 per IEC 61511.
- **Process Safety Intent:** Protects the 40 bar(g) mechanical casing rating of stage-3 pump `PP-6101C` (maintaining a 2.0 bar positive safety buffer) and prevents overpressure rupture of the 6.2 km overland slurry pipeline corridor engineered to `RB-4410-STD-TSF-002`.

   - Standard `RB-4410-STD-CE-001 Rev B`: specifies `TAHH 85 °C`, 1oo2 voting, tripping `ML-3101` and `CV-3101`.
   - Process Data Sheet `RB-4410-PS-0034 Rev B`: specifies `TAHH 75 °C`, `TAH 70 °C`.
   - Operating Manual `RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2` (Sec 12): specifies `TAHH 75 °C`, `TAH 70 °C`.

   - Data sheet `RB-4410-PS-PP6101 Rev B` establishes 40 bar(g) casing rating, which is fully compatible with SIF-6101 tripping at 38 bar(g).
