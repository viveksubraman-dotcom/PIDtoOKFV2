> ⚠️ CHP-containing overhead vapors on shell side. Refrigerated cooling required — loss of cooling capacity degrades both vacuum columns simultaneously. See [[hazards/cumene-hydroperoxide]].

*Shell (HOT) side = cumene overhead vapors from V-2301 and V-2302. Bundle (COLD) side = Refrigerated Cooling System (RCS) from utility unit 61. **Cooling medium confirmed: 80 wt% Water + 20 wt% Ethylene Glycol (C₂H₆O₂).** [Source: operator, 2026-06-13]. PP(70) insulation on shell prevents heat gain from atmosphere — operating temperature (~28–52°C under deep vacuum) is below tropical ambient (35-40°C in Map Ta Phut). At site ambient conditions, standard cooling water cannot condense these vapors — refrigerated glycol/water solution is required.*

*E-2301 is the LARGEST heat duty exchanger in the Concentration sub-section (16.52 MM kcal/h — more than the sum of E-2303 (6.10) + E-2304 (4.15) + E-2307 (18.59 MM kcal/h is in Decomposition). Among concentration equipment, it is the largest single duty item.*

## Process Role

E-2301 is the SHARED condenser for both vacuum columns:
- Receives 72" overhead vapor from **V-2301 Preflash Column** (two 72" vapor inlets)
- Receives 56" overhead vapor from **V-2302 Flash Column** (one 56" vapor inlet)

Condensed cumene liquid accumulates in the condenser shell sump before being pumped by **Preflash and Flash Columns Overhead Pumps (P-2307A/B, P&ID 0008B)**. The net condensate becomes **Recycle Cumene Stream 350** (~140,798 kg/h) returned to the Oxidation Section.

Non-condensed vapors exit as overhead to the **Flash Column Overhead Vapor Chiller (E-2310, 23-0010)** for further condensation.

## Key Engineering Notes

### 1. Refrigerated Cooling — Critical Utility Dependency
E-2301 is the only condenser for BOTH vacuum columns. Loss of refrigerated cooling (RCS from Unit 61) causes:
- Loss of condensation → rising column pressure → both V-2301 and V-2302 pressure increase toward atmosphere
- The deep vacuum driving force for Cumene evaporation disappears
- CHP concentrations cannot be built — both columns effectively stop functioning
- UC-2301 will trip on PXSHH-0801 (V-2302 vapor space pressure HH, SIL 2) before conditions become dangerous

In tropical ambient (Map Ta Phut, ~35-40°C), cooling tower water (~32°C) cannot condense vapors at 28-38°C. Refrigerated water (typically 5-15°C) is the only viable cooling medium. Monitor RCS supply temperature and flow as leading indicators of condenser performance.

### 2. Shared Cooling Circuit with Decomposer Cooler (E-2307)
The RCS return line (28"-RCR-23-008018-MS11-NI) routes **TO DECOMPOSER COOLER** (P&ID 0017, E-2307). This indicates the refrigerated cooling water from E-2301 connects to the same cooling circuit as E-2307 — creating an **inter-section utility dependency between Concentration and Decomposition sub-sections**. A fault in the shared RCS circuit can affect both the column condensation and the decomposer cooling simultaneously.

> ⚠️ Process safety implication: If the shared RCS cooling circuit degrades, both E-2301 (column pressure rise → UC-2301 trip) and E-2307 (decomposer cooling loss → UC-2302 PDXSHH-1701 trip) will be affected. This cascading failure mode should be captured in any HAZOP review.

### 3. Piping Symmetry Requirement
The overhead vapor piping to E-2301 must be **SYMMETRICAL** (per drawing note). Asymmetric vapor distribution between V-2301 and V-2302 inlets would cause unequal pressure drop and could unbalance the vacuum profile in the two columns. The expansion joints (EJN-E2301A/B/C) accommodate thermal expansion in the large-diameter (72", 56") lines.

### 4. Pipe Length Constraint — E-2301 to E-2310
Drawing notes: **"125 METERS MAXIMUM EQUIVALENT LENGTH OF PIPE FROM PREFLASH AND FLASH COLUMNS CONDENSER TO FLASH COLUMN OVERHEAD VAPOR CHILLER (E-2310)"**. Excessive pipe length between E-2301 and E-2310 would create pressure drop in the non-condensed overhead vapor line, raising the effective operating pressure in V-2302, which would suppress Cumene evaporation. This is a critical plant layout constraint — E-2310 must be located within 125m equivalent pipe length of E-2301.

### 5. UXV-0803 DCS-Only Reset
Most SIS valves in CDN have field reset pushbuttons. UXV-0803 (on the condenser overhead line) is an exception — reset is DCS software only. This means that during restart after a UC-2302 trip, the operator **must** be at the DCS workstation to reset this valve — local field reset is not available.

## Maintenance Notes

- **28"×24" Removable Spool**: Provided for bundle removal during maintenance. Large condenser — bundle pull requires significant mechanical access space.
- **Expansion joints (EJN-E2301A/B/C)**: Inspect for fatigue — the large vapor lines cycle between vacuum and atmospheric conditions at every startup/shutdown, creating significant mechanical loads.
- Firewater inlet connection with hose connection capability (per drawing note: blankoff when not in use).
