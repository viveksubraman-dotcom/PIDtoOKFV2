*Shell side = hot recirculating oxidate from Oxidation Section; Tube side = cold fresh oxidate feed. This is a feed-effluent heat exchanger for energy integration between OXI and CDN sections.*

## Process Role

E-2302A/B are heat recovery exchangers that preheat the cold fresh oxidate feed using hot recirculating oxidate from the Oxidation Section. The recirculating oxidate (from Oxidizer No.2 pumps at OXI section) flows through the shell side at elevated temperature; the fresh oxidate feed passes through the tube side.

This arrangement recovers heat from the OXI recirculation loop, reducing the steam requirement in the downstream Preflash Column Steam Heater (E-2303). The two units operate in parallel at 52.5% each, so one unit alone can sustain approximately full plant throughput — allowing maintenance without shutting the plant.

**TXSHH-0501** is a SIL 1 SIS initiator. If tube side (oxidate) outlet temperature exceeds the high-high setpoint, UC-2301 shuts the Concentration section. This protects against overheating of the CHP-containing feed stream before it enters V-2301. See [[instruments/sis-cdn]] for full UC-2301 cause list.

## Maintenance Notes

- Removable spools provided on both A and B inlet piping (per drawing note: "PIPING MUST BE SYMMETRICAL").
- Each unit can be isolated individually for cleaning/inspection while the other remains in service.
- Shell side carries OXI section recirculating oxidate (also CHP-containing) — full CHP precautions apply on both sides.
- Fouling on tube side (fresh oxidate deposits) will reduce preheating efficiency and shift more duty to E-2303.
