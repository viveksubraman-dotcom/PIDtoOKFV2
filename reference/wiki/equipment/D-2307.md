# D-2307 — Acid Aromatics Sump

## Function

D-2307 is the central collection vessel for all acid aromatics liquid streams from the CDN section. It receives liquid knockout from [[equipment/D-2306]] (Acid Aromatics KO Drum) and all drain connections from the Acid Aromatics Closed Drain Header (Drawing 0021). The contents — a mixture of phenol, acid aromatics, and process residues — are pumped by [[equipment/P-2304A]] to the Sodium Phenate Tank at the Phenol Recovery Section.

**Safety — Note 6 (Drawing 0020A):** "NO AIR ALLOWED IN THE CLOSED DRAIN SYSTEM FROM PUMPS, INSTRUMENTS, PIPELINES, EXCHANGERS AND THE RELATED EQUIPMENTS." D-2307 is kept under N₂ blanket at all times. Air ingress would create an explosive atmosphere with phenol/aromatic vapors.

> ⛔ **PSV TAG/SET-PRESSURE CORRECTION (2026-06-16).** Wiki previously recorded a "dual PSV arrangement": PSV-2001N at 0.84 kg/cm²g and PSV-2001B at 3.5 kg/cm²g. The AS-BUILT Pressure Relief Valve Process Data Sheet (PS-0018) shows only **one PSV pair — PSV-23-2001A/B — both set at 3.5 kg/cm²g** (external fire case, 6,687 kg/h capacity, API orifice L, spring-loaded balanced bellows, discharging to the **Relief Header**, not atmosphere as implied previously). The "N" suffix and the implied second low-set PSV both appear to be a data-entry artifact from the earlier P&ID-based ingest (likely conflating PCV-2001 with a PSV, and misreading "A" as "N"). **Corrected entry adopted above.** See [[instruments/pressure-relief-valves-cdn]].
