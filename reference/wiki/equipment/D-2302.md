## Process Role — Line Flush System

**D-2302 is NOT in the main CHP flow path.** It is a dedicated cumene supply drum for *line flushing procedures*. This is the critical distinction:

| Path | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Normal CHP path** | P-2301A/B (P&ID 0009) → 6"-CHP line → UXV-1201A/B/C → Decomposer (P&ID 0012, 0013) |
| **D-2302 cumene path** | Fresh Feed Pumps (P&ID 0006) → **D-2302** → 4"-CUL to P&ID 0012 (CHP line flush) |
| **D-2302 to D-2303** | D-2302 → 1¾"-CUL to P&ID 0012A → **D-2303 Decomposer Feed Flush Drum** |

**D-2302 functions:**
1. **Pre-Decomposer startup flush**: Cumene from D-2302 is used to fill/flush the CHP lines before CHP is admitted to the Decomposer, removing any residual water or air
2. **Post-shutdown flush**: After Decomposer ESD or planned shutdown, cumene purges CHP from the feed lines to a safe concentration
3. **Feed Flush Drum (D-2303) supply**: Provides cumene to D-2303 for direct flushing at the Decomposer feed nozzle
4. **Buffer reservoir**: Ensures sufficient cumene volume is available on-demand for emergency line clearing

**Split-range pressure control (PY-1101A/B):** D-2302 is maintained under CHP N2 blanket. PIC-1101 controls vessel pressure by:
- Low demand: PY-1101A opens (vents N2 excess or admits less N2)
- High demand: PY-1101B opens (admits more N2 to maintain blanket pressure)

This ensures continuous nitrogen blanket coverage regardless of vessel breathing (level changes, temperature swings). The split-range design is standard for inert-blanketed vessels where pressure must stay positive at all times to exclude air.

## Nitrogen Blanket (Safety Critical)

Per STD DWG 8-138 Type 1 and STD DWGs 8-121 and 8-141:
- All N2 connections to D-2302 use **CHP Nitrogen Header** (not regular nitrogen)
- CHP Closed Drain Header connections required for drains
- Vent routed to Oxidation Section decanter — not to atmosphere

Despite D-2302 containing cumene (not CHP) during normal operation, CHP nitrogen is used because:
1. D-2302 is in the CHP service zone (upstream of UXV-1205 boundary)
2. The flush lines from D-2302 connect directly to CHP piping
3. Residual CHP contamination in the flush lines cannot be excluded
4. UOP requirement: any vessel connected to CHP piping uses CHP N2 for blanketing

## No Direct SIS Connection

D-2302 has no UC-2301 or UC-2302 direct connections. The SIS isolation valves for CHP are downstream on P&ID 0012 (UXV-1201A/B/C and UXV-1205 — UC-2302 outputs). D-2302 level control operates independently through DCS (LIC-1101/LV-1101).

This makes sense — D-2302 contains cumene, not CHP. Loss of D-2302 does not create an immediate safety hazard for the Decomposer. Loss of the flush supply does prevent a controlled restart but is not a trip cause.
