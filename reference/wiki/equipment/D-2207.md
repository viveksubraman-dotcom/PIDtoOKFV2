## Function

Scrubs compressed air (from the plant air compressor) with dilute caustic solution before delivery to the Oxidation Section oxidizers (OX-2201/OX-2202). The scrubbing removes trace organic contaminants from the air that could interfere with the cumene oxidation reaction or foul oxidizer internals. The column operates at 1.5 kg/cm²g — significantly higher than the oxidizers — to provide pressure driving force for air distribution through the oxidizer spargers.

**Process flows (tray data):**
- Vapor (air): 61,539 kg/h at 2.8 kg/m³
- Liquid (0.1% NaOH solution): 28,208 kg/h at 992 kg/m³ (viscosity 0.63 cP, surface tension 69.2 dynes/cm)
- Foam derating factor: 1.0

KCS material is appropriate — the 0.1% caustic concentration is low and the operating temperature (44°C) is well within KCS limits for dilute aqueous NaOH.
