## Process Role

E-2303 provides the final temperature boost to the oxidate feed from ~82°C (after E-2302A/B) to the ~83°C target feed temperature for V-2301 (Preflash Column). Steam heating supplements the energy recovered in E-2302A/B.

The steam flow rate is controlled by **FIC-0501** using a **feed-forward control strategy** from Oxidation Section signals:
- **Signal from FLOW INDICATOR at Oxidation Section** — flow feed-forward
- **Signal from TEMPERATURE INDICATOR at Oxidation Section** — temperature feed-forward

This feed-forward scheme adjusts steam duty in advance of temperature changes arriving at V-2301, reducing column upsets. The base setpoint comes from the required heat input to maintain V-2301 feed temperature.

Steam condensate leaves via 1½"-S1.5-23-005004-MS11-H to the Preflash Column Steam Heater Condensate Drum [[equipment/D-2308]].

## SIS Interlock Valves

| Tag | Type | SIS Controller | Location | Action on ESD |
|-----|------|---------------|---------|---------------|
| UXV-0501 | SIS isolation valve | UC-2301 | Steam supply inlet | CLOSE — primary steam cutoff |
| UXV-0502 | SIS isolation valve | UC-2301 | Steam supply inlet | CLOSE — redundant steam cutoff |

Two steam isolation valves (UXV-0501 and UXV-0502) in series on the steam supply. Both close on any UC-2301 ESD. Redundant closure ensures steam flow is positively isolated even if one valve fails to close.

## Process Engineering Notes

1. **Feed-forward control philosophy**: The steam flow controller uses feed-forward signals from the OXI section. When OXI section oxidate flow increases or feed temperature decreases, E-2303 steam duty increases proportionally without waiting for the V-2301 feed temperature to deviate. This minimizes disturbances to V-2301 vacuum operation.

2. **Thermal runaway risk**: SC1.5 steam temperature (nominally 120–130°C) is well above CHP decomposition onset temperature (~90°C). The two redundant UXV valves and the 1oo2 TXSHH-0502 SIS ensure that any steam supply interruption failure results in safe shutdown rather than overheating.

3. **Condensate management**: Condensate in D-2308 must be maintained at proper level. Loss of P-2308A/B (condensate pumps) could eventually flood E-2303 condensate side, reducing heat transfer area and causing erratic temperature control. Level alarms on D-2308 (LAH-0502/LAL-0502) protect against this.
