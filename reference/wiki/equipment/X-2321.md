# X-2321 — Acid Aromatic Sump Pit

## Function

X-2321 is an underground concrete-lined sump pit that collects all leaks, spills, and floor drains from the Acid Aromatics area of the CDN unit. Contents are pumped out by the submerged sump pit pump [[equipment/P-2320]] to [[equipment/D-2307]] (Acid Aromatics Sump vessel) or to the oily water sewer (91-0058) depending on the contamination level.
