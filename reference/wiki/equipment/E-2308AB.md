# E-2308A/B — Dehydrators

> ⚠️ CHP is a peroxide — thermal decomposition risk. See [[hazards/cumene-hydroperoxide]].

**Technology:** UOP CDN Process | **Plant:** PTT Phenol Train II (PPCL)

## Function

E-2308A/B are shell-and-tube heat exchangers operated as 1 operating + 1 standby. They serve three simultaneous process objectives on the crude decomposition product:

1. **DMBA dehydration**: Dimethylbenzylcarbinol (DMBA) → Alpha-Methylstyrene (AMS) + H₂O at ~135°C, heated by SC3 steam.
2. **Residual CHP decomposition**: Any CHP surviving the Decomposer loop is destroyed before leaving the decomposition sub-section.
3. **Water removal overhead**: Water produced by the dehydration reaction exits via overhead vapor to the Acid Aromatics Knockout Drum 23-0022.

**Location**: On the **Decomposer Platform** (noted on P&ID 0014). Physically co-located with D-2304 and E-2307.
