# TK-4101 — Flotation Conditioning Tank

## Function

Agitated conditioning tank receiving secondary hydrocyclone overflow slurry (Stream S-324) from CY-3201 at 2,688 t/h dry solids and 5,969 m3/h slurry at 35.0 wt% solids. Provides slurry retention for pH adjustment with 20% w/w Ca(OH)2 milk-of-lime slurry from TK-4521 to pH 10.5 governed by analytical indicating controller AIC-4101 (set point 10.5 per Note 1 of RB-4410-PID-41-001 Rev B; alarms AL 10.0 / AH 11.2), collector addition (PAX at 25 g/t ore), and frother addition (MIBC at 20 g/t ore) from Area 45 per Note 2 of RB-4410-PID-41-001 Rev B to ensure selective mineral surface hydrophobicity prior to rougher flotation cells FC-4101.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- Slurry sanding and solids settling in conditioning tank bottom upon agitator failure or loss of drive power; restart requires high-pressure water sparging.
- PAX xanthate decomposition hazards: spontaneous decomposition in acidic or heated conditions releases flammable and highly toxic carbon disulfide (CS2) vapor.
- Caustic contact hazard from milk-of-lime dosing; severe skin and eye irritation requiring personal protective equipment (PPE).
- MIBC vapors are combustible and irritate respiratory tract; tank headspace must maintain adequate natural or mechanical ventilation.
- Alkalinity excursions: Uncontrolled pH drop below AL 10.0 promotes toxic CS2 gas generation from PAX degradation and allows non-selective pyrite activation.
- Caustic contact hazard from milk-of-lime dosing from TK-4521; severe skin and eye irritation requiring personal protective equipment (PPE).
