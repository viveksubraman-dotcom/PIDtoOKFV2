# CY-3201 — CY-3201 — Secondary Grinding Hydrocyclone Cluster

## Function

Closed-circuit classification hydrocyclone cluster separating combined SAG and ball mill slurry into coarse underflow (Stream S-323) recycled to ball mill ML-3201 and fine overflow (Stream S-324) sent by gravity to flotation conditioning tank TK-4101 and rougher flotation cells FC-4101. Configured as 16 x 660 mm hydrocyclones (13 operating, 3 standby). Operating inlet manifold pressure is maintained at 100–130 kPa (monitored and alarmed by PT-3201, PAL 90 kPa / PAH 140 kPa), while inlet slurry density is monitored on the vertical feed header by nuclear density transmitter DT-3201 (design 60.0% solids w/w, SG 1.618). Connected upstream to feed pump PP-3201A/B, and downstream to ball mill ML-3201 and flotation conditioning tank TK-4101. Secondary grinding hydrocyclone cluster comprising 16 x 660 mm cyclones (13 operating, 3 standby). Classifies secondary grinding slurry into coarse underflow (Stream S-323) recycled to ball mill ML-3201 and fine overflow (Stream S-324, P80 = 150 µm) advancing to flotation. Registered in Mechanical Equipment List RB-4410-PID-00-002 Rev B. Secondary grinding hydrocyclone cluster comprising 16 x 660 mm cyclones (13 operating, 3 standby per RB-4410-PS-CY3201 Rev B and RB-4410-PID-32-001 Rev B). Classifies secondary grinding slurry into coarse underflow (Stream S-323) recycled to ball mill ML-3201 and fine overflow (Stream S-324, P80 = 150 µm) advancing to rougher flotation conditioning tank TK-4101. Operating inlet manifold pressure is monitored by PT-3201 (normal window 100–130 kPa, PAL 90 kPa / PAH 140 kPa). Feed density is monitored on the vertical feed header by nuclear gauge DT-3201 (design 60.0% solids w/w, SG 1.618; radiation permit applies per PID Note 3). Secondary grinding hydrocyclone cluster comprising 16 x 660 mm cyclones (13 operating, 3 standby). Separates secondary grinding slurry into coarse underflow (Stream S-323) recycled to ball mill ML-3201 and fine overflow (Stream S-324, P80 150 µm) advancing to rougher flotation. Operating targets in RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2 specify feed pressure of 100-130 kPa, circulating load of 250-300%, and feed density of 65% solids w/w (flagged as a conflict vs 60.0% solids in engineering datasheets). Section 4 warns that operating above design feed density coarsens the overflow and reduces rougher recovery, requiring feed rate reduction before exceeding target.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- Cyclone roping hazard when underflow density exceeds critical rheological limit (~78% solids), discharging coarse oversize directly into flotation feed (Stream S-324) and blinding flotation froth.
- Inlet pressure instability caused by feed pump surging or sump level swings, compromising classification d50 cut point.
- Severe abrasive slurry wear on ceramic/polyurethane apex inserts and vortex finders leading to undetected classification deterioration.
- Inlet pressure instability caused by feed pump surging or sump level swings, compromising classification d50 cut point; monitored by PT-3201 (PAL 90 kPa / PAH 140 kPa).
- Feed pressure excursion: Low pressure (<90 kPa) impairs centrifugal vortex formation and separation efficiency; high pressure (>140 kPa) indicates apex choking or roping conditions.
- Slurry density excursions monitored by nuclear gauge DT-3201 (range 1.0–2.0 SG; normal operating density 1.618 SG at 60.0 wt% solids) to prevent slurry roping and apex choking.
- Cyclone feed pressure operating envelope: Design feed pressure is 100–130 kPa per RB-4410-PS-CY3201 Rev B, bounded by DCS alarms PAL 90 kPa and PAH 140 kPa (PT-3201 per RB-4410-PS-0032 Rev B). Operation below 90 kPa impairs centrifugal vortex formation; operation above 140 kPa indicates apex restriction, roping onset, or excessive feed flow.
- Standby cyclone management: Cluster consists of 16 x 660 mm cyclones with 13 operating and 3 standby per RB-4410-PS-CY3201 Rev B. Operating with an incorrect number of active cyclones alters manifold pressure and separation cut point P80 from the design target of 150 µm.
- Cyclone roping if feed solids exceed 60.0 wt% (SG > 1.618).
- Coarse oversize bypass into flotation feed if apex orifice chokes.
- Radiation exposure hazard from nuclear density gauge DT-3201 installed on the cyclone feed vertical riser; radiation safety permitting, source housing shutter lockouts, periodic wipe/leak testing, and controlled access exclusion zones are mandatory during maintenance per Drawing Note 3.
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — CYCLONE FEED DENSITY: Operating Manual RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2 specifies Cyclone feed density (design) as 65 % solids w/w, whereas Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-CY3201 Rev B, PFD RB-4410-PFD-001 Rev B, and P&ID RB-4410-PID-32-001 Rev B specify 60.0 % w/w solids (SG 1.618) — verify with process metallurgist before HAZOP.
- CYCLONE ROPING & RECOVERY LOSS: Operating cyclones above design feed density coarsens overflow product and causes severe copper recovery loss in rougher flotation; immediate response requires adding dilution water and checking pump speed per RB-4410-OM-001 Sec 7.
