# FP-5101 — Concentrate Filter Press

## Function

Vertical plate pressure filter dewatering thickened copper concentrate underflow (Stream S-511 at 51 t/h solids, 40 m3/h slurry, 65.0 wt% solids, SG 1.966) from thickener TH-5101 via feed pump PP-5101A/B (rated 210 m3/h per RB-4410-PS-PP5101 Rev B, 28 m TDH, 75 kW). Operates in cyclic batch mode (plate clamping, slurry filling, high-pressure membrane squeeze, core blow, compressed air drying, and automatic cake discharge) across 144 m² of filtration area to dewater copper concentrate cake to a design residual moisture of 8.5% w/w—safely below the Transportable Moisture Limit (TML = 9.8% w/w)—yielding commercial copper concentrate (26.0% Cu grade) for conveyancing to the concentrate storage and loadout facility, while returning filtrate to TH-5101. Vertical plate pressure filter press (144 m2) operating in Area 51. Dewaters thickened copper concentrate below Transportable Moisture Limit (target 8.5% w/w moisture vs TML 9.8% w/w) for ocean transport. Registered in Mechanical Equipment List RB-4410-PID-00-002 Rev B. Vertical plate pressure filter dewatering thickened copper concentrate underflow (Stream S-511 / Filter feed at 51 t/h solids, 40 m3/h slurry, 65.0 wt% solids, SG 1.966) from thickener TH-5101 via feed pump PP-5101A/B (rated 210 m3/h per RB-4410-PS-PP5101 Rev B, 28 m TDH, 75 kW). Operates in cyclic batch mode (plate clamping, slurry filling, high-pressure membrane squeeze, core blow, compressed air drying, and automatic cake discharge) across 144 m² of active filtration area per RB-4410-PID-51-001 Rev B to dewater copper concentrate cake to a design residual moisture of 8.5% w/w—strictly below the Transportable Moisture Limit (TML = 9.8% w/w) per Note 3 for maritime shipping under IMSBC Code—yielding commercial copper concentrate (26.0% Cu grade) for conveyancing to the concentrate storage and loadout facility, while returning filtrate to TH-5101 feedwell per Note 2. Vertical plate recessed pressure filter with 144 m² filtration area in Area 51. Dewaters concentrate thickener underflow (65 % solids w/w) to produce commercial copper concentrate cake. Operating Manual RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2 Section 10 establishes that cake moisture must be strictly maintained at target 8.5 % w/w and remain below the Transportable Moisture Limit (TML = 9.8 % w/w) to prevent cargo liquefaction and bulk carrier capsizing during maritime transit under the IMSBC Code.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- ⚠️ CONFLICT — CONCENTRATE PRODUCTION RATE: Drawing RB-4410-PFD-003 Rev B specifies 51 t/h solids (40 m3/h @ 65 wt% solids), whereas Drawing RB-4410-PFD-000 Rev B specifies 50.6 t/h solids — verify with engineer before HAZOP to ensure filter chamber volume, cycle frequency, and membrane squeeze compressor capacity handle the 51 t/h solids rate.
- Filter cloth blinding and plate seal blowout: Inadequate cloth washing or particulate blinding leads to slurry leakage past plate seals during high-pressure filling/squeezing, presenting high-pressure slurry spraying hazards and housekeeping risks.
- Excess cake moisture & cargo liquefaction: Failure of membrane squeeze or core blow leaves concentrate moisture above the Transportable Moisture Limit (TML, typically 9.5-10.0%), creating cargo liquefaction and vessel capsizing hazards during maritime shipping.
- Excess cake moisture & cargo liquefaction (TML 9.8% w/w): Failure of membrane squeeze, inadequate core blow, or cloth blinding leaves concentrate moisture above the Transportable Moisture Limit (TML = 9.8% w/w; design target 8.5% w/w). Transporting concentrate exceeding TML risks cargo liquefaction, dynamic shifting, and bulk carrier vessel capsizing during maritime shipping under the International Maritime Solid Bulk Cargoes (IMSBC) Code.
- Feed pump interlock & plate clamping integrity: Positive mechanical hydraulic clamping tonnage must be interlocked with feed pump PP-5101A to prevent high-pressure slurry injection prior to full plate pack sealing.
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — CONCENTRATE FEED PUMP RATED FLOW: Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-PP5101 Rev A specifies a rated slurry flow rate of 180 m³/h for PP-5101A/B, whereas Process Flow Diagram RB-4410-PFD-003 Rev B (Stream S-511) specifies a normal operating slurry flow of 40 m³/h (51 t/h solids, 27 t/h water at 65.0 wt% solids). The 180 m³/h rated pump capacity exceeds the normal operating flow by 350%; verify filter press filling flow rate profile and pump turndown/VSD control before HAZOP.
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — CONCENTRATE FEED PUMP RATED FLOW: Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-PP5101 Rev B specifies a rated slurry flow rate of 210 m³/h for PP-5101A/B (increased from 180 m³/h in Rev A after filter trade-off; supersedes Rev A), whereas Process Flow Diagram RB-4410-PFD-003 Rev B (Stream S-511) specifies a normal operating slurry flow of 40 m³/h (51 t/h solids, 27 t/h water at 65.0 wt% solids). The 210 m³/h rated pump capacity exceeds normal operating flow by 425% (operating at ~19.0% of rated design flow / BEP); verify filter press filling flow rate profile, pump turndown/VSD control, and minimum continuous flow recirculation line before HAZOP.
- Concentrate cake liquefaction and bulk cargo shift if moisture exceeds TML 9.8% w/w.
- Filter plate seal blowout under high slurry injection pressure.
- Cake moisture compliance with TML (Note 3): Note 3 of RB-4410-PID-51-001 Rev B mandates that concentrate cake moisture must remain strictly below the Transportable Moisture Limit (TML = 9.8% w/w, design target 8.5% w/w) for maritime bulk carrier shipping under the IMSBC Code. Off-spec high-moisture cake risks cargo liquefaction and catastrophic vessel capsize.
- Filtrate return line backpressure (Note 2): Filtrate returned to TH-5101 feedwell must remain unimpeded. Any line throttling or obstruction causes filtrate manifold overpressure and cloth seal blowing during core blow and membrane squeeze stages.
- CARGO LIQUEFACTION MARGIN: Cake moisture must not exceed TML 9.8 % w/w; target 8.5 % w/w. Moisture testing mandatory on every batch before port dispatch per RB-4410-OM-001 Sec 10.
