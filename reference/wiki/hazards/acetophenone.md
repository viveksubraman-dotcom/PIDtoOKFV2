# Hazard: Acetophenone (C₈H₈O)

> ⚠️ Acetophenone **solidifies at ~19.6°C** — close to ambient temperature. Heat tracing is required on all acetophenone-service lines and equipment to prevent solidification blockages.

**Chemical formula**: C₆H₅COCH₃ (C₈H₈O)  
**Molecular weight**: 120.15 g/mol  
**CAS No.**: 98-86-2  
**Process section**: CDN (minor cleavage by-product), DIST (acid aromatics / heavy ends)  
**Source**: SDS_98-86-2_acetophenone.pdf

## Health Hazards

### Acute Effects
- H302 (Acute Tox 4): harmful if swallowed
- Eye irritant (H319): causes irritation; flush with water immediately
- Inhalation: vapour irritates upper respiratory tract at concentrations above TLV; narcotic effects at high concentrations
- Skin: mild irritant on prolonged contact

### Exposure Control
- Low vapour pressure at ambient — vapour exposure is limited unless heated or in sprayed/aerosolised form
- TLV-TWA 10 ppm is conservative — indicates moderate chronic exposure concern

---

## Fire and Explosion Hazards

- Flash point 82°C — combustible liquid (Class IIIB); elevated fire risk only if heated above flash point
- High boiling point (202°C) and low vapour pressure — fire risk is lower than lighter process chemicals
- Firefighting: CO₂, dry chemical, water fog, foam
- In fires: produces CO and CO₂; no unusual decomposition hazards at normal fire conditions

## Emergency Response

### Spill
1. Contain with inert absorbent (sand, dry earth)
2. Below 20°C: material will solidify — collect as solid waste
3. Above 20°C: liquid; collect in labelled containers for disposal
4. Prevent entry into drains

### Fire
1. CO₂, dry chemical, or water fog; foam if quantity warrants
2. No unusual fire hazard unless heated significantly above flash point

### Person Contaminated
1. Inhalation: move to fresh air; seek medical attention if symptoms persist
2. Skin: wash with soap and water
3. Eyes: flush with water minimum 15 minutes; seek medical attention if irritation persists
4. Ingestion: seek medical attention; do not induce vomiting

## Plant-Specific Notes

- **Heat tracing required**: all lines, equipment, and instrumentation tapping points handling acetophenone must be heat-traced; trace failure causes solidification blockages
- Acetophenone is a minor by-product formed in cleavage from DMBA oxidation and from CHP decomposition side reactions
- Accumulates in heavy aromatics / acid aromatics streams in CDN section
- Low vapour pressure means low inhalation risk under normal conditions
