*Operating at 2.3 kg/cm²g / 135°C — significantly hotter and higher pressure than D-2308 (0.9 kg/cm²g / 117°C) because D-2309 handles SC3 steam condensate (3 kg/cm²g steam) vs D-2308 which handles SC1.5 condensate. H(90) insulation required to maintain temperature.*

## Function

Collects SC3 steam condensate from Flash Column Vaporizer E-2304. Level controlled by LIC-0701 → LV-0701. Condensate pumped by P-2309A/B to the SC3 condensate return system.

Structurally identical in purpose and arrangement to [[equipment/D-2308]] (Preflash Column Steam Heater Condensate Drum), but operates at higher pressure and temperature due to SC3 vs SC1.5 steam service.
