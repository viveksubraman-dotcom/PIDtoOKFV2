## Function

Serves as a break tank (siphon break) for CHP process water in the Oxidation Section. The break tank interrupts siphon forces in the process water circuit, preventing uncontrolled drainage of process water into downstream vessels. The high SG (0.9704) confirms this is predominantly water with dissolved process species. Carbon steel construction is acceptable at 50°C service temperature for dilute aqueous streams with low CHP concentration.

## Safety Notes
