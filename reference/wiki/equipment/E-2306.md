# E-2306 — Flash Column Bottoms Cooler

> ⚠️ Concentrated CHP on process side. See [[hazards/cumene-hydroperoxide]].

*Shell side = concentrated CHP process fluid (from Flash Column V-2302 bottoms). Tube side = cooling water (CW). High surface area (662.8 m²) relative to small duty (0.75 MM kcal/h) reflects the low temperature driving force: concentrated CHP at ~60°C cooled by cooling water at ~30-35°C — only ~15-25°C approach.*

## Function

E-2306 cools the concentrated CHP bottoms stream from Flash Column V-2302 before it proceeds to the Decomposer feed system. The cooling protects against premature CHP decomposition in downstream piping and ensures the feed enters the Decomposer system at a controlled temperature.

| Side | Description |

*Both inlet and outlet to CHP service — full CHP precautions on shell side. Tube side (cooling water) tube failure would result in CHP leaking into CW system — monitor CW return for CHP contamination.*

## Notes

- Low duty / large surface area — if process flow or temperature changes significantly, E-2306 performance changes proportionally. Rising outlet temperature from E-2306 indicates either flow increase or CW supply issue.
- No SIS instruments identified on E-2306 directly — protection is provided by UC-2302 upstream (Decomposer ESD cuts the CHP feed before it reaches this point).
