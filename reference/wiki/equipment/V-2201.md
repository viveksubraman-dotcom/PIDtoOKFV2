## Function

Washes the cumene recycle feed with water and dilute NaOH solution before it enters the oxidation section. The column removes water-soluble impurities, trace acids, and polar contaminants that could poison or interfere with the cumene oxidation reaction. Cumene (light hydrocarbon phase, SG 0.85) rises through the column while water/caustic (heavy phase, SG 1.0) flows down — counter-current liquid-liquid contact across 7 trays.

**Horizontal orientation**: Unusual for a liquid-liquid extractor — horizontal mounting is used here to achieve the long residence time required for multi-stage extraction with minimal column height (favored in plot-constrained installations). The liquid-full operation (no gas phase) is normal for this service.

**Caustic service**: The NaOH-containing wash water (1% NaOH in trays 4–6) makes this a caustic-service vessel. SA 240 Type 304L selected for compatibility with dilute caustic + aromatic hydrocarbon service.

**Operating pressure differential** (1.8 kg/cm²g top vs 4.7 kg/cm²g bottom) is purely due to the hydrostatic head of the liquid-full horizontal column — the pressure difference reflects the weight of 29.4 m of liquid at SG ~0.85–1.0.

## Connections

- **Cumene inlet (BD, 8"):** Recycle cumene from CDN section / ALKY section
- **Cumene outlet (AC, 8"):** Washed cumene → D-2201 Combined Feed Surge Drum → oxidizers
- **Water inlet (AD, 2"):** Fresh wash water (top section, trays 1–3)
- **NaOH inlet (X, 3"):** 1% NaOH solution (middle section, trays 4–6)
- **Aqueous outlet (CD, 4"):** Spent wash water + extracted impurities → effluent treatment

## Safety Notes

- Caustic service — all internals, gaskets, and seals must be NaOH-compatible
- Teflon/Neoprene/Viton A gaskets specified — rubber or asbestos gaskets are NOT acceptable (will degrade in aromatic hydrocarbon + caustic service)
- Liquid-full horizontal column: no free gas space — confirm N₂ blanketing procedures for startup/shutdown transitions
- High design pressure (15 kg/cm²g) relative to low operating pressure (1.8/4.7 kg/cm²g) provides large safety margin
