# OX-2201 — Oxidizer No.1

> ⚠️ CHP is produced in this vessel. Cumene + O₂ reaction is exothermic. CHP concentration rises through the vessel height. Temperature must be closely controlled — CHP thermal decomposition onset ~80°C. See [[hazards/cumene-hydroperoxide]].

## Function

First-stage oxidation reactor in the Hock (Cumene) Process. Air is dispersed into liquid cumene at 86°C through a sparger system; partial oxidation converts cumene to Cumene Hydroperoxide (CHP). The reaction product (oxidate) accumulates and is continuously withdrawn. Internal eductors maintain circulation to ensure uniform air-liquid contact. Quench spray nozzles provide emergency cooling capability.

**API 620 code**: Selected because the large diameter (21.7 m) and low operating pressure (0.187 kg/cm²g) place this vessel in the API 620 category — the ASME VIII code is typically impractical for large, low-pressure flat-bottom tanks of this size.

**Dual Stamp 304/304L**: Provides the corrosion resistance required for CHP/cumene service (stainless steel) with the dual stamp verifying both composition and carbon content limits for welding compatibility.

## Safety Notes

- Temperature control is critical — CHP decomposition is accelerated above 80°C and becomes violent at higher temperatures
- F(80) fire-rated insulation is required to limit heat input in a fire scenario (delay to boilover)
- Nitrogen connections (K) provide inert blanket capability to exclude oxygen during upset conditions
- Vacuum relief (PVRV-1001A/B) prevents vacuum collapse during cooling/drainage per API-2000
- Dual vacuum relief valves provide redundancy for this critical function
- Large pumpout capacity (1,138 m³/hr max) enables rapid emergency drawdown

## Connections

- **Feed (F, 16"):** Fresh/recycle cumene from D-2201 Combined Feed Surge Drum
- **Circulation outlet (B, 24"):** To oxidizer circulation pumps → back via eductors
- **Air inlet (D, 2×18"):** Scrubbed compressed air from D-2207 Compressed Air Scrubber
- **Vapor outlet (A, 30"):** Vent gas to D-2208 Warm Vent Gas Separator and D-2203 Chilled Vent Gas Separator
- **Product outlet:** Oxidate (cumene + CHP) to D-2201 Combined Feed Surge Drum → CDN section
