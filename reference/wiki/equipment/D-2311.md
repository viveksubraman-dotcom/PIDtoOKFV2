# D-2311 — 98% Sulfuric Acid Refill Tank

> ⚠️ **98% Sulfuric acid — severe contact hazard.** See [[hazards/cumene-hydroperoxide]] for general CDN hazard context.

## Function

D-2311 is the bulk storage/refill tank for 98% H₂SO₄ catalyst. It is filled periodically from road tanker or plant acid supply and then used to top up the working injection tank [[equipment/D-2310]]. Smaller than D-2310, it acts as the secondary reserve.
