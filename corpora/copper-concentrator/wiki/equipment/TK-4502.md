# TK-4502 — PAX Dosing Tank

## Function

PAX Dosing Tank TK-4502 acts as an intermediate storage and buffer tank in Area 45 receiving 20% w/v aqueous potassium amyl xanthate (PAX, CAS 2720-73-2, C6H11KOS2) solution prepared in mixing tank TK-4501. It supplies PAX dosing pump PP-4501A to continuously meter collector solution to flotation conditioning tank TK-4101 and rougher flotation cells FC-4101 through FC-4107 per operating manual RB-4410-OM-001. Located in an enclosed room continuously ventilated by extraction fan FN-4501 to prevent toxic and flammable CS2 vapor accumulation (LEL 1.3%, auto-ignition 90 °C). Grounded by SDS_2720-73-2_potassium-amyl-xanthate.pdf. Depicted on P&ID RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B and referenced in Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-TK4501 Rev B.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- CS2 evolution on decomposition: Aqueous PAX solutions decompose slowly over time releasing toxic, volatile, flammable carbon disulfide (CS2) gas. Continuous mechanical ventilation of the enclosed room via FN-4501 is mandatory per Note 1 of RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B.
- Corrosion & Metallurgy: 316L stainless steel is required to withstand aqueous xanthate solution corrosion.
- Reagent Dosing Governance: Reagent dosing rates to the flotation circuit are governed by Operating Manual RB-4410-OM-001 (Note 4 of RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B).
- Chemical Contact: Full chemical PPE required during sampling and maintenance.
- Chemical Contact & PPE: Chemical goggles, nitrile gloves, and respiratory protection required during sampling and maintenance per SDS_2720-73-2. First aid requires 15-minute water flush.
- Firefighting Directive (SDS_2720-73-2): In case of xanthate fire, use dry chemical, CO2, or foam. Do not use water jet on burning xanthate.
