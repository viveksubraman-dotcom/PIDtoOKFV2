## Function

Separates the hydrocarbon (Cumene + CHP) phase from the aqueous (process water) phase in the oxidizer condensate and vent gas condensate streams. The organic layer floats on top of the denser aqueous phase; the interface level at vessel centerline is the normal operating point.

Receives liquid from:
- D-2203 (Chilled Vent Gas Separator) — gravity drain
- D-2208 (Warm Vent Gas Separator) — gravity drain
- D-2211 (Charcoal Adsorber KO Drum) — gravity drain

The recovered Cumene (hydrocarbon phase) is returned to the oxidizer feed system. The aqueous phase (process water) is routed to the CHP process water break tank D-2202 or water treatment.

**Elevation note**: D-2203 and D-2211 must be ≥ 2500 mm above D-2205 top; D-2208 must be ≥ 600 mm above D-2205 top to ensure gravity drainage.
