# P-2320 — Acid Aromatics Sump Pit Pump

## Function

P-2320 is a submerged sump pump installed inside [[equipment/X-2321]] (Acid Aromatic Sump Pit). It pumps accumulated liquid (leaks, spills, floor drains from acid aromatics area) either to [[equipment/D-2307]] (Acid Aromatics Sump) for process recovery, or to the oily water sewer (91-0058) if contamination level warrants direct disposal.

**Vendor-supplied package:** Both the pump and its support structure are designed and supplied by the pump vendor. Piping connection points only are shown on the P&ID.

## Operating Conditions

P-2320 operates intermittently — runs when liquid accumulates in the sump pit. No automatic level control instruments shown on P&ID; likely manual start or vendor-supplied level float control.

## Connections

| Direction | Line | From / To |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| Suction | Submerged inlet | From [[equipment/X-2321]] sump pit floor |
| Discharge Option A | Pump discharge → D-2307 | Recovery path: to Acid Aromatics Sump |
| Discharge Option B | Pump discharge → OWS 91-0058 | Disposal path: oily water sewer |
| Spillback | Pump spillback line | Returns to X-2321 sump |

**Discharge routing selection:** Operator-selected valve arrangement (not shown in detail on P&ID). Contaminated fluid likely routed to D-2307; clean spillage to OWS.

## Safety Note

Per Drawing 0020A **Note 6:** "NO AIR ALLOWED IN THE CLOSED DRAIN SYSTEM FROM PUMPS, INSTRUMENTS, PIPELINES, EXCHANGERS AND THE RELATED EQUIPMENTS." This applies to P-2320 discharge piping — all connections must be sealed against air ingress.
