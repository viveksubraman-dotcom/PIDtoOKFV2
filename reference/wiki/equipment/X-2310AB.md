# X-2310A/B — Direct Neutralization Static Mixers

## Function

X-2310A and X-2310B are the **direct neutralization contact points** where diamine from [[equipment/P-2306AB]] mixes with the crude CDN product stream. The static mixers provide thorough mixing of the amine neutralizing agent with the crude product (which carries residual sulfuric acid catalyst from the Decomposer). Two static mixers are installed — one operating and one available for service continuity.

After neutralization in X-2310A/B, the crude product stream (now neutralized phenol + acetone + cumene) proceeds to the **Fractionation Feed Tanks** at the Fractionation Section.

**Note (Drawing 0019 Note 6):** Key interlocked on operating set of valves for locked open shall be provided on inlet side — same requirement as the calorimeters. Prevents isolation errors during operation.

## Injection Nozzle Detail (Drawing 0019 — Detail "B")

A specific nozzle design is specified for the diamine injection point into the crude product pipe:

| Specification | Value |
|---------------|-------|
| Type | Distributor — Type 1 |
| Nozzle material | 1" SCH 160 304L Stainless Steel pipe |
| Injection hole | ½" diameter, flush with plate |
| Hole orientation | Downstream, at centreline of pipe |
| Installation | Horizontal line, top or side only |
| Provide | Indication of location of hole |

The injection nozzle injects diamine as a fine stream directed downstream to minimise stagnant zones and maximise mixing length before the static mixer internals.

## Crude Product Flow Path (Drawing 0019)

```
FROM E-2309 (Crude Product Cooler, Dwg 23-0014A)
  → 6"-CP-23-014004-L2A1-NI
    → Diamine injection from P-2306A (into X-2310A line)
    → X-2310A — Static Mixer (or X-2310B — standby)
    → Sprung Phenol blended in (from Phenol Recovery Section)
    → Crude Product to Fractionation Feed Tanks (Fractionation Section)
```

**AT-1901 note (Drawing 0019):** Analyzer is "By Analyzer Vendor" — UOP-specified analyzer for residual acid measurement. Tagged as sulfuric acid analyzer; provides feedback signal to the diamine ratio controller. Low acid (AAL) = too much diamine; High acid (AAH) = insufficient diamine.

**Confirmed by Analyzer Process Data Sheet (PS-0003, 2026-06-16)** as **AT/AY-23-1901**: colorimetric titration method, range 20–60 wt ppm H₂SO₄, normal 38 ppm. Located on the crude product line at "Crude Product to Fractionation Feed Tanks" — downstream of this static mixer, consistent with its role as the post-neutralization verification point. Reaction cup material must NOT be PTFE. Stream composition: Phenol 51.31 wt%, Acetone 30.76 wt%, Cumene 13.48 wt%, AMS 2.05 wt%, Water 1.11 wt%, Cumyl Phenols 0.48 wt%. See [[instruments/analyzers-cdn]].

## Key Process Connections

| From / To | Line | Service |
|-----------|------|---------|
| FROM E-2309 (Crude Product Cooler) | 6"-CP-23-014004-L2A1-NI | Crude CDN product at 43°C |
| FROM P-2306A/B | 1¼" DA lines | Diamine injection |
| FROM Pressure Controller 23-0014A | Signal line | Crude product pressure/flow signal for ratio control |
| FROM Sprung Phenol Coalescer (Fractionation) | ¾" line | Recovered phenol from aqueous phase |
| TO Fractionation Feed Tanks | 3"-CP line to Fractionation | Neutralized crude product |
| TO Acid Aromatics Sump 23-0020A | Drain | Neutralization area drains |

**Sprung Phenol:** Recovered aqueous phenol from the Phenol Recovery section at Fractionation is blended back into the neutralized crude product stream here. This recycles phenol that would otherwise go to waste treatment.

**"Minimum Run of Straight Pipe"** requirement: The drawing specifies minimum straight pipe runs before and after X-2310A/B to ensure proper flow profile for mixing and instrumentation.
