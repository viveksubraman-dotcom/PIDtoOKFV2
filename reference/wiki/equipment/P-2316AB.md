*55 kW motors are the largest in the CDN vacuum system. Ex nA (non-sparking, Zone 2) motor classification — reflects the presence of flammable vapors in the vacuum system area. Two pumps A and B are in standby/parallel configuration.*

## Process Role

P-2316A/B are the second-stage compressors in the two-stage vacuum producing system (X-2301). The steam jet ejectors (J-2301/J-2302A/B) compress non-condensible vapor from 10 mmHgA to 79.7 mmHgA; P-2316A/B then compress from 79.7 mmHgA to 804 mmHgA (near atmospheric) for discharge.

**Liquid ring principle:** P-2316A/B are liquid ring vacuum pumps — the impeller rotates eccentrically in a casing partially filled with sealant liquid (process water). The rotating liquid forms a ring inside the casing, trapping and compressing gas between impeller blades. This design:
- Handles vapor/liquid mixtures without mechanical damage
- Requires no lubrication of gas-contact parts
- Can handle process vapors (cumene) without contamination risk
- Self-sealing design prevents gas leakage

## Sealant Circuit

The liquid ring requires continuous sealant supply:
- **Supply:** Process water from CHP Process Water Pumps at Oxidation Section
- **Recirculation:** P-2316A/B discharge → D-2316 Separator → P-2317A/B → E-2317A/B (coolers) → back to P-2316A/B sealant inlet
- **Temperature control:** E-2317A/B sealant coolers maintain sealant temperature for pump efficiency and to limit vapor pressure of sealant

## SIS and Safety

| Tag | Type | Description | SIS Role |
|-----|------|-------------|---------|
| **Motor trip** | UC-2303 output | Motor de-energized on UC-2303 ESD | Effect |
| **HXS-23-2316** | DCS-only restart switch | "RESET HAND SWITCH (ONLY DCS S/W) FOR P-2316A/B, P-2317A/B: HXS-23-2316" per Note 7, Drawing 0010A | Restart requirement |

**DCS-only restart significance:** After any UC-2303 trip, P-2316A/B cannot be restarted from field starters. The operator must use the DCS software hand switch HXS-23-2316. This ensures:
1. Full assessment of the trip cause at the DCS before restart
2. All UC-2303 initiators have been checked and cleared
3. Sequential restart of sealant pumps (P-2317A/B) before vacuum pumps is enforced by DCS logic

## Connections

- **Suction:** From E-2316 Inter Condenser outlet (from X-2301 ejector system)
- **Discharge:** To D-2316 Separator (gas/liquid separation)
- **Sealant in:** From P-2317A/B via E-2317A/B sealant coolers
- **Drain:** To [N7] on Drawing 0010A

## Engineering Notes

1. **Ex nA motor class** — Zone 2 explosive atmosphere classification in the vacuum pump area. Motor must be spark-free to prevent ignition of trace flammable vapors.
2. **79.7 mmHgA suction** — approximately 10% of atmospheric pressure. Net positive displacement must be maintained at this very low absolute pressure.
3. **Two in parallel** — one operating, one standby. Standby start requires DCS HXS-23-2316 initiation (no auto-start).
4. **Nash Scope:** "NASH SCOPE SHIP LOOSE" label on drawing — Nash Scope is an inspection/measurement device for liquid ring pump performance verification during commissioning or maintenance.
