## Process Role

E-2304 acts as the reboiler (vaporizer) for Flash Column V-2302. SC3 steam on the shell side provides heat to partially vaporize the concentrated CHP bottoms circulating on the tube side. The resulting vapor-liquid mixture returns to V-2302 for separation. This drives the second-stage cumene evaporation, concentrating the CHP from ~40 wt% (Preflash Column bottoms) to ~80-85 wt% (Flash Column bottoms).

Steam condensate leaves via the steam condensate line to D-2309 (Drawing 0007A).

## SIS Temperature Instruments

> Four SIS temperature initiators — UC-2301 Causes #8 and #9 — SIL 2 and unrated respectively.

| SIS Tag | DCS Equivalent | Voting | Time Delay | SIL | Description |
|---------|---------------|--------|-----------|-----|-------------|
| TXSHH-0701A | TAHH-0701A | **1oo2 (with 0702A)** | **YES — A only** | **2** | Vaporizer vapor space/outlet Temp HH, pair A |
| TXSHH-0702A | TAHH-0702A | 1oo2 (with 0701A) | **YES — A only** | **2** | Vaporizer vapor space/outlet Temp HH, pair A |
| TXSHH-0701B | TAHH-0701B | **1oo2 (with 0702B)** | NO | — | Vaporizer vapor space/outlet Temp HH, pair B |
| TXSHH-0702B | TAHH-0702B | 1oo2 (with 0702B) | NO | — | Vaporizer vapor space/outlet Temp HH, pair B |

**TIME DELAY FOR A ONLY**: TXSHH-0701A and TXSHH-0702A have a time delay before triggering UC-2301. The B pair (TXSHH-0701B/0702B) acts immediately when the threshold is exceeded. This architecture provides:
- Normal startup/transient protection: time-delayed "A" pair prevents spurious trips from brief temperature excursions
- Final protection: immediate "B" pair triggers without delay for sustained high temperatures

Each pair is 1oo2 — either transmitter in the pair exceeding the HH setpoint triggers the trip for that pair. The A pair (SIL 2) is the primary rated shutdown initiator.

**DCS critical alarms**: TXAHH-0701 (CRIT) and TXAHH-0702 (CRIT) provide operator alerts before the SIS thresholds are reached.

## Engineering Note

The two 1oo2 voting pairs (A-pair SIL 2 with time delay, B-pair immediate) for TXSHH on E-2304 is more stringent than most other Concentration section instruments. E-2304 handles the most concentrated CHP in the entire concentration section (V-2302 bottoms at ~80-85 wt% CHP) — any overheating here has the highest CHP decomposition potential outside the Decomposer itself. The SIL 2 / time-delayed / immediate architecture reflects this elevated risk.

**Emergency coolant note**: Reliable cooling water and fire water may be used as emergency coolant through the shell side — this drove the shell design pressure increase from 7.0 to 10.5 kg/cm²g (per Note 5). An expansion joint accommodates the thermal differential between hot shell-side steam and cold emergency coolant.

**Design reuse check**: Per the data sheet, the existing Train I exchanger (PPCL data sheet 13850-8100-DS-0042, E-1304) performs −30.7% undersurface at the new duty of 4.147×1.05 MM kcal/h and is **not thermally suitable to reuse** — Train II required a new design.
