# D-2206 — CHP Sump

> ⚠️ CHP is the primary fluid in this sump. This is the catchment vessel for CHP spills and drains in the Oxidation Section. Secondary containment is mandatory. See [[hazards/cumene-hydroperoxide]].

## Function

Collects drains, spills, and pump seal leakage from CHP-containing equipment in the Oxidation Section. The sump is located in an underground pit; the riser (788 mm ID) extends to grade level for pump access and liquid removal. CHP sump pumps transfer collected liquid back to the oxidizer system for recovery.

**Secondary containment**: EPA-mandated secondary containment is built around D-2206. The sump itself serves as the primary containment volume for the OXI section drain system.

**Underground installation**: The vessel body is below grade; only the riser and pump nozzle connection emerge at or above grade. 304L material selected for CHP service compatibility.

## Connections

- Inlet (BI, 6"): Drains from OXI section equipment, pump seals, flanged drain points
- Pump outlet (AX): CHP sump pump discharge → back to oxidizer system
- Vent (BV, 6"): To vent/flare header (hydrocarbons and CHP vapors)

## Safety Notes

> ⚠️ CHP is a peroxide — thermal decomposition risk above ~80°C (onset). Sump must be maintained at ambient/cool temperature. Do not apply steam tracing or heat to this vessel. Decomposition in a confined sump could cause pressure surge and vessel failure. See [[hazards/cumene-hydroperoxide]].

Secondary containment must be maintained in serviceable condition at all times. Any berm breaches or damage to the concrete containment structure must be repaired before resuming normal operations.
