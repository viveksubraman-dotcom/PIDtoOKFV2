## Hazards & Safeguards

- High-pressure pipeline sanding & catastrophic plugging: Operating at 62.0 wt% solids across 6.2 km creates high risk of settling; an unmanaged series pump trip without immediate emergency clean water flush causes rapid solids deposition, seizing the pipeline and requiring hazardous mechanical unblocking or pipeline replacement.
- Hydraulic water hammer & transient overpressure: Simultaneous trip of 3 series pumps with high kinetic momentum in a 6.2 km slurry column can generate massive transient hydraulic shock waves, threatening overland pipeline rupture and environmental slurry release.
- Gland seal water starvation & abrasive wear: Tailings slurry at 1.652 SG is highly abrasive; failure of gland seal water supply causes rapid slurry penetration into stuffing boxes, destroying shaft sleeves and mechanical seals.
- Overland pipeline rupture / overpressure: PT-6103 initiates SIF-6101 (SIL 2) at PAHH 38 bar(g) (pre-alarm PAH 36 bar(g)) to trip all three series pumps PP-6101A/B/C simultaneously, mitigating catastrophic overpressure failure of the 6.2 km overland pipeline.
