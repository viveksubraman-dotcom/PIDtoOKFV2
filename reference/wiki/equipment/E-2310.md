*E-2310 is the second-stage condenser for the concentration overhead vapor circuit. E-2301 (16.52 MMkcal/hr) condenses the bulk of the overhead cumene; E-2310 catches the residual vapor that E-2301 cannot condense under tropical ambient conditions. E-2310 is ~57× smaller than E-2301 by duty.*

## Process Role

E-2310 handles the uncondensed overhead vapor that passes through E-2301 without condensing. This vapor exists because:
- E-2301 uses refrigerated cooling (RCS, ~8-12°C refrigerant) to condense vapors at 28-52°C under deep vacuum
- A small fraction of vapor carries over, particularly at higher-than-normal loads or during startup
- E-2310 provides the final condensation step before residual non-condensibles enter the vacuum system (X-2301)

The process flow is:  
E-2301 (uncondensed vapor outlet) → **E-2310 shell side** → condensate collected → non-condensibles exit to X-2301 ejector/pump system.

E-2310 uses chilled water (CHS from Unit 62) on the tube side, providing colder cooling than E-2301 to capture remaining vapors.

## Critical Layout Constraint

> **125 METRES MAXIMUM EQUIVALENT LENGTH OF PIPE FROM PREFLASH AND FLASH COLUMNS CONDENSER TO FLASH COLUMN OVERHEAD VAPOR CHILLER**

This constraint (from Drawing 0010 and confirmed on Drawing 0008A) limits the physical distance E-2310 can be installed from E-2301. The limit exists because:
- The vapor line operates under deep vacuum (18-19 mmHgA) — any additional pressure drop reduces column efficiency
- The 30" CUG vapor line must slope continuously (no horizontal runs — see below)
- Longer lines increase the risk of vapor condensing in the pipe and collecting as liquid pockets

> **HORIZONTAL RUNS NOT ALLOWED — 45° MINIMUM ANGLE FROM HORIZONTAL on all vapor condensate lines from E-2310.**  
This is a layout mandate, not a preference. Lines carrying vapor/condensate under vacuum must be sloped to prevent liquid accumulation that would obstruct vapor flow and potentially cause liquid hammer.

## Insulation Note

C(50)/C(50) cold insulation on both shell and tube sides — same type as E-2301. This prevents moisture condensation on the cold metal surfaces and maintains chilling efficiency at Map Ta Phut tropical ambient (~35-40°C).

## Engineering Significance

Without E-2310, any vapor that escapes E-2301 would enter the vacuum system (X-2301) directly. This would:
1. Increase non-condensible load on the ejectors and liquid ring pumps
2. Allow cumene vapor to pass through to the charcoal adsorber (increasing adsorber loading)
3. Reduce vacuum system efficiency (higher operating pressure → columns operate at higher pressure → reduces separation efficiency)

The 224.4 m² surface area is large relative to its 0.29 MMkcal/hr duty, indicating the E-2310 operates with a small temperature driving force — consistent with the very low temperatures involved (column overhead vapor at ~5-10°C chilled to near 0°C condensation point).
