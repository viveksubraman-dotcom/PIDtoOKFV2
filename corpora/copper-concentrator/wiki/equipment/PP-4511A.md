# PP-4511A — Frother Dosing Pump

## Function

Frother Dosing Pump PP-4511A is a chemical metering pump located in the bunded Area 45 reagent area. It draws liquid frother from storage tank TK-4511 and meters it to flotation cells per Operating Manual RB-4410-OM-001 (nominal 20 g/t dry ore, equivalent to 53.8 kg/h at design throughput). The fluid handled is subject to a cross-document discrepancy between Polyglycol ether frother DF-250 type (per P&ID RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B) and MIBC (per Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-TK4511 Rev B). Depicted on P&ID RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- ⚠️ CONFLICT — FROTHER REAGENT CHEMISTRY & ELECTRICAL APPARATUS: P&ID RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B specifies Polyglycol ether frother (DF-250 type), whereas Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-TK4511 Rev B specifies MIBC (flash point 41 °C, Class 3 flammable liquid). If MIBC is used, pump motor, local junction boxes, and instrumentation must be certified for Zone 2 per RB-4410-STD-HAC-001. Seal elastomers (e.g. EPDM vs Viton) must also be verified for chemical compatibility with the selected frother chemistry before HAZOP.
- Dosing rate disruption: Over-frothing causes rapid unselective mass pull and concentrate grade collapse; under-frothing leads to froth bed thinning and chalcopyrite losses to tailings. Dosing rates must strictly adhere to RB-4410-OM-001.
- Spill containment: Pump is located inside bunded area per Note 2 of RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B.
