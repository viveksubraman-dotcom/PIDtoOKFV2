## Purpose

Administrative introduction to the PTT GC internal HAZOP Leadership Training Course. Covers course objectives, daily schedule, and workshop rules. No process-specific technical content.

## Course Scope (3 Days)

| Module | Topic |
|--------|-------|
| 00 | Introduction — course expectations |
| 01 | Hazard & Risk — definitions, risk concept → see [[sources/hazop-leadership-training-ch1]] |
| 02 | Overview PHA techniques — comparison of PHA tools |
| 03 | HAZOP introduction — history, purpose, limitations |
| 04 | HAZOP study methodology — 9-step method, guidance for each step |
| 05 | HAZOP preparation — types of review for effective HAZOP |
| 06 | Facilitator skills — tips for smooth and effective HAZOP |
| 07 | GC practice guidelines — related documents, IPL consideration |

## Course Objectives

- Understand HAZOP methodology to guide and lead a HAZOP team
- Understand steps and concerns for preparing, planning, running, and reporting HAZOP
- Qualify as HAZOP Leader per PTT GC requirements
