# ML-4201 — ML-4201 — Regrind Mill (vertical stirred)

## Function

Vertical stirred concentrate regrind mill receiving rougher flotation concentrate slurry (Stream S-411) from rougher cells FC-4101 at 215 t/h solids and 580 m3/h slurry at 30.0 wt% solids. Regrinds rougher concentrate for mineral liberation from rougher flotation size down to P80 = 38 µm using 3–5 mm ceramic grinding media ahead of cleaner flotation in cleaner column FC-4301. Vertical stirred regrind mill operating in Area 4300 to grind rougher flotation concentrate (Stream S-411 at 215 t/h solids, 30.0 wt% solids) down to target liberation size P80 = 38 µm using 3–5 mm ceramic media before feeding cleaner flotation column FC-4301. Registered in Mechanical Equipment List RB-4410-PID-00-002 Rev B. Vertical stirred regrind mill receiving rougher concentrate (Stream S-411 at 215 t/h solids, 580 m³/h slurry). Powered by 3,000 kW drive motor utilizing 3–5 mm ceramic grinding media. Sized to regrind rougher concentrates down to P80 38 µm ahead of cleaner flotation column FC-4301 per RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- Overgrinding leading to mineral slimes (-10 µm) which exhibit poor flotation recovery and high reagent consumption.
- Grinding media lockup and slurry thermal buildup upon loss of dilution water or slurry feed flow.
- High abrasive and corrosive wear on ceramic/steel grinding media and liner components.
- High abrasive and corrosive wear on ceramic grinding media (3-5 mm) and stirred mill liner components.
- Slurry feed interruption while 3,000 kW vertical stirred drive operates leads to media bed compaction, slurry boiling, and severe media attrition.
- Regrind mill media lockup and thermal overheating upon slurry feed interruption; low flow interlock required.
- MEDIA ATTRITION & BED OVERHEATING: Feed slurry interruption during 3,000 kW stirred mill operation risks rapid media attrition, bed heating, and ceramic bead degradation; feed flow interlock mandatory per RB-4410-OM-001 Sec 9.
