# CR-2101 — Primary Gyratory Crusher

## Function

Primary 60 x 113 gyratory crusher receiving run-of-mine ore (up to 5,500 t/h peak) via dump pocket/feeder FE-2101 and reducing rock to SAG mill feed size (P80 = 150 mm, CSS = 165 mm). Discharges to CV-2101 overland conveyor advancing to coarse ore stockpile ST-2101 via stream S-101. Lubrication and hydroset systems are engineered per vendor drawing VD-2101. Registered in Mechanical Equipment List RB-4410-PID-00-002 Rev B and detailed on P&ID RB-4410-PID-21-001 Rev B. Primary gyratory crusher (60 x 113) reducing run-of-mine ore down to P80 150 mm at up to 5,500 t/h design throughput to feed the coarse ore stockpile via discharge conveyor CV-2101 per RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2. Sized with 1,200 kW installed drive motor. Corroborates 1,200 kW drive rating against 1,000 kW in the mechanical equipment list.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- Heavy rotating machinery and high-tonnage ore crushing hazard; mandatory interlocks on feed pocket level to prevent crusher choke or stall.
- Airborne silica/mineral dust emission during discharge; dust suppression and extraction systems must operate continuously.
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — DESIGN THROUGHPUT: Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-CR2101 Rev B specifies 5,500 t/h crusher design throughput, whereas PFD RB-4410-PFD-001 Rev B specifies a nominal operating solids throughput of 2,688 t/h for Stream S-101 — verify with process engineer before HAZOP (instantaneous dumping/feeder mechanical capacity vs continuous plant average mass balance).
- Tramp metal or uncrushable foreign objects can cause catastrophic mantle/concave damage or spider bushing failure; requires metal detection, rock breaker positioning, and motor trip interlocks.
- Lubrication and hydraulic spider/eccentric cooling failure risks severe bearing wipe on 1,200 kW drive; lube pressure, flow, and temperature trips must be interlocked with drive motor.
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — INSTALLED MOTOR POWER: Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-CR2101 Rev B specifies 1,200 kW installed power, whereas Mechanical Equipment List RB-4410-PID-00-002 Rev B specifies 1,000 kW installed / motor power — verify electrical load list and vendor drive sizing before HAZOP.
- Lubrication and hydraulic spider/eccentric cooling failure risks severe bearing wipe on drive motor; lube pressure, flow, and temperature trips must be interlocked with drive motor.
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — Installed Power: RB-4410-PS-CR2101 Rev B specifies 1,200 kW, whereas RB-4410-PS-CR2101 Rev B / RB-4410-PID-00-002 Rev B specifies 1,200 (PS-CR2101) / 1,000 (PID-00-002) (⚠️ CONFLICT) kW — verify with engineer before HAZOP
- Airborne silica/mineral dust emission during discharge; dust suppression sprays and dust extraction systems must operate continuously (P&ID Note 2).
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — DESIGN THROUGHPUT: Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-CR2101 Rev B and P&ID RB-4410-PID-21-001 Rev B specify 5,500 t/h max crusher design throughput, whereas PFD RB-4410-PFD-001 Rev B specifies a nominal operating solids throughput of 2,688 t/h for Stream S-101 — verify with process engineer before HAZOP (instantaneous dumping/feeder mechanical surge capacity vs continuous plant average mass balance).
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — INSTALLED MOTOR POWER: Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-CR2101 Rev B and P&ID RB-4410-PID-21-001 Rev B specify 1,200 kW installed power, whereas Mechanical Equipment List RB-4410-PID-00-002 Rev B specifies 1,000 kW installed / motor power — verify electrical load list and vendor drive sizing before HAZOP.
- Lubrication and hydroset system failure: Crusher lubrication and hydraulic hydroset positioning systems are governed by vendor package drawing VD-2101; loss of lube pressure or oil over-temperature risks immediate eccentric bearing seizure.
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — Crusher Mechanical Design Throughput: RB-4410-PS-CR2101 Rev B specifies 5,500 t/h, whereas RB-4410-PID-21-001 Rev B; RB-4410-PS-CR2101 Rev B specifies 5,500 (⚠️ CONFLICT vs PFD S-101 2,688 t/h nominal) t/h — verify with engineer before HAZOP
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — Installed Power: RB-4410-PS-CR2101 Rev B; RB-4410-PS-CR2101 Rev B / RB-4410-PID-00-002 Rev B specifies 1,200 kW, whereas RB-4410-PID-21-001 Rev B; RB-4410-PS-CR2101 Rev B; RB-4410-PID-00-002 Rev B specifies 1,200 (PID-21-001 & PS-CR2101) / 1,000 (PID-00-002) (⚠️ CONFLICT) kW — verify with engineer before HAZOP
- CAVITY BRIDGING & OVERLOAD: Oversize ore boulders (>1,525 mm) or unmetered dump pocket surging causes mantle jamming and motor overload; rock breaker and cavity level interlocks mandatory per RB-4410-OM-001 Sec 2.
