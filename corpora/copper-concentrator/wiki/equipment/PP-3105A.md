# PP-3105A — SAG Mill HP Lift Pump A

## Function

High-pressure positive-displacement lift pump A operating in 1 duty / 1 standby configuration with PP-3105B. Draws ISO VG 460 lubricating oil from 12 m3 reservoir TK-3105 and injects it at high pressure (normal discharge 120 bar(g), system design 170 bar(g)) under the SAG Mill ML-3101 trunnion bearing pads. Establishes a continuous hydrostatic fluid film to jack and lift the 2,450 t total mill charge off the white metal Babbitt pads prior to mill inching or rotation, preventing catastrophic journal and bearing wiping. Monitored by pressure transmitter PT-3104 (interlocked to SIF-3102 mill start inhibit) and protected against blocked discharge overpressure by safety relief valve PSV-3105. Note that coolers, filters, and heaters are omitted from drawing RB-4410-PID-31-002 Rev B per Note 3.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- Loss of hydrostatic lift pressure / pump failure: Failure to lift the mill journal prior to drum rotation causes metal-to-metal rubbing, Babbitt white metal wiping, and journal gouging under the 2,450 t charge load. Interlocked via SIF-3102 (PT-3104 PALL 70 bar(g) inhibits mill start; pre-trip alarm PAL at 90 bar(g)).
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — PSV-3105 Relief Valve Set Pressure vs System Design Pressure: Drawing RB-4410-PID-31-002 Rev B specifies PSV-3105 set pressure as 180 bar(g), which exceeds the 170 bar(g) system design pressure and violates drawing Note 1 ("PSV-3105 protects HP lift pump discharge; set pressure shall not exceed system design pressure"). Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-0032 Rev B specifies set pressure as 160 bar(g). Setting PSV-3105 at 180 bar(g) creates an unmitigated piping/pump manifold overpressure hazard. Reconcile with piping/mechanical engineer before HAZOP.
- Pump cavitation or oil starvation due to low level in lube oil reservoir TK-3105.
- Lube oil contamination or viscosity degradation: High oil temperature or particulate ingress accelerates bearing wear. Note 3 states coolers, filters, and heaters are not shown on this P&ID; auxiliary vendor documentation must be verified.
