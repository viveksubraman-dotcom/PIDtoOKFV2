## Function

Collects steam condensate from the PIPB (Di-isopropylbenzene) Column reboiler in the Alkylation Section. The PIPB column separates di-isopropylbenzene (DIPB) from cumene product; the reboiler condensate pot D-2122 is smaller than D-2121 (Cumene Column pot) in direct proportion to the smaller PIPB column. Same design conditions as D-2121 — identical high-pressure steam service at 260°C.

- Outlet: Condensate recovery header

## Comparison with D-2121

D-2122 is functionally identical to [[equipment/D-2121]] but smaller (550 mm ID vs 1150 mm ID, 1900 mm vs 2550 mm height). The smaller size reflects the lower duty of the PIPB column reboiler compared to the Cumene Column reboiler. Both pots share identical design pressure (50 kg/cm²g), temperature (300°C max), and fluid (steam condensate, SG 0.8).
