# TK-4501 — PAX Xanthate Mixing Tank

## Function

PAX Xanthate Mixing Tank TK-4501 mixes solid potassium amyl xanthate (PAX) with process water to prepare a 20% w/v aqueous collector solution in a 40 m³ 316L stainless steel vessel, and transfers the prepared solution to downstream dosing tank TK-4502. The mixing tank is located inside an enclosed room equipped with dedicated extraction fan FN-4501 running continuously to exhaust toxic and flammable carbon disulfide (CS2) gas generated during xanthate handling and decomposition. Solution is metered from TK-4502 via dosing pump PP-4501A to flotation conditioning tank TK-4101 and rougher cells per RB-4410-OM-001. Registered in Mechanical Equipment List RB-4410-PID-00-002 Rev B and P&ID RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B. Atmospheric 40 m³ 316L stainless steel reagent mixing tank in Area 45 used for batch dissolution of solid potassium amyl xanthate (PAX) pellets into 20 % w/v aqueous collector solution. Operating procedures in RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2 mandate that make-up water temperature must never exceed 40 °C to prevent accelerated decomposition into toxic and flammable carbon disulfide (CS2, LEL 1.3%, auto-ignition 90 °C). Mechanical room exhaust fan FN-4501 must run continuously whenever PAX is present, and non-sparking tools are strictly required. Doses at 25 g/t dry ore. PAX Xanthate Mixing Tank TK-4501 mixes solid potassium amyl xanthate (PAX) with process water to prepare a 20% w/v aqueous collector solution in a 40 m³ 316L stainless steel vessel, and transfers the prepared solution to downstream dosing tank TK-4502. The mixing tank is located inside an enclosed room equipped with dedicated extraction fan FN-4501 running continuously to exhaust toxic and flammable carbon disulfide (CS2) gas generated during xanthate handling and decomposition. Evaluated under HAZOP Study RB-4410-STD-HAZOP-001 Rev 1 Node HZ-45-03, with mandatory recommendation ACT-HAZOP-45-03 requiring installation of CS2 detector AT-4501 interlocked to FN-4501 high speed and PAX feeder stop. PAX Xanthate Mixing Tank TK-4501 mixes solid potassium amyl xanthate (PAX, CAS 2720-73-2, C6H11KOS2) with process water to prepare a 20% w/v aqueous collector solution in a 40 m³ 316L stainless steel vessel, and transfers the prepared solution to downstream dosing tank TK-4502. The mixing tank is located inside an enclosed room equipped with dedicated extraction fan FN-4501 running continuously to exhaust toxic and flammable carbon disulfide (CS2) gas generated during xanthate handling and decomposition. Make-up water temperature must strictly remain below 40 °C per RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2 to prevent accelerated hydrolysis into CS2 (LEL 1.3%, auto-ignition 90 °C). Solid PAX is classified as a Self-heating solid per SDS_2720-73-2. Evaluated under HAZOP Study RB-4410-STD-HAZOP-001 Rev 1 Node HZ-45-03, with mandatory recommendation ACT-HAZOP-45-03 requiring installation of CS2 detector AT-4501 interlocked to FN-4501 high speed and PAX feeder stop. PAX Xanthate Mixing Tank TK-4501 mixes solid potassium amyl xanthate (PAX, CAS 2720-73-2, C6H11KOS2) with process water to prepare a 20% w/v aqueous collector solution in a 40 m³ 316L stainless steel vessel, and transfers the prepared solution to downstream dosing tank TK-4502. The mixing tank is located inside an enclosed room equipped with dedicated extraction fan FN-4501 running continuously to exhaust toxic and flammable carbon disulfide (CS2, CAS 75-15-0, flash point -30 °C, auto-ignition 90 °C, LEL 1.3%, TWA 1 ppm skin) evolved during xanthate handling and decomposition per SDS_75-15-0_carbon-disulfide.pdf. Make-up water temperature must strictly remain below 40 °C per RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2 to prevent accelerated hydrolysis into CS2. Evaluated under HAZOP Study RB-4410-STD-HAZOP-001 Rev 1 Node HZ-45-03, with mandatory recommendation ACT-HAZOP-45-03 requiring installation of CS2 detector AT-4501 interlocked to FN-4501 high speed and PAX feeder stop.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- CS2 evolution on decomposition: Potassium amyl xanthate (PAX) solid and aqueous solutions decompose in the presence of moisture and acidic or ambient air conditions to release toxic, flammable carbon disulfide (CS2) and carbon oxysulfide (COS) vapors.
- Enclosed space atmospheric hazard: Enclosed reagent mixing room requires continuous mechanical ventilation via extraction fan FN-4501 to prevent accumulation of lethal and explosive concentrations of CS2 vapor.
- Metallurgical compatibility: 316L stainless steel construction is specified to prevent chemical corrosion and pitting from aqueous xanthate solutions.
- Chemical exposure & spillage: Personnel handling solid PAX addition require chemical PPE and dedicated washdown containment to prevent severe chemical irritation and toxic exposure.
- CS2 evolution on decomposition in enclosed room; extraction fan FN-4501 mandatory.
- Enclosed space atmospheric hazard: Enclosed reagent mixing room requires continuous mechanical ventilation via extraction fan FN-4501 (Note 1 of RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B) to prevent accumulation of lethal and explosive concentrations of CS2 vapor. Loss of fan operation must trigger immediate DCS alarm and evacuation.
- Reagent dosing control: PAX dosing to conditioning tank TK-4101 and rougher cells is governed by Operating Manual RB-4410-OM-001 (Note 4 of RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B) at nominal rate 25 g/t dry ore (67.2 kg/h pure PAX equivalent).
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — Connected Equipment (Downstream): RB-4410-PS-TK4501 Rev B specifies TK-4502, whereas RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B, RB-4410-PS-TK4501 Rev B specifies TK-4502 (Downstream PAX chemical dosing vessel) — verify with engineer before HAZOP
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — Connected Equipment (Ventilation): RB-4410-PS-TK4501 Rev B specifies FN-4501, whereas RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B (Note 1), RB-4410-PS-TK4501 Rev B specifies FN-4501 (Enclosed reagent room continuous extraction fan) — verify with engineer before HAZOP
- CS2 TOXIC & FLAMMABLE ACCUMULATION: PAX hydrolyzes in moisture/acid or above 40 °C, generating toxic and explosive CS2 gas (LEL 1.3%, auto-ignition 90 °C). Keep FN-4501 running whenever PAX is present; never mix with water > 40 °C; use non-sparking tools per RB-4410-OM-001 Sec 8.
- CS2 evolution on decomposition: Potassium amyl xanthate (PAX) solid and aqueous solutions decompose in the presence of moisture and acidic or ambient air conditions to release toxic, flammable carbon disulfide (CS2) and carbon oxysulfide (COS) vapors. Keep FN-4501 running whenever PAX is present; never mix with water > 40 °C.
- HAZOP NODE HZ-45-03 (PAX MIXING ROOM CS2 ACCUMULATION): Wet/hot PAX (>40 °C) or acid contamination causes spontaneous decomposition into CS2 gas, creating toxic and flash fire hazards. Safeguard: Extraction fan FN-4501. Mandatory recommendation ACT-HAZOP-45-03 mandates installing CS2 gas detector AT-4501 (alarm 10 % LEL) interlocked to FN-4501 high speed and PAX feeder stop.
- Chemical exposure & spillage: Personnel handling solid PAX addition require chemical PPE (chemical goggles, nitrile gloves, respirator per SDS) and dedicated washdown containment to prevent severe chemical irritation and toxic exposure. First aid requires 15-minute flush with water.
- FIREFIGHTING DIRECTIVE (SDS_2720-73-2): In case of fire involving xanthate, use dry chemical, CO2, or foam. DO NOT USE WATER JET ON BURNING XANTHATE, as it violently scatters burning chemical and accelerates exothermic CS2 gas evolution.
- CS2 evolution on decomposition: Potassium amyl xanthate (PAX) solid and aqueous solutions decompose in the presence of moisture and acidic or ambient air conditions to release toxic, flammable carbon disulfide (CS2, CAS 75-15-0) and carbon oxysulfide (COS) vapors. Keep FN-4501 running whenever PAX is present; never mix with water > 40 °C.
- CS2 OFF-GAS SAFETY (SDS_75-15-0): Carbon disulfide (CAS 75-15-0) evolved in the TK-4501 room has a flash point of -30 °C, auto-ignition temperature of 90 °C, LEL of 1.3 %, and ACGIH TWA of 1 ppm (skin notation). First aid: 15-minute eye/skin water flush. Firefighting: dry chemical, CO2, foam; DO NOT USE WATER JET ON BURNING XANTHATE.
- FIREFIGHTING DIRECTIVE (SDS_2720-73-2 & SDS_75-15-0): In case of fire involving xanthate, use dry chemical, CO2, or foam. DO NOT USE WATER JET ON BURNING XANTHATE, as it violently scatters burning chemical and accelerates exothermic CS2 gas evolution.
