# X-2301 — Concentration Vacuum Producing Equipment

> ⚠️ Deep vacuum service on CHP-containing vapors. Oxygen analyzer AI-1001 is safety-critical — monitors for explosive atmosphere in vacuum system. Non-condensibles route to Charcoal Adsorber, not direct vent. See [[hazards/cumene-hydroperoxide]].

## System Overview

X-2301 is the complete vacuum producing package for the CDN Concentration sub-section. It maintains the operating vacuum on Preflash Column V-2301 (~18.5 mmHgA) and Flash Column V-2302 (~17 mmHgA).

**Two-stage compression system:**

| Stage | Equipment | Suction | Discharge | Description |
|-------|-----------|---------|-----------|-------------|
| Stage 1 | J-2301, J-2302A/B (Steam Jet Ejectors) | 10 mmHgA | 79.7 mmHgA | Steam jet ejectors, motive steam SC12 |
| Interstage | E-2316 (Inter Condenser) | — | — | Condenses steam from Stage 1 discharge |
| Stage 2 | P-2316A/B (Liquid Ring Vacuum Pumps) | 79.7 mmHgA | 804 mmHgA | Liquid ring pumps compress to near-atmospheric |

The 10 mmHgA suction design point ensures V-2301 and V-2302 can maintain their operating vacuums even under maximum load. The two-stage approach is chosen because:
- Liquid ring pumps alone cannot efficiently achieve 10 mmHgA (impractical)
- Steam ejectors alone require multiple stages with complex condensate handling at very low pressure
- Combined ejector + liquid ring is the UOP standard for phenol plant vacuum service

## Process Flow

```
Non-condensibles from E-2310 (vapor outlet)
↓
J-2301 (First-stage steam jet ejector) — 10 → ~37 mmHgA
↓
E-2316 (Inter-condenser) — removes ejector steam
↓
J-2302A/B (Second-stage ejectors) — → 79.7 mmHgA
↓
P-2316A/B (Liquid ring vacuum pump) — 79.7 → 804 mmHgA
↓
D-2316 (Separator) ─┬─ Gas → TO CHARCOAL ADSORBER COOLDOWN SECTION
                    └─ Liquid → P-2317A/B → E-2317A/B (coolers) → back to P-2316A/B sealant

Ejector condensate (from E-2316 stage) → TO AQUEOUS WASTE CLOSED DRAIN HEADER (91-0051)
Sealant water supply → FROM CHP PROCESS WATER PUMPS AT OXIDATION SECTION
```

**UXV-1001 DCS-only reset (Note 6, Drawing 0010):** The SIS valve UXV-1001 cannot be reset from the field. Similar to UXV-0803 (E-2301 condenser). Operator must be at DCS workstation to reset vacuum system. This prevents accidental restart of the vacuum system without full DCS oversight.

**HXS-23-2316 DCS-only restart (Note 7, Drawing 0010A):** ALL vacuum pumps (P-2316A/B and P-2317A/B) require DCS software hand switch HXS-23-2316 for restart after UC-2303 trip. No field restart capability. Same safety philosophy as UXV-1001 reset.

## AI-1001 Oxygen Analyzer — Safety Significance

The O2 analyzer (vendor-supplied, on D-2316 top) monitors oxygen concentration in the non-condensible vapor stream from the vacuum system. High O2 in the presence of cumene/CHP vapor can form an explosive mixture.

Sources of O2 ingress that AI-1001 detects:
- Air in-leakage through valve packing or flange joints on vacuum equipment (suction side is below atmospheric)
- Process upsets that change vapor composition

Action on high O2 reading: Operator investigation of vacuum system integrity. Sustained high O2 should trigger vacuum system shutdown to prevent explosive atmosphere accumulation.

**Confirmed by Analyzer Process Data Sheet (PS-0003, 2026-06-16)** as **AT-23-1001**: paramagnetic O₂ analyzer, 0–10% O₂ range (±2% accuracy), sample conditioning (regulator → filter/moisture separator → sample pump → analyzer), discharging downstream to the Charcoal Adsorber System (not direct atmospheric vent). Normal sample composition: O₂ 4.97 mol%, N₂ 92.51 mol%, H₂O 2.17 mol%, Cumene 0.31 mol%, trace Acetone/Formic acid. Supplier: Teledyne Analytical Instruments (or equal). Note: this data sheet's own range (0–10%) carries no discrete alarm setpoint — the <5% O₂ target cited above is sourced from the PS-X2301 vendor spec. See [[instruments/analyzers-cdn]].

## Layout Constraints

Two mandatory layout constraints from Drawing 0010:
1. **HORIZONTAL RUNS NOT ALLOWED (45° MINIMUM ANGLE FROM HORIZONTAL)** on vapor condensate lines from E-2310 to X-2301 suction
2. **125m maximum equivalent pipe length** from E-2301/E-2310 to X-2301 suction nozzle (pressure drop constraint for vacuum vapor lines)

## Seal Reservoir Instruments

Each pump (P-2316A, P-2316B, P-2317A, P-2317B) has a dedicated seal reservoir with:
- Level gauges (LG) on drive end and idle end
- Level switch low (LSL) for low seal oil alarm
- Level switch high (LSH) for high seal oil alarm
- Pressure indicators (PI)
