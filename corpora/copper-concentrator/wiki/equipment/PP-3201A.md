# PP-3201A — PP-3201A — Cyclone Feed Slurry Pump (PP-3201A/B duty, PP-3201C standby)

## Function

Centrifugal heavy-duty slurry pump (PP-3201A, operating in 2 duty + 1 standby configuration PP-3201A/B/C) pumping grinding circuit pulp (ball mill ML-3201 discharge + SAG screen SC-3101 product) from the cyclone feed hopper to hydrocyclone cluster CY-3201. Rated at 5,539 m³/h each @ SG 1.618 per P&ID RB-4410-PID-32-001 Rev B and PFD RB-4410-PFD-001 Rev B. Sump/hopper slurry level is monitored by level controller LIC-3201 which regulates pump speed (VFD). Standby pump PP-3201C is identical to duty pumps PP-3201A/B (PID Note 2). Heavy-duty centrifugal slurry pump in secondary grinding classification circuit. Configured as 2 duty + 1 standby (PP-3201A/B/C) with rated capacity of 5,539 m³/h each. Operating procedures in RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2 mandate that PP-3201A and B are started before SAG mill discharge reaches the pump hopper to prevent basement sanding. In the event of hopper sanding or overflow due to pump under-capacity, operators must immediately reduce fresh feed rate on CV-3101 and start standby pump PP-3201C.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- Slurry pump cavitation and wet-end liner/impeller erosion if sump level drops or air entrainment occurs from tumbling mill discharge flumes.
- Pipeline sanding and catastrophic line choking upon sudden pump trip; automated water flush line interlock required to flush riser line within 30 seconds of trip.
- Gland seal water failure leading to severe slurry ingress into drive bearings and mechanical seal failure.
- Discharge pressure deviations: PT-3201 alerts operators to pump cavitation or line leakage at PAL 90 kPa, and warns of downstream manifold choking or roping at PAH 140 kPa.
- Slurry rheology and density excursions: High slurry density (>1.65 SG on DT-3201) dramatically escalates pump casing wear and triggers cyclone roping; controlled via sump dilution water.
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — CYCLONE FEED PUMP RATED FLOW: Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-PP3201 Rev B specifies a rated slurry flow rate of 4,650 m³/h per pump, whereas Process Flow Diagram RB-4410-PFD-001 Rev B (Stream S-322) specifies an operating flow rate of 5,539 m³/h per operating duty pump (total feed 11,078 m³/h split across 2 duty pumps PP-3201A/B) — verify pump hydraulic capacity, impeller sizing curve, and operating point with lead process engineer before HAZOP.
- High-chrome white iron wet-end liner and impeller subject to severe erosive wear under high-velocity abrasive slurry flow (60.0 wt% solids, SG 1.618); regular ultrasonic wall thickness monitoring required.
- Casing design pressure is 10 bar(g) against normal dynamic head of 32 m (~5.08 bar with SG 1.618 slurry); pressure containment margin must withstand transient water hammer and hydraulic shock upon emergency pump trip.
- Gland seal water failure leading to severe slurry ingress into drive bearings and mechanical packing failure.
- Motor overload (1,600 kW motor rating) risk if slurry density exceeds 1.618 SG or during circuit startup/upset with high circulating load.
- Slurry pump cavitation and wet-end liner/impeller erosion if sump level drops.
- Pipeline sanding and line choking upon sudden pump trip; automated water flush required.
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — CYCLONE FEED PUMP RATED FLOW: Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-PP3201 Rev B specifies a rated slurry flow rate of 4,650 m³/h per pump, whereas Process Flow Diagram RB-4410-PFD-001 Rev B (Stream S-322) and Piping & Instrumentation Diagram RB-4410-PID-32-001 Rev B specify a rated slurry flow rate of 5,539 m³/h each @ SG 1.618 (total feed 11,078 m³/h split across 2 duty pumps PP-3201A/B) — verify pump hydraulic capacity, impeller sizing curve, and operating point with lead process engineer before HAZOP.
- Cyclone feed hopper level control failure: Malfunction of level controller LIC-3201 or variable speed drive (VFD) hunting can cause hopper slurry overflow onto the grinding floor or pump cavitation and severe suction line air entrainment if the hopper runs dry.
- Standby pump interchangeability: Standby pump PP-3201C is identical to PP-3201A/B per Note 2 of RB-4410-PID-32-001 Rev B; switching pumps under load requires automated sequencing of suction/discharge knife gate valves and gland seal water supply.
- SUMP OVERFLOW & HOPPER SANDING: If duty pumps cannot handle recirculating load, immediately cut fresh ore feed on CV-3101 and start standby pump PP-3201C per RB-4410-OM-001 Sec 7. Start PP-3201A/B before mill discharge reaches hopper during startup.
