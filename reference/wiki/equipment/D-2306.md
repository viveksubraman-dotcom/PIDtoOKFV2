# D-2306 — Acid Aromatics Knockout Drum

> ⚠️ CHP is a peroxide — thermal decomposition risk. See [[hazards/cumene-hydroperoxide]].

## Function

D-2306 is a vapor/liquid separator that collects and separates all relief and vent streams from the CDN Decomposition section and the pressure relief system (Drawing 0022). Liquid acid aromatics fall out and drain to the Acid Aromatics Sump [[equipment/D-2307]]. The gas/vapor phase exits to the PRU (Phenol Recovery Unit) relief header. D-2306 is the gateway between all CDN relief events and the safe downstream disposal path.

**Two design temperature limits:** 325°C (fire/emergency case — governing) and 250°C (normal). Operating at near-atmospheric conditions (0.01 kg/cm²g). Full vacuum design (FV) covers condensate jacketing events.

**Internal:** Distributor + Baffle for liquid/vapor separation.

> **NOTE — Vapor nozzles revised (DS Rev Z1):** Vapor inlet nozzle (BJ) and vapor outlet nozzle (AO) revised from 12" to 14" diameter per DS record of revision. Reason: flare network back-pressure requirement necessitated larger nozzle size. Verify this revision is reflected in as-built P&ID Dwg 0020.

## Relief Header Notes (Drawing 0022)

The Pressure Relief Header (Drawing 0022) collects PSV discharges from:
- X-2312 (Dehydrator Reactor Loop): PSV-1403A/B/C
- E-2308A/B (Dehydrators tube side): PSV-1401A/B
- Dehydrator product line: PSV-1405A/B
- X-2309A (Calorimeter No.1): PSV-1801A/B
- X-2309B (Calorimeter No.2): PSV-1802A/B

The header has a **continuous N₂ purge** via RO-2201 (1.5mm restriction orifice) + SRY-L2301 purge skid to maintain inert atmosphere and prevent air ingress into the CHP/acid service relief header. All relief discharges to D-2306.

## Downstream Flow Path

```
CDN equipment reliefs/vents
    → D-2306 (KO Drum) — liquid knockout
        ├─ Liquid → D-2307 (Acid Aromatics Sump) → P-2304A → Sodium Phenate Tank (Phenol Recovery)
        └─ Vapor → PRU Relief Header (Phenol Recovery Section)
```
