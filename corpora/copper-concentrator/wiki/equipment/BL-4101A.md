# BL-4101A — Rougher Flotation Air Blowers (BL-4101A/B)

## Function

Low-pressure centrifugal air blowers configured in duty/standby service (BL-4101A/B) supplying continuous forced aeration air to the rougher flotation tank cells (FC-4101 to FC-4107) in Area 41. Air is delivered through a common header regulated by flow indicating controller FIC-4102 to maintain superficial air velocity between 0.8 and 1.6 m/s across the 7 x 300 m3 cells, sustaining selective bubble-particle attachment and mineralized froth formation for copper-gold flotation.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- Blower trip and loss of aeration air: Immediate collapse of mineral froth across the rougher bank, resulting in catastrophic loss of copper recovery into final tailings Stream S-611 discharging to TH-6101.
- Over-aeration and cell boiling: Uncontrolled high airflow (>1.6 m/s superficial air velocity) destroys quiescent froth bed, entraining non-mineralized gangue into rougher concentrate S-411.
- Standby blower cut-in failure: Delay or failure of automatic changeover from duty BL-4101A to standby BL-4101B causes rapid pulp de-aeration and sanding around rotor-stator air ports.
