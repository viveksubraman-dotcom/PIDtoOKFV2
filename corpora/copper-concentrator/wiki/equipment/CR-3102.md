# CR-3102 — Pebble Crusher

## Function

Cone crusher (extra-coarse) operating in the SAG mill closed circuit. Receives critical-size pebbles (typically 25–65 mm) screened from the SAG mill ML-3101 discharge via vibrating pebble screen SC-3101, crushes them down to a 12 mm closed side setting (CSS), and discharges onto conveyor CV-3101 via recycle Stream S-305 (484 t/h dry solids) returning to the SAG mill feed to maximize comminution efficiency and throughput. Registered in Mechanical Equipment List RB-4410-PID-00-002 Rev B. Extra-coarse cone crusher operating in closed circuit with SAG discharge screen SC-3101 in Area 31. Driven by a 750 kW electric motor with a design throughput of 484 t/h. Reduces critical-size pebbles to 12 mm CSS before recycling via Stream S-305 to SAG mill feed conveyor CV-3101 per RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- Tramp metal and broken SAG grinding ball ingestion: Ejected SAG grinding media (up to 125 mm steel balls) or broken mill liner components passing over pebble screen SC-3101 can enter the crushing chamber, risking catastrophic bowl/mantle liner fracture, main shaft bending, or hydraulic tramp release cylinder failure. An upstream self-cleaning cross-belt magnet and metal detector must be installed and interlocked with the feed conveyor.
- Crusher cavity choking and motor stall: Feed surges exceeding 484 t/h design capacity or severe moisture/fines accumulation can pack the crushing cavity, triggering motor overload on the 750 kW drive. Cavity level monitoring and feed conveyor speed modulation are required.
- Lubrication and hydraulic pressure loss: Loss of forced lubrication to eccentric bronze bushings or loss of hydraulic clamping/tramp relief pressure will cause rapid bearing seizure or mantle unseating under load. Low lube oil flow, low lube pressure, high lube temperature, and low hydraulic accumulator pressure must execute an executive trip of the 750 kW drive motor.
- Respirable mineral dust release: High-velocity dry pebble transfer and impact crushing generate airborne silica/mineral dust. Dust containment skirting and operational water spray suppression systems must be verified active prior to crusher operation.
- Tramp metal and broken SAG grinding ball ingestion risking bowl/mantle liner fracture.
- Crusher cavity choking and motor overload on 750 kW drive.
- TRAMP METAL & MEDIA DAMAGE: Broken SAG grinding media (>100 mm steel balls) entering crushing chamber can fracture mantle or stall 750 kW motor; self-cleaning tramp magnet and metal detector mandatory per RB-4410-OM-001 Sec 2.
