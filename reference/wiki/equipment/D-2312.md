# D-2312 — Diamine Injection Tank

> ⛔ **CHEMICAL IDENTITY CONFLICT — RESOLVE BEFORE HAZOP TOUCHES THIS NODE.** AS-BUILT Process Data Sheet (PS-D2312 Rev Z1, an authoritative engineering document) states the fluid name as **"DIAMINE(HMDA)"** — i.e. **Hexamethylenediamine, CAS 124-09-4**, density 914 kg/m³, Amine service = YES. This directly conflicts with [[hazards/diamine-tbc]], which "confirmed" the additive as **DIPA (Diisopropanolamine, CAS 110-97-4)** based on an SDS whose own filename contains "placeholder" (`SDS_110-97-4_dipa-placeholder-diamine-tbc.pdf`). Per the Standards Primacy Rule, the as-built data sheet is the higher-confidence source — DIPA's density (~1010 kg/m³) does not match the DS density (914 kg/m³), while HMDA (liquid density ~890–900 kg/m³ near melting, miscible) is closer. **A genuine plant-specific SDS for HMDA must be obtained before this hazard page or any HAZOP deviation analysis on D-2312 / P-2306A/B / X-2310A/B is finalized.**

## Function

D-2312 is the working tank supplying **diamine** (neutralizing agent) to the two Neutralizing Agent Injection Pumps [[equipment/P-2306AB]]. Diamine is injected into the crude product stream via the Direct Neutralization Static Mixers [[equipment/X-2310AB]] to neutralize residual sulfuric acid catalyst carried over from the Decomposer section before the crude product is sent to Fractionation.

**Neutralizing agent:** Diamine — confirms the CDN process uses amine-based neutralization (not NaOH). This prevents formation of sodium phenate salts that would contaminate the product.

## Connections

- **Supply:** From Chemical Treatment Injection Drum at Phenol Recovery Section (Diamine tote: 2"-DA-23-019015-B1A2-NI)
- **Discharge:** To [[equipment/P-2306AB]] (Diamine Injection Pumps) via 1¼" DA lines
- **Drain:** To [[equipment/X-2320]] (Acid Neutralization System, Drawing 23-0015): ¾"-DA-23-019021-B1A2-NI and ¾"-DA-23-019013-B1A2-NI
- **Vent:** Via PCV-1909 (30 mm H₂O) to vent system; utility N₂ blanket (¾"-PL-25-006011-N2A1-ET)

**Piping spec DA (B1A2):** Diamine/amine service piping specification — different from L1A1 (CHP acid lines) and SA (sulfuric acid lines).
