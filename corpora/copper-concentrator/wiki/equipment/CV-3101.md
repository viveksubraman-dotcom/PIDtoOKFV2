# CV-3101 — SAG Mill Feed Conveyor

## Function

SAG mill feed conveyor transferring fresh crushed ore (Stream S-301) from stockpile reclaim feeders FE-3101A/B/C and recycled crushed pebbles from Pebble Crusher CR-3102 directly into the feed chute of SAG Mill ML-3101. Operates at nominal solids rate of 3,172 t/h (2,688 t/h fresh ore solids + 484 t/h pebble recycle solids). Integrated with weight indicating controller WIC-3101 to regulate mill feed rate. Governed by Safety Instrumented Function SIF-3101 (SIL 1) per RB-4410-STD-CE-001 Rev B, which automatically stops CV-3101 on SAG trunnion bearing high-high temperature trip (TAHH 85 °C, 1oo2 voting on TT-3101/TT-3102) simultaneously with ML-3101 GMD to prevent continuous ore surge packing in the SAG mill feed chute.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- Conveyor nip points, drive pulleys, and rotating idlers create severe entanglement and crushing hazards; emergency pull-wire stop switches along both walkways are mandatory.
- Chute plugging at SAG mill feed chute causes ore spillage, belt slippage, and friction fire risk; chute tilt and high-level plug switches required.
- Tonnage surge and belt overload hazard from simultaneous surge of fresh ore and recycled pebbles exceeding conveyor carrying capacity.
- Respirable crystalline silica dust emissions during transfer into SAG mill feed chute; dust suppression sprays or extraction required.
- Mill Feed Chute Slurry Overfill & Conveyor Overcharging: Feed conveyor CV-3101 is interlocked to SIF-3101 (SIL 1) per RB-4410-STD-CE-001 Rev B, which automatically stops CV-3101 upon SAG trunnion bearing high-high temperature trip (TAHH 85 °C, 1oo2 voting on TT-3101/TT-3102). Failure of CV-3101 to trip simultaneously with ML-3101 GMD results in continuous charging of up to 3,172 t/h solids into a stationary SAG mill feed chute, causing catastrophic chute plugging and conveyor belt over-tonnage stalling.
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — SAG Trunnion Bearing Trip Setpoint Tripping CV-3101: RB-4410-STD-CE-001 Rev B specifies CV-3101 trip at TAHH 85 °C (1oo2 voting), whereas RB-4410-PS-0034 Rev B and RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2 document mill bearing trip at TAHH 75 °C without explicit reference to CV-3101 feed trip — verify interlock loop wiring before HAZOP.
