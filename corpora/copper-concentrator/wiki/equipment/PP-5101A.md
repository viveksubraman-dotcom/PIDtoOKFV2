# PP-5101A — Concentrate Thickener Underflow Pump (PP-5101A/B)

## Function

Centrifugal heavy-duty slurry pump operating in duty/standby configuration (PP-5101A/B) transferring dense thickened copper concentrate slurry (Stream S-511) from the underflow cone of concentrate thickener TH-5101 to the concentrate filter press FP-5101 feed tank / manifold for cyclic batch pressure dewatering. Operates under positive static gravity suction head from the thickener cone. Centrifugal slurry underflow transfer pump operating in 1 duty + 1 standby configuration (PP-5101A duty, PP-5101B standby) in Area 51. Transfers thickened copper concentrate (Stream S-511 at 65.0 wt% solids) from thickener TH-5101 underflow to filter press FP-5101. Registered in Mechanical Equipment List RB-4410-PID-00-002 Rev B. Centrifugal heavy-duty slurry pump operating in duty/standby configuration (PP-5101A duty, PP-5101B standby) transferring dense thickened copper concentrate slurry (Filter feed / Stream S-511 at 65.0 wt% solids) from the underflow cone of concentrate thickener TH-5101 to the concentrate filter press FP-5101 feed manifold for cyclic batch pressure dewatering per RB-4410-PID-51-001 Rev B. Operates under positive static gravity suction head from the thickener cone with discharge monitored by density transmitter DT-5101. Centrifugal slurry pump in Area 51 operating in 1 duty + 1 standby configuration (PP-5101A/B) transferring thickened copper concentrate underflow (65 % solids w/w, SG 2.0) from thickener TH-5101 to vertical filter press FP-5101. Rated at 210 m³/h and 28 m TDH per RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2 and PS-PP5101 Rev B. Continuous operating flow is 40 m³/h.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- ⚠️ CONFLICT — RATED FLOW / OPERATING FLOW: Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-PP5101 Rev B specifies a rated slurry flow of 210 m³/h (increased from 180 m³/h in Rev A after filter trade-off; supersedes Rev A), whereas Process Flow Diagram RB-4410-PFD-003 Rev B (Stream S-511) specifies a normal operating slurry flow of 40 m³/h (51 t/h solids, 27 t/h water at 65.0 wt% solids and SG 1.966). Operating a 210 m³/h rated pump at 40 m³/h represents severe flow throttling (19.0% of best efficiency point), risking severe internal recirculation, cavitation, hydraulic pulsation, accelerated impeller/casing wear, and slurry settling/sanding in the pump volute. Verify pump hydraulic sizing curve, variable speed drive (VSD) operating envelope, and minimum continuous flow recirculation line before HAZOP.
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — SLURRY SPECIFIC GRAVITY: Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-PP5101 Rev B specifies Slurry SG 2.0, whereas Drawing RB-4410-PFD-003 Rev B specifies SG 1.966 (at 65.0 wt% solids) — verify slurry rheological characterization and Bingham plastic yield stress before HAZOP closure.
- Slurry settling & suction line compaction: Thickened concentrate at 65.0 wt% solids (SG 1.966–2.0) has high yield stress; sudden trip of PP-5101A/B without immediate clean flush water causes rapid settling and line blockage between the thickener cone and pump suction.
- Gland seal water starvation & abrasive slurry intrusion: Dense chalcopyrite concentrate slurry is extremely abrasive; gland seal water loss leads to rapid shaft sleeve scoring and seal destruction.
- High dynamic head and motor load: 75 kW installed motor must handle 28 m TDH on high-density 2.0 SG slurry; motor overload can occur if underflow density spikes above 65 wt% solids during bed compaction upsets in TH-5101.
- Severe casing/impeller wear and pump cavitation under throttled flow conditions (40 m3/h normal vs 210 m3/h rated).
- Underflow density monitoring and line settling (DT-5101): Inline density transmitter DT-5101 monitors underflow slurry density targeting 65 % w/w solids. High density (>68% solids) causes severe rheological yield stress spikes risking pump cavitation, suction line sanding, and line blockage; low density (<60% solids) increases filter press cycle time and filtration volume.
- VOLUTE WEAR & LINE SANDING: Operating at throttled flow (40 m3/h vs 210 m3/h rated) risks internal recirculation, volute wear, and suction line sanding; variable speed drive or recirculation loop required per RB-4410-OM-001 Sec 10.
