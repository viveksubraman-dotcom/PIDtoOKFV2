# TK-4511 — Frother Storage Tank

## Function

Frother Storage Tank TK-4511 stores 30 m³ of liquid frother reagent in Area 45 within a dedicated containment bund and feeds frother dosing pump PP-4511A to supply flotation cells per operating manual RB-4410-OM-001. A significant cross-document discrepancy exists regarding reagent chemistry: P&ID RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B specifies Polyglycol ether frother (DF-250 type), whereas Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-TK4511 Rev B specifies MIBC (methyl isobutyl carbinol, flash point 41 °C, Zone 2 flammable liquid). Area classification is governed by RB-4410-STD-HAC-001 per drawing Note 2. Liquid level is monitored by level indicator LI-4511. Registered in Mechanical Equipment List RB-4410-PID-00-002 Rev B and P&ID RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B. Atmospheric 30 m³ vertical storage tank in Area 45 providing bulk storage and dosing of pure methyl isobutyl carbinol (MIBC) frother. Operating Manual RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2 confirms MIBC specification with a 41 °C flash point and Zone 2 hazardous area classification. Doses at 20 g/t dry ore to stabilize bubble size and froth mechanics in rougher cells FC-4101 and cleaner column FC-4301. Frother Storage Tank TK-4511 stores 30 m³ of liquid frother reagent in Area 45 within a dedicated containment bund and feeds frother dosing pump PP-4511A to supply flotation cells per operating manual RB-4410-OM-001. Evaluated under HAZOP Study RB-4410-STD-HAZOP-001 Rev 1 Node HZ-45-06 (wrong reagent deviation), which issues mandatory recommendation ACT-HAZOP-45-06 to confirm frother identity on P&ID (DF-250) vs data sheet (MIBC) and SDS to ensure hazardous-area classification (Zone 2) remains valid. Frother Storage Tank TK-4511 stores 30 m³ of liquid frother reagent in Area 45 within a dedicated containment bund and feeds frother dosing pump PP-4511A to supply flotation cells per operating manual RB-4410-OM-001. A significant cross-document discrepancy exists regarding reagent chemistry: P&ID RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B specifies Polyglycol ether frother (DF-250 type), whereas Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-TK4511 Rev B, Operating Manual RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2, and Safety Data Sheet SDS_108-11-2_mibc.pdf specify MIBC (methyl isobutyl carbinol, CAS 108-11-2, formula C6H14O, flash point 41 °C, Zone 2 flammable liquid, STOT SE 3). Area classification is governed by RB-4410-STD-HAC-001 per drawing Note 2. Liquid level is monitored by level indicator LI-4511. Evaluated under HAZOP Study RB-4410-STD-HAZOP-001 Rev 1 Node HZ-45-06 (wrong reagent deviation), which issues mandatory recommendation ACT-HAZOP-45-06 to confirm frother identity on P&ID (DF-250) vs data sheet (MIBC) and SDS to ensure hazardous-area classification (Zone 2) remains valid. Atmospheric 30 m³ vertical storage tank in Area 45 providing bulk storage and dosing of frother reagent within a dedicated concrete containment bund to feed frother dosing pump PP-4511A and supply rougher flotation cells FC-4101 and cleaner column FC-4301. ## Hazards & Safeguards

- Flammable liquid Class 3 hazard: MIBC has a closed-cup flash point of 41 °C. In ambient summer temperatures or under direct solar exposure, tank liquid vapor space enters the flammable envelope.
- ⚠️ CONFLICT — HAZARDOUS AREA CLASSIFICATION: Process Data Sheet RB-4410-PS-TK4511 Rev B designates the tank installation as Zone 2 (flammable liquid, Class 3), whereas preliminary general safety notes referenced Class I Div 1 / Zone 1 — verify electrical equipment ratings with safety/electrical engineer before HAZOP.
- Electrostatic charge generation: Unloading and recirculation of non-conductive flammable organic liquids generate static electric potential. Tank, loading piping, and tanker bonding must be continuous and verified prior to liquid transfer.
- Toxic vapor inhalation & eye irritation: MIBC vapor is an acute irritant to eyes, skin, and respiratory tract. Overexposure causes headaches, dizziness, and central nervous system depression. Breather vents must be routed away from personnel access.
- Flammability hazard; flash point 41 °C; classified Zone 2 per RB-4410-PS-TK4511 Rev B.
