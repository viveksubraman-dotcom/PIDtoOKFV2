# X-2320 — Acid Neutralization System

## Function

X-2320 collects and neutralizes all acid-contaminated drains from the Decomposer Acid Injection system — including spills, flush drains, and free-draining connections from the acid tanks (D-2310, D-2311) and acid injection pumps (P-2305A–F). Neutralized effluent is routed to the oily water sewer.

## Connections

- **Inlets:**
  - Drain from [[equipment/D-2310]] (Acid Injection Tank)
  - Drain from [[equipment/D-2311]] (Acid Refill Tank)
  - Free-draining connections from [[equipment/P-2305ABCDEF]] (P-2305A/B SRY drains, pump drains)
  - Amine neutralizing agent feed (from Drawing 23-0019, line: 1"-SA-23-015017-L1A1-NI)
- **Outlet:** To oily water sewer (91-0058)
- **Location:** Diked area below acid injection system; gravity collection

## Notes

X-2320 is located within a diked containment area (visible on Drawing 0015 — "DIKE" and "GRATING" callout). The dike ensures any spill or leak from the acid system is captured and directed to X-2320 rather than escaping to grade.
