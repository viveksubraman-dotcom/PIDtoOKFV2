# FC-4101 — Rougher Flotation Tank Cells (FC-4101 to FC-4107)

## Function

Rougher flotation of cyclone overflow (Stream S-324) across a bank of 7 x 300 m3 forced-air tank cells (tagged FC-4101 to FC-4107) in Area 41 - Rougher Flotation. The cell bank receives conditioned copper ore slurry from conditioning tank TK-4101 at 2,688 t/h solids and 5,969 m3/h slurry (35.0 wt% solids). Utilizes rotor-stator air dispersion with 0.8–1.6 m/s superficial air velocity, 7 x 250 kW agitator drive mechanisms, collector PAX (25 g/t), frother MIBC (20 g/t), and milk-of-lime regulated to pH 10.5 by analytical indicating controller AIC-4101 (AL 10.0 / AH 11.2) to selectively float copper-gold bearing sulfide minerals over a 32-minute residence time. Discharges rougher concentrate (Stream S-411: 215 t/h solids, 580 m3/h slurry @ 30.0 wt% solids) to regrind mill ML-4201 and final rougher tailings (Stream S-611: 2,637 t/h solids, 6,314 m3/h slurry @ 33.0 wt% solids) to tailings thickener TH-6101. Bank of seven 300 m3 forced-air mechanical flotation tank cells (FC-4101 through FC-4107) performing bulk rougher and scavenger flotation on conditioned cyclone overflow slurry. Discharges rougher concentrate to regrind mill ML-4201 and final tailings to thickener TH-6101. Registered in Mechanical Equipment List RB-4410-PID-00-002 Rev B. Rougher flotation of cyclone overflow (Stream S-324) across a bank of 7 x 300 m3 forced-air tank cells (tagged FC-4101 to FC-4107) in Area 41 - Rougher Flotation. The cell bank receives conditioned copper ore slurry from conditioning tank TK-4101 at 2,688 t/h solids and 5,969 m3/h slurry (35.0 wt% solids). Aeration air is supplied by blowers BL-4101A/B and regulated under flow control loop FIC-4102 to maintain 0.8–1.6 m/s superficial air velocity. Driven by 7 x 250 kW agitator drive mechanisms (1,750 kW total). Slurry level in each cell is individually regulated by modulating dart valves (typical across all seven cells per Note 3 of RB-4410-PID-41-001 Rev B). Collector PAX (25 g/t) and frother MIBC (20 g/t) are added at distribution points per RB-4410-PID-45-001 (Note 2), with milk-of-lime regulated to pH 10.5 by analytical indicating controller AIC-4101 (AL 10.0 / AH 11.2 per Note 1) to selectively float copper-gold bearing sulfide minerals over a 32-minute residence time. Discharges rougher concentrate (Stream S-411: 215 t/h solids, 580 m3/h slurry @ 30.0 wt% solids) to regrind mill ML-4201 and final rougher tailings (Stream S-611: 2,637 t/h solids, 6,314 m3/h slurry @ 33.0 wt% solids) to tailings thickener TH-6101. Bank of seven 300 m³ forced-air mechanical tank cells (FC-4101 through FC-4107) performing bulk rougher flotation on conditioned cyclone overflow slurry (Stream S-324). Produces rougher concentrate (Stream S-411) sent to regrind mill ML-4201 and tailings (Stream S-611) sent to tailings thickener TH-6101. Operating targets in RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2 specify feed pH of 10.5, 32-minute residence time, 0.8–1.6 m/s superficial air rate, 89% copper recovery, and 70% gold recovery. Reagent dosing rates: PAX 25 g/t, frother 20 g/t, lime to pH 10.5.

## Hazards & Safeguards

- Pulp level fluctuation causing either froth collapse (loss of recovery) or gross slurry entrainment into concentrate launders (severe concentrate grade dilution).
- Rotor drive stoppage leading to immediate sanding and packing of coarse solids around mechanism; restart without high-pressure water lancing risks motor burnout or shaft shearing.
- Tailings launder overflow hazard during downstream thickener trips; 6,314 m3/h slurry flow will rapidly overwhelm containment sumps if diversion to emergency pond fails.
- Tailings launder overflow hazard during downstream thickener TH-6101 trips; 6,314 m3/h slurry flow will rapidly overwhelm containment sumps if diversion to emergency pond fails.
- Pulp alkalinity excursions: Operating below AL 10.0 causes heavy pyrite flotation and loss of concentrate grade; operating above AH 11.2 depresses copper recovery and fouls cells with calcium carbonate scale (monitored by AIC-4101).
- Rotor drive stoppage across the 7 x 250 kW agitators leading to immediate sanding and packing of coarse solids (+150 µm) around the mechanism in the 300 m3 tank cells; restart without high-pressure water lancing risks motor burnout or shaft shearing.
- Air dispersion boundary excursion: Superficial air velocity below 0.8 m/s reduces bubble surface area flux and impairs recovery; air velocity above 1.6 m/s causes bubble coalescence, excessive froth turbulence, cell boiling, and gangue entrainment into rougher concentrate Stream S-411.
- Residence time degradation: Surges in volumetric slurry feed above 5,969 m3/h reduce effective cell residence time below the 32 min design threshold, increasing unfloated copper sulfide losses to tailings Stream S-611.
- Rapid cell sanding and rotor seizure upon power blackout or motor trip.
- Air dispersion collapse if superficial velocity deviates from 0.8–1.6 m/s.
- Dart valve jamming or mechanical sticking (PID Note 3): Slurry solids settling, sanding, or scale accumulation in dart valve seats causes sudden uncontrolled pulp level surges, resulting in concentrate launder flooding or froth collapse.
- Aeration air supply failure or blower trip (BL-4101A/B / FIC-4102): Loss of forced air immediately collapses mineralized froth across all 7 cells, causing complete loss of copper recovery with valuable metal discharging to tailings thickener TH-6101.
- Excessive aeration velocity (>1.6 m/s superficial air velocity): High air delivery causes violent cell boiling, destroying quiescent froth zone and entraining non-sulfide gangue into rougher concentrate S-411.
- Pulp alkalinity excursions: Operating below AL 10.0 causes heavy pyrite flotation and loss of concentrate grade; operating above AH 11.2 depresses copper recovery and fouls cells with calcium carbonate scale (monitored by AIC-4101, SP 10.5 per PID Note 1).
- ROUGHER RECOVERY COLLAPSE: Coarse cyclone overflow (+150 µm from cyclone roping) or blower aeration failure (<0.8 m/s) causes immediate collapse of mineralized froth and unrecoverable chalcopyrite loss to tailings thickener TH-6101 per RB-4410-OM-001 Sec 4 & 9.
