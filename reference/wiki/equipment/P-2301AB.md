# P-2301A/B — Flash Column Bottoms Pumps

> ⚠️ Concentrated CHP service (~80-85 wt% CHP). Highest CHP concentration in the Concentration sub-section. DM-CHP seal, CHP Closed Drain Header (STD DWG 8-137). On Reliable Power Supply (emergency bus). See [[hazards/cumene-hydroperoxide]].

*105 m³/hr capacity handles the ~60,873 kg/h concentrated CHP flow (≈62.6 m³/hr at 0.972 SG) with ~68% design margin — significant surge capacity. "Pumps to be on Reliable Power Supply" is explicitly stated on drawing — P-2301A/B are the ONLY concentration section pumps on the emergency power bus. UOP considers loss of this pump to be a safety-significant event.*

## Process Role

P-2301A/B pump the concentrated CHP bottoms from Flash Column V-2302 (via E-2306 Flash Column Bottoms Cooler) to the Decomposer feed system. This is the critical handoff point between the Concentration and Decomposition sub-sections.

**Main discharge:** 6"-CHP-23-009002/003-L1A1-NI → TO DECOMPOSER DRUM area (P&ID 0012, Decomposer Feed system including D-2302 Cumene Flush Drum and D-2303 Decomposer Feed Flush Drum).

This ensures adequate pipe volume for CHP concentration stabilization before entering the Decomposer feed control zone.

## Reliable Power Supply — Engineering Significance

P-2301A/B are explicitly noted "PUMPS TO BE ON RELIABLE POWER SUPPLY" — the only Concentration sub-section pumps on the emergency bus.

**Why this matters:** If P-2301A/B fail and no standby is available:
- V-2302 Flash Column bottoms continue to accumulate (LXSHH-0802 triggers UC-2301 ESD when level HH is reached)
- Concentrated CHP with no outlet path → temperature rise in column sump
- Decomposer starved of feed → temperature falls → TXSLL-1301A/B triggers UC-2302 ESD (decomposer low-temp shutdown, SIL 2)
- The emergency power connection ensures that even during a plant-wide power failure, these pumps can continue to move concentrated CHP away from the column

Type B motor control (no hardwired auto-start) means the operator must manually start the standby pump on running pump failure — consistent with the more deliberate operational control required for concentrated CHP service.

## Drain and Seal System

Per STD DWG 8-137 and Note 4:
- Seal Oil Drain: P-2301A and P-2301B → 23-0009 (designated drain)
- Case Drain: P-2301A and P-2301B → 23-0009
- Low-point drains on suction lines per STD DWG 8-137-7 (duplicated connection per Note 4)
- CHP Closed Drain Header connection required

All drains and seal flushes must be routed to the designated CHP drain system — never to general sewer. Cumene flush lines from header 22-0027 serve as seal supply.
