> ⚠️ **CRITICAL PROCESS SAFETY / DISCREPANCY WARNING:**
> - **MANDATORY HAZOP STUDY RECOMMENDATIONS (RB-4410-STD-HAZOP-001 Rev 1):** Recommendations from the Concentrator HAZOP review are **mandatory unless formally closed** through documented engineering MOC.
> - **HAZOP NODE HZ-31-02 (PSV-3105 SET PRESSURE CONFIRMATION):** HP lift pump deadheading against a closed valve risks high-pressure oil line rupture and fire at the SAG mill. HAZOP mandates confirming that PSV-3105 set pressure on P&ID matches data sheet. P&ID `RB-4410-PID-31-002 Rev B` specifies `SET 180 bar(g)` (violating 170 bar(g) system design pressure and Note 1), whereas Data Sheet `RB-4410-PS-0032 Rev B` and Operating Manual `RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2` corroborate `SET 160 bar(g)`. P&ID redline to 160 bar(g) is required.
> - **HAZOP NODE HZ-31-04 (SAG TRUNNION BEARING TRIP TAHH ALIGNMENT):** Loss of lube oil flow or cooling risks bearing wipe and a 10–14 day unplanned outage. HAZOP mandates aligning the TAHH setpoint between instrument data sheet (`RB-4410-PS-0034 Rev B`: 75 °C) and SIS Cause & Effect matrix (`RB-4410-STD-CE-001 Rev B`: 85 °C, 1oo2 voting).
> - **HAZOP NODE HZ-45-03 (NEW INSTRUMENT AT-4501 / FN-4501 INTERLOCK):** To protect against toxic and flammable $CS_2$ accumulation from wet/hot PAX or acid contamination, HAZOP mandates installing continuous $CS_2$ infrared gas detector `AT-4501` (alarm at 10% LEL) interlocked to switch extraction fan `FN-4501` to high speed and stop solid PAX feeding.
> - **HAZOP NODE HZ-45-06 (FROTHER REAGENT IDENTIFICATION ON P&ID VS DATA SHEET & SDS):** HAZOP mandates confirming frother reagent identity on P&ID vs data sheet and SDS to prevent invalidating Zone 2 hazardous-area classification (P&ID `RB-4410-PID-45-001 Rev B` specifies DF-250 polyglycol ether vs Data Sheet `RB-4410-PS-TK4511 Rev B` specifying MIBC, flash point 41 °C).
> - **HAZOP NODE HZ-61-01 (TAILINGS CASING DESIGN PRESSURE CONFIRMATION):** HAZOP mandates confirming stage-3 casing design pressure on P&ID. P&ID `RB-4410-PID-61-001 Rev B` specifies 4.0 bar(g) (typographical decimal error against 34 bar(g) normal discharge and 38 bar(g) PAHH trip), whereas Process Data Sheet `RB-4410-PS-PP6101 Rev B` specifies 40 bar(g).
> - **⚠️ CONFLICT — SAG TRUNNION BEARING TEMPERATURE TRIP SETPOINT (SIF-3101):** The controlling SIS Cause & Effect Matrix `RB-4410-STD-CE-001 Rev B` explicitly specifies the executive trip set point for `SIF-3101` as **TAHH 85 °C** with **1oo2 voting** on duplex temperature transmitters `TT-3101` and `TT-3102`, tripping both the SAG mill Gearless Motor Drive (`ML-3101 GMD`) and SAG feed conveyor `CV-3101`. In contrast, Temperature Instrument Process Data Sheet `RB-4410-PS-0034 Rev B` and Concentrator Operating Manual `RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2` (Section 12) specify the trip set point as **TAHH 75 °C** (pre-alarm `TAH 70 °C`). An 85 °C setting provides a 10 °C higher operating threshold before tripping, but approaches the thermal softening point of white metal Babbitt bearing linings under the 2,450 t mill charge load. This discrepancy must be formally evaluated in the upcoming HAZOP / LOPA review and reconciled with mechanical bearing vendor limits.
> - **HYDROSTATIC LIFT SYSTEM DEPRESSURIZATION (PT-3104 BLEED PER RB-4410-SG-005 Rev 3):** Before any maintenance personnel enter the SAG mill `ML-3101` or Ball Mill `ML-3201` drum, the high-pressure hydrostatic lift and lube systems must be isolated, and lift oil pressure transmitter `PT-3104` must be manually and instrumentally bled down to **0 bar(g)**. Trapped hydraulic pressure poses severe injection and unplanned rotational jacking hazards.
> - **⚠️ CONFLICT — CYCLONE FEED DENSITY (DT-3201 / CY-3201):** Operating Manual `RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2` (Table 3.32 and Section 4) explicitly sets the design cyclone feed density to **65 % solids w/w** (regulated by `DT-3201` via sump dilution water), whereas Process Data Sheet `RB-4410-PS-CY3201 Rev B`, PFD `RB-4410-PFD-001 Rev B`, and P&ID `RB-4410-PID-32-001 Rev B` specify a design feed density of **60.0 % solids w/w** (SG 1.618). High density causes cyclone roping, coarser overflow ($P_{80} > 150\ \mu\text{m}$), and flotation recovery loss.
> - **CORROBORATION & RESOLUTION — PSV-3105 RELIEF SET PRESSURE:** Operating Manual `RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2` (Section 12) explicitly specifies the set pressure of HP lift relief valve `PSV-3105` as **160 bar(g)** (against system design pressure of 170 bar(g)). This corroborates Process Data Sheet `RB-4410-PS-0032 Rev B` (160 bar(g)) and confirms that the marking of `SET 180 bar(g)` on P&ID `RB-4410-PID-31-002 Rev B` violates design pressure limits and constitutes an erroneous draft.
> - **⚠️ CONFLICT — STAGE-3 TAILINGS PUMP CASING DESIGN PRESSURE (PP-6101A/B/C / PID-61-001):** Drawing `RB-4410-PID-61-001 Rev B` explicitly specifies the Stage-3 pump casing design pressure as **4.0 bar(g)** (while designating stage-3 normal operating discharge pressure as **34 bar(g)**), whereas Process Data Sheet `RB-4410-PS-PP6101 Rev B` and Operating Manual `RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2` specify normal discharge at 34 bar(g) and a casing design pressure of **40 bar(g)**. Sizing or rating the stage-3 slurry pump casing at 4.0 bar(g) under a continuous 34 bar(g) discharge column is physically impossible and constitutes an obvious typographical decimal error (4.0 vs 40 bar(g)) on the P&ID. Installing a 4.0 bar(g) rated casing would result in immediate catastrophic casing burst upon pump startup, violent high-pressure slurry discharge, personnel injury, and environmental release. Reconcile with lead mechanical engineer and issue formal P&ID redline prior to HAZOP closure.

> - **SAG MILL START INHIBIT (SIF-3102 / PT-3104):** Per Note 2 of `RB-4410-PID-31-002 Rev B`, Section 5/12 of `RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2`, and controlling matrix `RB-4410-STD-CE-001 Rev B`, pressure transmitter `PT-3104` Low-Low Pressure Alarm (`PALL 70 bar(g)`, 1oo1 voting) initiates Safety Instrumented Function `SIF-3102` (SIL 1) to inhibit SAG Mill start and execute an emergency running trip. > - **SAG MILL START INHIBIT (SIF-3102 / PT-3104):** Per Note 2 of `RB-4410-PID-31-002 Rev B` and Section 5/12 of `RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2`, pressure transmitter `PT-3104` Low-Low Pressure Alarm (`PALL 70 bar(g)`) initiates Safety Instrumented Function `SIF-3102` to inhibit SAG Mill start. > - **ROUGHER FEED PH REGULATION (AIC-4101 / NOTE 1):** Note 1 of `RB-4410-PID-41-001 Rev B` and Section 8/9 of `RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2` mandate lime addition to conditioning tank `TK-4101` on pH control `AIC-4101` at set point **10.5 pH** (operating boundaries AL 10.0 / AH 11.2) to suppress pyrite and maximize chalcopyrite recovery (89 % Cu target).

## Detailed Technical Specifications & Control Philosophy
### 16. Reagents Safety Analysers & Atmospheric Monitoring (Area 45)
- **AT-4501 (PAX Room CS2 Gas Detector):** Continuous infrared gas analyser installed in the enclosed PAX mixing room per mandatory HAZOP recommendation `ACT-HAZOP-45-03` (`RB-4410-STD-HAZOP-001 Rev 1` Node HZ-45-03). Continuously monitors ambient air for fugitive carbon disulfide ($CS_2$) evolved from wet or decomposing potassium amyl xanthate. Calibrated range: 0–100% LEL ($CS_2$ LEL is 1.3 vol%). Alarm setpoint is configured at **10 % LEL**. Upon detecting 10% LEL, `AT-4501` triggers executive hardwired/DCS interlocks to:
  1. Force extraction fan `FN-4501` from low/normal speed to high ventilation speed to maximize room air clearance.
  2. Execute an immediate emergency stop of the solid PAX screw/drum feeder to prevent further chemical addition into `TK-4501`.
  3. Activate visual and audible strobe alarms outside room entrance doors to prohibit personnel entry.
- **PSI Analyser (Cyclone Overflow Particle Size Indicator):** Online particle size analyser installed on the cyclone cluster `CY-3201` overflow stream feeding rougher conditioning tank `TK-4101`. Continuously measures $P_{80}$ targeting **150 µm** per `RB-4410-OM-001 Rev 2` Section 4. Deviations above 150 µm alert control room operators to hydrocyclone roping or excessive feed rate, requiring immediate fresh feed trim on `WIC-3101` or sump water addition.
