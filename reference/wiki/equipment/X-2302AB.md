## Process Role

X-2302A/B remove particulates from the oxidate feed before the feed enters the Preflash Column V-2301 heat exchangers. They protect the downstream E-2302A/B tube internals and prevent fouling of the Preflash Column packing. Feed enters from Oxidizer No.2 pumps at the Oxidation Section (8"-PL-23-003002-L1A1-H) and exits to Preflash Column (4"-PL-23-003001-L1A1-H).

A short-circulation line (2"-line to Oxidizer No.2 at Oxidation Section) allows full bypass of the filters — used when both units must be taken offline simultaneously or during hot standby operation.

## Maintenance Notes

- PDAH-0301 is the primary indicator for filter change-out. Operator should switch to standby unit and clean or replace basket when DP alarm activates.
- Cleanout provisions: 2" blankoff connections on each filter for basket removal.
- Nitrogen blanketing must be maintained via CHP Nitrogen Header (never substitute regular N₂) — see [[units/cdn]] for CHP Nitrogen Header segregation rule.
- During filter switchover, the short-circulation line can be used to maintain OXI section oxidate circulation without interrupting feed.
